Analysis
Analysis: the gap between TSMC's record fundamentals and its falling share price, alongside CoreWeave's CDS distress, suggests markets are starting to price AI-infrastructure financing risk separately from AI demand itself; this currently looks like a financing-structure story rather than a demand story, though the HBM4 order-slip reports for SK Hynix are unconfirmed and could simply reflect capital shifting toward conventional DRAM.
Structural signal
Credit markets (CoreWeave CDS, data-center bonds) are flashing stress before broad volatility gauges (VIX 16.46) have caught up, a divergence worth tracking.
Bull case ▲
TSMC's raised 44% revenue growth guidance and Huang's comment that CoreWeave needs no backstop suggest the underlying AI-demand chain is intact and the selloff is a financing-specific, containable event.
Bear case ▼
If CoreWeave's CDS distress is an early marker of broader AI-infrastructure leverage stress, the roughly $1 trillion in chip-sector market-cap losses this week could be the first leg of a larger repricing rather than a rotation.
Implication
The divergence between record chip-industry fundamentals and sinking AI-infrastructure credit is the key variable to track into next week, since it will determine whether this stays a single-name event or broadens into chip-sector financing stress.
