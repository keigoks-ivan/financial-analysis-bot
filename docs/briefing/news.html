Analysis
This looks like a rotation rather than a broad AI drawdown: capital appears to be moving from data-center and power-infrastructure names with direct policy exposure toward asset-light megacap platforms that can better absorb slower buildout growth, which may explain why NDX and SOX diverged by more than 4 percentage points in a single session.
Structural signal
SOX diverging sharply from NDX on a single day is a warning sign for the SMH leg of the index rule, independent of megacap strength.
Bull case ▲
Industry voices such as Pegatron's chairman called the regulatory fear overblown, and chip demand remains structurally strong with global semiconductor sales topping $500 billion in a quarter for the first time.
Bear case ▼
If Texas and other blue-state governors follow through on permit pauses affecting up to 13GW of planned capacity, further capex guidance cuts could follow, as Microsoft already trimmed quarterly capex by about $5 billion citing delays from a cloud partner.
Implication
The split between megacap platform strength and AI-infrastructure/semiconductor weakness is the key fact to track for the SMH leg of the index rule and for the single-stock AI/semis sleeve.
