Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO amid scandal; market cap falls below $4T
2026-11-12 · Reuters／Bloomberg／CNBC
Situation
Nvidia announced on Nov 12 that Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member effective immediately, following media reports of multiple affairs, one involving a woman with reported national-security concerns; CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO and Harvey Jones became the company's first-ever board chair. NVDA shares fell roughly 4.3%-5% on the day, with some reports citing an intraday drop of up to 14% before a partial recovery, and Nvidia's market value fell below $4 trillion for the first time since July 2025. Reports state Huang has agreed to testify before Congress; coverage is not fully consistent on whether he resigned or was pushed out.
Key data
NVDA share move-4.3% to -5%Thursday session close, intraday low deeperLargest single-day move tied to a management departure this cycle.
Nvidia market capBelow $4 trillionFirst time since July 2025Signals an immediate investor repricing of leadership and governance risk.
AMD/Broadcom reactionReported gainsBarron's: boosted by Nvidia's lossPossible rotation within the semiconductor complex rather than an exit from the AI trade.
Analysis
This looks like an idiosyncratic governance shock rather than an AI-demand problem: TSMC reported unprecedented advanced-chip demand the same day and Cisco raised FY27 AI infrastructure revenue guidance, so the near-term read is leadership-risk repricing specific to Nvidia. As analysis rather than confirmed fact, the size of the share reaction may also reflect concern that Huang's scrutinized China ties invite tighter export-control legislation now moving through Congress.
Structural signal
Capital appears to be rotating toward AMD and Broadcom within semis rather than exiting AI exposure outright.
Bull case ▲
Underlying demand signals (TSMC's record packaging revenue, Cisco's AI infrastructure guidance) remain intact, so the selloff could be a leadership-risk overreaction.
Bear case ▼
Huang's exit removes the figure most tied to Nvidia's CUDA ecosystem and customer relationships just as Congress moves toward tighter chip export controls, risking a more structural de-rating.
Implication
The index sleeve's semiconductor exposure via SMH carries direct single-name Nvidia governance risk this week, distinct from the broader AI capex cycle.
