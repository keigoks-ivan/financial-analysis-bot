Situation
New US export rules stripped Chinese chip designers of a route used to get advanced chips made overseas, requiring licenses at 16nm and below; TSMC said it will comply and Samsung notified Chinese clients of a temporary shipment halt (DIGITIMES/TrendForce, Nov 20). Nvidia fell sharply on the session and Reuters reported the SEC is probing its accounting practices, with Nvidia slipping behind Alphabet in market value. The Taiex fell 6.23% to 36,033.29, its third-largest single-day point drop on record, and Tokyo Electron, MediaTek (-9.1%) and SK Hynix (-6.1%) all sold off sharply alongside it. Options traders placed large bearish bets on further semiconductor downside, per CNBC, while Micron (reported around -13%) and SanDisk extended declines.
