It remains unclear whether this is a memory-specific repricing or the start of a broader AI-capex sentiment shift, given Broadcom's 'casino' remark landed the same week.
Structural signal
US semis (SOX -0.23%) are so far decoupling from the sharper Korean memory-stock declines, but SKEW sitting near a five-day high (154.74) shows hedging demand has not faded.
Bull case ▲
If CXMT's milestone proves unreplicable at scale, Samsung and SK Hynix shares could recover once sentiment resets around this week's ASML and TSMC earnings.
Bear case ▼
If the CXMT threat is validated by actual server DDR5 shipments, DRAM and HBM pricing power for Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron could erode just as AI-capex skepticism is rising.
Implication
The gap between calm US semis and crashing Korean memory stocks is the clearest fault line in the AI-infrastructure trade heading into this week's ASML and TSMC earnings.
