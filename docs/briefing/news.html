Analysis
This looks like a genuine supply-driven structural tightness story — all three DRAM makers have committed their 2026 HBM output and 2027 capacity is being reallocated toward HBM — but the SK Hynix and Samsung selloffs on 'softening demand' reports suggest near-term positioning risk exists even within a structurally tight market. Micron's September 30 commentary on 2027 supply visibility is the next concrete test of whether this is profit-taking or an actual demand inflection, and that judgment is analysis, not yet confirmed fact.
Structural signal
HBM allocation for 2026 is fully committed across Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron, with 2027 capacity share guided toward roughly 30% of total DRAM wafers, up from about 20% today.
Bull case ▲
Sold-out 2026 HBM supply and rising contract prices point to continued pricing power into and beyond Micron's earnings.
Bear case ▼
SK Hynix fell about 6% and Samsung over 4% on September 28 amid reports of softening AI memory demand, a signal that positioning is more fragile than the supply story implies.
Implication
Micron's after-close report on September 30, particularly any 2027 supply commentary, is the next concrete data point for the memory/HBM tightness narrative that underpins the semiconductor sleeve.
