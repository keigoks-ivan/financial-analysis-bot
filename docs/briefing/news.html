Analysis
This appears to be part of a broader pattern of AI capex being funded through debt and private credit rather than cash flow or equity (analysis); WSJ reported Oracle, Broadcom and SpaceX are all seeking blockbuster debt deals to pay for AI chips, following Paramount's $52 billion debt sale. If credit investors demand wider spreads on this paper, it could be an early tell for AI-capex stress before it shows up in equity volatility.
Structural signal
AI infrastructure spending is increasingly funded by debt rather than cash flow, concentrating credit risk in AI-exposed names.
Bull case ▲
Debt financing lets the compute buildout continue even as equity markets wobble, keeping Nvidia's order book and chip demand intact.
Bear case ▼
Rising CDS spreads and reported Fed scrutiny of bank lending to private-credit firms with AI-related exposure suggest credit markets are starting to price downside risk in the AI buildout.
Implication
The financing structure behind AI capex, not just equity multiples, is becoming a channel worth watching for stress.
