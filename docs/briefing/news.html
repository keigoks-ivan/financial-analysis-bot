Analysis
This looks like the market testing whether hyperscaler capex is generating commensurate returns: cloud segments (AWS +39%, Google Cloud +103%) are accelerating, but headline profitability and cash generation at Alphabet and Amazon both missed, and the selloff concentrates in names seen as AI-capex payers or as vulnerable to AI-driven disruption rather than spreading evenly. This is analysis, not settled fact: a single week of earnings reactions does not confirm an AI-capex cycle turn.
Structural signal
Megacap tech (NYFANG -2.21%) and momentum (MTUM -1.70%) are underperforming the broader index (NDX -1.38%) while breadth widens, consistent with capital rotating out of the most AI-capex-exposed names.
Bull case ▲
Cloud growth acceleration (AWS +39%, Google Cloud +103% with a $561B backlog) shows AI infrastructure demand remains intact even as near-term margins absorb heavier capex.
Bear case ▼
Amazon's reported negative free cash flow alongside guidance for 'meaningfully more' 2027 capex, plus Intel's weak PC outlook and ServiceNow's AI-disruption-driven miss, suggest capital intensity is rising faster than near-term returns can offset.
Implication
The reaction is concentrated in AI-capex-linked and AI-disruption-exposed names rather than broadening into credit or small caps, which argues for watching Friday's open in SOX and NDX futures rather than treating this as a systemic event yet.
