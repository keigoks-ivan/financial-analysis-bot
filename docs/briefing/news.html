Analysis
Analysis: the scale of Micron's beat combined with TrendForce's upward ASP revision suggests the HBM shortage is becoming a structural, multi-year feature of the AI buildout rather than a transient supply pinch, which would keep memory-linked capex well-bid across the Taiwan and Korea supply chain. This is an interpretation of disclosed guidance and third-party forecasts, not a certainty, since Mehrotra himself flagged narrowing margins next quarter as an offsetting caution.
Structural signal
Memory pricing power is broadening from spot-market spikes into multi-year contract terms, a structural rather than purely cyclical signal for the AI infrastructure chain.
Bull case ▲
Sustained HBM scarcity into 2027 supports pricing and margins across Micron, SK Hynix, and the Taiwan substrate and testing names feeding advanced packaging capacity.
Bear case ▼
Micron's own warning of narrowing near-term margins, combined with the Netlist patent suit against Micron, Nvidia, Broadcom and Google, introduces profitability and legal risk to the same trade.
Implication
The AI memory supply chain remains the highest-conviction structural beneficiary of disclosed AI infrastructure capex this week, though near-term margin and legal overhangs warrant monitoring.
