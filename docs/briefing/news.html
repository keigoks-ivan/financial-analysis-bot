Analysis
This is analysis, not settled fact: sustained scarcity across HBM, DRAM, NAND, and CoWoS packaging looks increasingly structural rather than cyclical, which would keep pricing power concentrated with memory and packaging suppliers rather than downstream AI compute buyers. If correct, this reinforces the view that the AI capex cycle is being gated by hardware supply, not demand, through at least 2027.
Structural signal
Pricing power sits with memory and packaging suppliers, not with downstream AI compute buyers, for the foreseeable future.
Bull case ▲
Sustained memory and packaging scarcity through 2027 supports multi-year earnings upgrades for HBM and CoWoS-exposed suppliers such as SK Hynix, Samsung, and TSMC.
Bear case ▼
Surging HBM, DRAM, NAND, and 2nm wafer costs compress margins for AI compute buyers and could slow capex if return-on-investment narrows, a risk echoed in FT reporting on AI concentration and a cooling dealmaking environment.
Implication
The AI infrastructure trade is increasingly a memory and packaging supply story as much as a GPU demand story.
