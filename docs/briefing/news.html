The risk is that financing-driven selling in credit-sensitive AI names spills into broader semiconductor equity multiples even where fundamentals have not changed.
Structural signal
Credit markets (HYG -0.35%) have not yet confirmed the scale of the equity-vol stress, suggesting the selloff remains contained to single-name and sector-specific credit rather than systemic contagion so far.
Bull case ▲
TSMC's 41.2% revenue growth and raised 44% FY26 guidance show AI chip demand still running ahead of supply, with management describing the gap as 'quite big.'
Bear case ▼
CoreWeave's CDS implying a 60%-plus default probability and a reported four-session loss above $10 billion signal that AI-infrastructure financing stress could broaden into a sector-wide repricing of leverage.
Implication
The divergence between strong AI hardware fundamentals and deteriorating AI-financing credit metrics is the central tension for semiconductor exposure this week.
