Quantum error rates drop below 1% for two-qubit gates
Quantum computing
US Department of Energy, IBM, Google, Quantinuum, Microsoftlab result
The US Department of Energy released a national quantum computing roadmap on September 24 targeting a scientifically relevant, error-corrected quantum computer by 2028. Two-qubit gate error rates have dropped below 1% across all major platforms, and five organizations have now verified logical qubit demonstrations, with IBM, Google, Quantinuum and Microsoft all targeting 2029 for useful, error-corrected machines.
Why it matters ▸ Sub-1% two-qubit error rates are the threshold that enables practical error correction, the main blocker for commercially useful quantum computing.

Synthetic biology
