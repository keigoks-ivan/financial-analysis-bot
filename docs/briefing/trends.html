National Taiwan University reports p-type 2D semiconductor from doped hBN
Novel compute
National Taiwan Universitylab result
Researchers reported producing a p-type 2D semiconductor by adding carbon to single-layer hexagonal boron nitride, achieving a hole mobility of about 100 cm2/V-s, with the work described as published in Nature. No device-level data such as contact resistance or on/off ratio was given.
Why it matters ▸ A working p-type partner to existing n-type 2D channel materials could matter for post-silicon 2D-channel CMOS, though wafer-scale growth and low-resistance contacts remain unproven.
