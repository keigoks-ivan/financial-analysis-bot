Alice & Bob estimate 22,593 qubits needed to break RSA-2048
Quantum computing
Alice & Boblab result
Alice & Bob published a preprint estimating about 22,593 physical cat qubits could factor RSA-2048 in roughly 3.14 days under zero-error assumptions, and about 29,588 qubits could recover a Bitcoin elliptic-curve key in 61.8 minutes. At one bit-flip per hour, the RSA-2048 estimate rises to about 45,000 qubits.
Why it matters ▸ This is a theoretical resource estimate, not a built machine, but it sharpens the timeline debate for when quantum computers could threaten current encryption standards.
