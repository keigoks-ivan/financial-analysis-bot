Google lands first customer for superhot geothermal power
Energy and power
Google, Quaise Energypilot
Quaise Energy announced on November 17 that Google is its first major customer for a novel superhot-rock geothermal project in central Oregon designed to power data centers. The technology uses a drilling approach aimed at reaching rock hot enough to generate baseload power at scale.
