Singapore team measures atomic clock frequency to 19 decimal places
Novel compute
Centre for Quantum Technologies, Singaporelab result
Researchers at Singapore's Centre for Quantum Technologies achieved a lutetium-ion optical atomic clock frequency measurement to 19 decimal places, with an uncertainty of 1.0x10^-19 and clock comparison precision of 5.7x10^-19, published in Nature on September 23, 2026. It is described as the most precise clock comparison ever made.
Why it matters ▸ Precision timing at this level underpins next-generation telecom synchronization, financial network timestamping and GPS alternatives, but needs room-temperature and miniaturized hardware to scale.
