One last thing
💡 Nvidia options implied an $335 billion swing
Ahead of its November 17 earnings report, options markets priced an implied move of about 8.5% for Nvidia shares, which Reuters calculated as a potential $335 billion swing in market value. That is larger than the entire market capitalization of most S&P 500 companies. The pricing reflected the unprecedented uncertainty around the company's first report since Jensen Huang's departure.
📎 It captures just how much weight a single earnings report now carries for the index book, given Nvidia's outsized share of both QQQ and SMH.
