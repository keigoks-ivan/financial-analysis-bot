💡 A $2 bitcoin damages verdict, literally
A Delaware jury awarded Qualcomm just $2 in damages after finding Arm breached its license agreement, one of the smallest damages verdicts in a major tech litigation case in recent memory. The case centered on licensing terms for chip designs central to the mobile and AI chip supply chain. Despite the token award, the jury's finding that Arm breached the agreement could still matter for future licensing disputes.
📎 The ruling lands the same week chip-supply-chain tensions, from CXMT's DRAM claim to Broadcom's AI 'casino' warning, are rattling the semiconductor sector the index book depends on.
