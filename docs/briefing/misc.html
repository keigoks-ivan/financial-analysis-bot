One last thing
💡 Taiwan's first-ever chip industry strike
Micron's Taoyuan union staged a 24-hour warning strike this week, the first labor strike in Taiwan's semiconductor industry's history. Nearly 70% of union members rejected the company's bonus offer before the action concluded on Oct 23. Taiwan's chip sector has long been seen as strike-resistant given its tight labor discipline and export importance.
📎 The strike adds a new, unusual risk factor to the memory supply chain just as SK hynix scraps its Solidigm IPO and AI-driven memory demand tightens capacity across the board.
