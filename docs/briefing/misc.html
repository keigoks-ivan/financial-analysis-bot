One last thing
💡 SpaceX's AI business now bigger than its rockets' margin
SpaceX, long known for reusable rockets and Starlink satellites, reported that AI now accounts for 52% of its income, according to TechCrunch's October 20 reporting on its third-quarter results. The company posted its first profit as a public reporting company, with net income of $4.4 billion on revenue of $14.58 billion. Its data centers and Grok AI agents were reportedly more profitable than investors had expected.
📎 It is a direct illustration of today's broader theme: AI revenue is now showing up in unexpected places, even at a company whose public identity is rockets and satellites, right as credit markets start pricing default risk into AI-linked balance sheets.
