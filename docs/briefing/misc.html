One last thing
💡 Intel's stake wipeout echoes a 1970s-scale shock
Intel's roughly 25%-29% single-day decline on October 30 was described by multiple outlets as its worst day since the 1970s, a company that has existed through nearly every major semiconductor cycle. The drop wiped about $12 billion off the US government's equity stake, a holding that did not exist before 2026's direct government investments in chipmakers. It is a reminder that even a company at the center of the AI buildout can be undone by a single quarter of soft PC demand and a factory shutdown.
📎 Ties directly to today's top story on Intel's guidance-driven crash, which dragged Dell, AMD and Nvidia lower in sympathy.
