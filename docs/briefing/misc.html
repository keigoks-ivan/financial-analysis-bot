💡 A single 2nm wafer now costs as much as a car
TSMC's most advanced 2nm wafers are pricing near $30,000 apiece, roughly the cost of a new compact car, and the company's order books for that capacity are already full through 2028. Each wafer yields dozens of chips destined for Apple, Nvidia and AMD products, meaning the wafer price increase cascades directly into the cost of every next-generation phone and AI accelerator.
