Last US session
📌 The October 29 session was dominated by hyperscaler earnings disappointments, with Amazon missing EPS estimates and guiding softer on Q4 sales, ServiceNow posting its worst day ever on a profit miss, and Coinbase reporting a 35% revenue decline, while Rivian and Mastercard both beat expectations.
After hours Amazon AMZN MISS
Q3 net sales rose 12% to $201.9B against a $202.3B estimate; EPS of $1.89 missed the roughly $1.96 consensus; AWS revenue rose 39% to $46.0B; free cash flow was negative $42.4B.
Shares fell between roughly 5% and 11% after hours depending on the outlet.
A rare EPS miss and negative free cash flow test investor patience with Amazon's AI capex just as Q4 guidance of $240.0B-$247.0B came in softer than hoped.
Pre-market Mastercard MA BEAT
Q3 2026 profit rose on payment network and services growth, reported before the open.
Continued profit growth from Mastercard supports the payments-network thesis within the index book's financial exposure.
After hours Rivian RIVN BEAT
Q3 revenue of $2.155B beat the roughly $2.02B estimate, driven by rising software revenue and R2 SUV deliveries; the company raised 2026 guidance for deliveries, adjusted earnings and capex.
Shares rose after the report on the earnings beat and raised guidance.
Rivian's R2 SUV becoming its best-selling vehicle and a second guidance raise this year signals accelerating EV demand traction.
After hours Starbucks SBUX MISS
Sales missed expectations overall, though US comparable sales topped Wall Street estimates while international business fell short.
A US turnaround gaining steam alongside international weakness shows an uneven recovery for the coffee chain.
After hours ServiceNow NOW MISS
Q3 profit missed despite a headline revenue beat; the company raised its full-year subscription-revenue and cash-flow margin outlook.
Shares fell 23-27% in the following session, the worst single-day drop on record.
The profit miss and record stock drop revived investor fears that AI agents could disrupt enterprise software incumbents.
After hours Coinbase COIN MISS
Q3 2026 revenue of $1.2 billion fell 35% from a year ago and was roughly flat quarter over quarter, partially offset by prediction-market activity.
A 35% revenue decline reflects a broad slowdown in crypto trading volumes affecting exchange-dependent businesses.

One last thing
💡 Alphabet's $500B rout among largest single-day losses
Alphabet's roughly 12-13% share drop on October 29 erased about $500 billion in market value in a single session, among the largest one-day value losses for any company on record. The decline came despite Google Cloud revenue growing 103% to $30.7 billion, showing how sensitive AI-era valuations have become to capex and margin signals rather than headline growth.
📎 The same day, Amazon and Intel also sold off on guidance concerns and ServiceNow posted its worst day ever, making October 29 a broad referendum on AI-spending payoffs across the index book.
