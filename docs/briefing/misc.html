💡 Taiwan's market fell its sixth-biggest point drop ever
Taiwan's stock index fell 1,655.96 points on October 15, 2026, the sixth-largest single-day point decline on record, as foreign investors sold a record NT$302.56B in a single session. The drop came amid a global AI-stock selloff that also pushed South Korea's KOSPI to a six-month low and knocked more than 5% off Japan's Nikkei 225 the same day.
📎 The sell-off traces directly to the same AI-funding anxiety hitting Nvidia, AMD, SK Hynix and Samsung this week, showing how tightly Taiwan's index is now coupled to the global AI capex narrative.
