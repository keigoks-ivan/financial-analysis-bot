One last thing
💡 Fed meetings are shrinking for the first time in decades
Chair Kevin Warsh said the Fed will hold fewer rate-setting meetings starting next fall, breaking from the decades-old custom of eight FOMC meetings a year to seven. The last comparable change to the Fed's meeting cadence dates back generations, making this a rare structural shift in US monetary policy mechanics.
📎 The announcement came alongside the Oct 28 decision to hold rates at 3.75%-4.00%, overshadowing the vote itself in some coverage.
