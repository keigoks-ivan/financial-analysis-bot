Last US session
📌 CoreWeave's strong beat and raised guidance dominated the session, reviving AI-trade sentiment, while Qualys and Grocery Outlet both beat on earnings but saw shares fall after hours on margin and guidance concerns.
Regular CoreWeave CRWV BEAT
Q3 revenue of $3.66 billion with a loss of $1.27 per share; raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $13.0-13.3 billion.
Shares rose about 19-25% during the session, the biggest one-day gain since mid-2025.
The beat and guidance raise eased investor concerns about the sustainability of AI-cloud spending.
After hours Qualys QLYS BEAT
Non-GAAP EPS of $2.12 beat the $1.95 consensus on revenue of about $188 million; raised 2026 EPS guidance to $8.07-$8.14.
Shares fell after hours despite the EPS beat.
Unchanged revenue guidance despite the EPS beat points to margin-driven rather than demand-driven improvement.
After hours Grocery Outlet GO BEAT
Adjusted EPS of $0.16 beat the $0.15 estimate; raised Q4 comparable-sales guidance but FY2026 revenue guidance of $4.70-$4.72 billion matched estimates.
Shares closed at $14.02 and fell 4.78% to $13.35 after hours on margin pressure.
Margin pressure overshadowed the top-line beat and guidance raise.
