💡 SpaceX's first profitable quarter took 24 years
SpaceX, founded in 2002, reported its first quarterly profit only this quarter, with revenue up 161% year over year to $14.58 billion. The milestone arrived the same week Elon Musk floated merging SpaceX with Tesla, another company that took over a decade to turn consistently profitable.
📎 The profit announcement is driving both SpaceX-linked sentiment and Tesla shares today, and comes as index-book names like Meta and IBM also post AI-era capex and earnings milestones.
