💡 VIX's biggest one-day jump since April
Wall Street's fear gauge surged more than 11 points from intraday low to high on Nov 5, its largest single-day move since the April 2026 tariff shock. The move came as the Nasdaq 100 fell 6.4% in a single session, erasing roughly $3.2 trillion in US equity value. Traders described the swing as comparable in speed, if not in cause, to the market's reaction to April's 'Liberation Day' tariff announcement.
📎 The VIX spike is the clearest single number capturing how fast the AI-capex selloff spread from chipmakers to the broader index book in one session.
