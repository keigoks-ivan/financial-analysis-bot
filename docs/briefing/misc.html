One last thing
💡 Lumentum began life as a $1 billion spinoff
Lumentum was spun out of JDS Uniphase in 2015 at roughly a $1 billion valuation as a maker of optical components. On Nov 2, 2026, Marvell agreed to buy it for $115 billion, a roughly 115-fold increase in little over a decade.
📎 The deal, confirmed by Bloomberg and The Information as one of the largest semiconductor mergers on record, is this week's clearest sign of how far AI-driven demand for optical interconnects has pushed valuations in the chip supply chain.
