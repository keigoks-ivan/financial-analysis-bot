One last thing
💡 Micron's margin now rivals Nvidia's best years
Micron's fiscal Q1 2027 guidance implies gross margins around 87%, a level more associated with software companies than memory chipmakers. DRAM revenue alone jumped 343% year-over-year to $39.8 billion in the just-reported quarter. Memory has historically been one of the most cyclical, lowest-margin corners of the chip industry.
📎 Underscores how acute the current HBM and DRAM shortage has become, with pricing power now rivaling logic chipmakers rather than typical commodity memory economics.
