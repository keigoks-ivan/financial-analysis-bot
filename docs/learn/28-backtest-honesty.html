Seven Years of Almost Free Money

7 年幾乎像白撿的錢

For seven years, an exchange-traded note called XIV did something that looked, on paper, like one of the best risk-adjusted trades available to any retail investor. Launched in November 2010, it offered inverse exposure to VIX futures — in plain terms, a daily-reset bet that market volatility would stay calm or keep falling. From 2011 into late 2017, with only brief and shallow interruptions, it did almost exactly that: its net asset value compounded to more than ten times its starting level, its annualized volatility ran well below the stock market's own, and any Sharpe ratio computed over that stretch would have made nearly every other publicly available strategy look mediocre by comparison. Independent traders who ran their own backtests on the same mechanism got the same answer: seven clean years of in-sample evidence, and a return stream that looked close to a free lunch.

有一檔叫 XIV 的交易所交易票券，連續 7 年交出漂亮到不像話的紙面數字。它在 2010 年 11 月掛牌，賭的是市場波動率（股價上下震盪的劇烈程度，數字越高代表越震盪）會維持平靜或繼續往下走，技術上是對 VIX（一個衡量美股恐慌程度的波動率指數）期貨採取反向曝險（曝險指資金實際暴露在市場漲跌下的部位；「反向」代表 VIX 跌它就賺），而且每天重新計算一次。從 2011 年到 2017 年底，它幾乎一路做到了：中間只有幾次短暫又不深的回檔，淨值累積漲到起始水位的 10 倍以上，年化波動率比股市大盤還低。用這段期間算出來的夏普比率（衡量報酬相對承受的波動划不划算的比率，數字越高代表用同樣的風險換到更多報酬），幾乎讓所有公開可查的策略都相形見絀。獨立操作者自己拿同一套機制回測，答案一樣：7 年乾淨的樣本內證據，一條看起來像白撿的報酬曲線。

On February 5, 2018, the VIX index roughly doubled within a single session as a broad market selloff triggered a rush to hedge. XIV, structurally built to move inversely to VIX futures on that same daily-reset basis, was wiped out. Its indicative value fell intraday to at or below 20% of the prior day's closing indicative value of $108.37 — the exact threshold its own prospectus had named for years as grounds for the issuer to terminate the note. Credit Suisse invoked it the next day, February 6. The final value was fixed on February 15 and paid on February 21, at a small fraction of what the note had been worth the previous Friday. Investors who had ridden a seven-year winning streak were left holding a product that no longer existed. Nothing about the mechanism had failed — inverse VIX exposure did exactly what its structure always promised to do under those conditions. What failed was an assumption embedded in every backtest anyone had run on it: that the sample window used to measure risk contained the full range of what volatility could actually do. It did not. The sample simply had not yet lived through a day like that one.

2018 年 2 月 5 日，全市場一波拋售引發搶購避險，VIX 指數在一個交易日內大約翻了一倍。XIV 的結構就是每天重設、朝 VIX 期貨的反方向走，那天等於被直接抹平。它的盤中指標淨值跌到前一日收盤指標淨值 108.37 美元的 20% 以下，這正是它自己的公開說明書多年來寫明的門檻：一旦觸及，發行機構就可以終止這檔票券。瑞士信貸隔天（2 月 6 日）就動用了這個條款。最終價值在 2 月 15 日定案、2 月 21 日支付，只剩下前一個星期五價值的一小部分。搭了 7 年順風車的投資人，手上留下一個已經不存在的商品。機制沒有失靈。反向 VIX 曝險在那種市況下，做的正是它結構上該做的事。失靈的是一個假設：所有人跑的回測都預設，用來衡量風險的那段樣本，已經涵蓋了波動率能做到的全部範圍。它沒有。樣本裡根本還沒出現過那樣的一天。

None of this required inside information to see coming in outline, even if the exact date could not be predicted. The note's pricing supplement, filed with securities regulators in November 2010 before anyone could buy it, stated the termination trigger in plain numbers: if the intraday indicative value fell to 20% or less of the prior day's closing indicative value, the notes could be accelerated and “you will likely lose all or a substantial portion of your investment.” The precise condition that ended the product was public, in writing, for its entire seven years on the market. A backtest of the note's historical returns, however carefully built, would never have surfaced it, because a backtest only measures what happened inside its sample; the prospectus described what the structure allowed to happen, independent of whether the sample had lived through it yet.

確切的日期沒人能預測，但這件事的輪廓不需要內線消息就看得到。這檔票券 2010 年 11 月申報給證券主管機關的定價補充文件，把終止條件寫成了具體數字：一旦盤中指標淨值跌到前一日收盤指標淨值的 20% 或更低，這些票券就可能被提前終止，而「你很可能會損失全部或大部分的投資」。真正結束這個商品的那個條件，在它掛牌的 7 年裡，一直用文字公開攤在那裡。而不管回測做得多仔細，都不會讓這句話浮現出來。回測只量得到樣本裡實際發生過的事；說明書描述的是這個結構「允許發生什麼」，跟樣本有沒有真的經歷過無關。

This is a teaching case, not a comment on any product available today — the note stopped trading in 2018. The mechanism worth carrying forward: the gap between the conditions a sample lived through and the conditions reality allows does not show up as an error message. It shows up, if at all, on the one day it matters most.

這是教學案例，不是對任何現行商品的評論，這檔票券在 2018 年就下市了。要帶走的機制是：樣本經歷過的條件範圍跟現實之間有落差，而這個落差不會跳出錯誤訊息提醒你。它現形的時候，通常就是最要命的那一天。

Takeaway收斂

A backtest tells you what happened inside the window you fed it. It cannot tell you what the window left out — and the window always leaves something out.

回測告訴你的是「餵給它的那段窗口裡發生了什麼」。它沒辦法告訴你這個窗口漏掉了什麼——而窗口永遠會漏掉一些東西。

Survivorship Bias and Unadjusted Prices: The Starting Line Is Already Tilted

存活者偏誤與未還原股價：起跑線本身就是歪的

Two of the oldest and most basic distortions in any backtest happen before a single trading rule is even applied — they live in how the raw data was assembled.

任何回測裡最古老、也最基本的兩種扭曲，發生在任何一條交易規則被套用之前——它們藏在原始資料是怎麼被組出來的這件事本身。

The first is survivorship biasBuilding a historical test using only the companies, funds, or index members that still exist today, silently excluding everything that failed, was delisted, or was acquired along the way.. If a backtest of, say, quality large-cap stocks pulls today's list of index members and applies a rule to that list going back thirty years, every company that was removed from the index along the way — because it went bankrupt, was delisted, or simply shrank out of relevance — is invisible to the test. Enron and WorldCom were both large, well-known index constituents before their accounting failures became public in 2001 and 2002; both were removed from major indices as their share prices collapsed. A backtest built off “today’s S&P 500 membership, run backward” never sees either company's failure, because by the time you pulled the list, both were long gone from it. The effect is not random noise: it mechanically removes failures and keeps successes, which is why academic studies of mutual fund databases that fail to account for it have found the resulting historical average return inflated by somewhere in the neighborhood of one to two percentage points a year — a gap large enough to turn a mediocre long-run record into a seemingly good one.

第一種叫存活者偏誤建立歷史測試時只用「今天還存在」的公司、基金或指數成分股，把沿路失敗、下市、或被收購的標的，全部悄悄排除在外。。假設一份優質大型股的回測，是拿「今天」的指數成分股清單，套一條規則往回跑 30 年。那麼沿路被剔除出指數的每一家公司（破產的、下市的、或單純萎縮到不再重要的）在這份測試裡完全不存在。安隆（Enron）與世界通訊（WorldCom）在會計弊案分別於 2001 年與 2002 年曝光之前，都是規模龐大、人人知道的指數成分股；股價崩跌後，兩家都被主要指數剔除。用今天的標普 500 清單往回套，這兩次失敗永遠不會出現，因為你抓清單的時候它們早就不在上面了。這不是隨機雜訊。它是機械性地把失敗拿掉、把成功留下。學術上研究共同基金資料庫發現，沒處理這個問題的話，算出來的歷史平均報酬率會被灌高大約每年 1 到 2 個百分點。這個幅度足以把一份平庸的長期紀錄，包裝成看起來還不錯的紀錄。

The second, related but distinct, is failing to use adjusted pricesA price history rescaled so that stock splits and dividend payments do not create artificial jumps in the series — also called total-return or split-and-dividend-adjusted prices.. A stock split or a large special dividend changes the per-share price without changing anything about the underlying business or a shareholder's actual wealth. A raw, unadjusted price series treats a 4-for-1 split as if the price had genuinely fallen 75% overnight; feed that into a rule that buys on sharp price drops, and the rule will fire on a corporate action that was never a real drawdown at all, distorting both the entry signal and any return calculated across that date. The error is not small and not random — it is tied precisely to the date of the corporate action and can be off by a factor of two, four, or more depending on the split ratio involved.

第二個問題跟第一個有關，但不一樣：沒有使用還原股價重新按比例縮放過的股價歷史，讓股票分割與配息不會在序列裡製造出人為的跳空——也叫做總報酬價或拆股與配息調整後價格。。股票分割或大額特別股息，改變的只是每股價格，跟底層生意和股東的實際財富無關。但未還原的原始股價序列，會把一股拆四股當成股價一夜之間真的跌了 75%。把這種資料餵給「股價大跌就買進」的規則，它就會對一次根本不是回撤的公司行動觸發買進，進場訊號和跨越那個日期的報酬率同時被扭曲。這個誤差不小也不隨機。它精確綁在公司行動那一天，依分割比例不同，可能整整差了 2 倍、4 倍甚至更多。

The distortion runs in both directions. General Electric completed a 1-for-8 reverse stock split, effective after the close on July 30, 2021, with the stock trading on a split-adjusted basis from August 2 — a mechanical combination of every eight pre-split shares into one post-split share that, again, changed nothing about the underlying business or a shareholder's actual stake. A raw, unadjusted price series applied naively across that date would record the stock jumping by roughly the split ratio itself: eight times the prior price is a 700% single-day “gain,” the mirror image of the 75% single-day “loss” a forward split can manufacture. A rule built to sell into strength, or to flag unusual single-day moves for review, would fire on this exactly as hard as a rule built to buy dips fires on a forward split — the direction of the artifact flips, but the underlying failure is identical.

這種扭曲兩個方向都會發生。奇異公司（GE）在 2021 年 7 月 30 日收盤後生效，執行了一次一股併八股的反向分割，股價從 8 月 2 日起以分割後的價格交易。這是把每 8 股併成 1 股的機械動作，同樣沒有改變底層生意，也沒有改變股東實際持股比例。如果把未還原的原始股價序列天真地套在那個日期前後，會記錄到股價跳漲了大約分割比例那麼多：分割前價格的 8 倍，等於單日「上漲」約 700%，正好是遠期分割製造出的單日 75%「下跌」的鏡像。一條「強勢就賣出」或「單日異常波動就標記複查」的規則，會對這個訊號開火，力道跟「逢低買進」規則對遠期分割開火一樣猛。扭曲的方向翻過來了，失效的方式一模一樣。

The counter-example worth holding onto: a data provider that explicitly labels its price series as split-and-dividend-adjusted, and a universe construction method described as point-in-timeHistorical data reconstructed to show exactly what a database would have contained at each past date — including companies since delisted, acquired, or gone bankrupt — rather than today's list applied backward. — meaning the index membership used for any given historical date reflects who was actually in the index on that date, delistings and all. Neither guarantee is visible in a single headline chart; both are usually disclosed, if at all, in a methodology footnote most readers skip.

值得記住的反例長這樣：資料供應商明確標註股價序列已經「拆股與配息調整過」，母體建構方式標成時點正確重建的歷史資料能精確顯示資料庫在過去每一個日期「當時實際包含什麼」，包括後來下市、被收購、破產的公司，而不是拿今天的清單往回套用。，意思是每個歷史日期用的成分股名單，就是那一天真正在指數裡的名單，後來下市的也算。這兩個保證都不會出現在頭條圖表上。它們通常只寫在方法論的一小段註腳裡，而多數讀者不看那一段。

How to see it from public information: look for the specific phrases “adjusted close,” “total return,” or “point-in-time” in a report's methodology section. If a report only says it used “historical constituents of the index” without specifying whether that means today's list applied backward or a reconstructed point-in-time list, treat the number as unverified rather than assuming the better-faith interpretation.

怎麼從公開資訊看出來：翻到報告的方法論段落，找「調整後收盤價」「總報酬」「時點正確」這幾個字眼。如果它只寫「使用指數的歷史成分股」，沒說是今天的清單往回套、還是重建過的時點正確清單，就把數字當成尚未查證。不要自動往對報告有利的方向解讀。

Look-Ahead Bias: Using Information the Market Did Not Have Yet

前視偏誤：用了市場當時還沒有的資訊

Look-ahead biasA backtest accidentally using information that would not actually have been available at the historical point in time it claims to be trading — the test quietly gets to see the future. is subtler than survivorship bias because it rarely announces itself. A common version: testing an earnings-based rule using a company's financial data as it appears today, in a database that has since been restated. Companies revise reported figures after the fact for all kinds of reasons — accounting corrections, occasionally outright fraud unwound years later — and a database that stores only the current, corrected numbers is quietly feeding a trading rule information that no investor actually holding the stock at that historical date could have known. A rule built on “buy when reported earnings quality looks clean” can look excellent in a backtest that only ever sees the cleaned-up, restated version of history, and far weaker once tested on data as it was originally reported at the time.

前視偏誤回測不小心用了在它宣稱交易的那個歷史時點，實際上根本還不存在的資訊——測試安靜地「偷看」了未來。比存活者偏誤更隱蔽，因為它幾乎不會主動現形。常見版本是這樣：你用一家公司「現在」的財報數字去測一條盈餘規則，但資料庫裡那幾年的數字後來被重編過。公司事後修正已公布的數字，原因很多——會計更正，有時候是好幾年後才被拆穿的舞弊。一個只存最新、修正後數字的資料庫，等於偷偷餵給規則一份當時沒人拿得到的資訊。一條「盈餘品質乾淨就買進」的規則，如果只看得到事後清乾淨的版本，回測會很漂亮；改用當年原始公布的數字重測，通常弱得多。

A concrete, on-the-record version of this risk: in April 2002 the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission charged Xerox with improperly accelerating the recognition of equipment revenue, making reported earnings in the late 1990s look stronger than the underlying cash economics supported. Xerox settled by paying a $10 million civil penalty and agreeing to restate its results. The restatement it filed that June reversed roughly $6.4 billion of equipment-sales revenue recognized across 1997 through 2001 and cut cumulative pre-tax earnings over that span by about $1.4 billion. Several former executives settled related charges afterward. A backtest built today on a financial database that only stores Xerox's current, corrected numbers for 1997–2000 would score any earnings-based rule against results no investor holding the stock in, say, 1999 could have read in that year's actual filings — the restatement itself did not exist yet.

這種風險有一個有紀錄可查的具體版本：2002 年 4 月，美國證管會（SEC）起訴全錄公司（Xerox）不當提前認列設備收入，讓 1990 年代後期公布的獲利，看起來比底層現金流支撐得住的更好看。全錄和解時支付了 1,000 萬美元民事罰款，並同意重編財報。同年 6 月提出的重編，沖回了 1997 到 2001 年間認列的大約 64 億美元設備銷售收入，並把這段期間累計的稅前獲利下修約 14 億美元。之後也有幾位前高階主管另外和解了相關指控。如果今天有一份回測，用的財務資料庫只存了全錄 1997 到 2000 年「現在、已修正過」的數字，任何以盈餘為基礎的規則，都會被拿去對著一組在 1999 年當時、任何持有這檔股票的投資人在那一年真正申報的文件裡根本讀不到的結果打分數——因為那時候，這次重編根本還沒發生。

The counter-example is a database explicitly built to preserve the original, as-first-reported figures alongside any later restatement, letting a researcher choose which version a rule should see. That such databases exist and are sold separately is itself the evidence that the problem is real: the major fundamentals vendors offer a distinct point-in-time product precisely because their standard product overwrites a company's historical figures whenever that company restates them, and researchers who need to know what was knowable on a past date cannot use the standard version. Paying extra for the same numbers in a different vintage only makes sense if the vintage changes the answer.

反例是一種資料庫：它同時保留「最初公布的版本」與「後來每一次修正的版本」，讓研究者自己選規則該看到哪一版。這種資料庫存在、而且要另外收費，本身就是問題真實存在的證據。主要的財報資料商都另外賣一套「時點正確」產品，因為他們的標準產品只要公司重編，就會把歷史數字直接覆蓋掉，需要知道「當年看得到什麼」的研究者根本不能用標準版。同一批數字換一個版本要多付錢，只有在「版本會改變答案」的情況下才說得通。

How to test for it without access to the underlying code: ask whether the report describes using “as-reported” or “as-first-available” data, or whether it simply says “financial data from [database name]” without specifying the vintage. A more direct test, when the underlying process is available for inspection, is to take the historical data after some cutoff date, distort it deliberately — for instance, multiply prices after that date by ten — and rerun the exact same signal and weight calculations. If any output before the cutoff date changes at all, the system was looking at data it should not have had access to yet; if the pre-cutoff output is bit-for-bit identical, the system passes this specific test, though it does not by itself rule out every other form of the bias.

拿不到底層程式碼時怎麼測：看報告有沒有寫明用的是「當時公布」或「當時可得」的資料，還是只寫「資料來自某某資料庫」，完全沒交代版本。如果能看到底層流程，有一個更直接的測法：挑一個切點，把切點之後的歷史資料刻意弄壞——例如把後面的價格全部乘以十——再跑一次完全相同的訊號與權重計算。切點之前的輸出如果變了，代表系統偷看了當時不該拿到的資料。切點之前的輸出如果一格都沒變，它就通過了這一項測試。通過不等於乾淨，其他形式的前視偏誤仍然要另外查。

Where to check this yourself: finding a restatement in public filings. First, open EDGAR, the SEC's free public filing database that every US-listed company must file into, and search the company name alongside the word “restate” or “restatement.” One limit to know before you trust a blank result: EDGAR's full-text search covers filings from 2001 onward only, so for anything older you browse the company's filing list by form type instead of searching text. Second, look for two specific things: a form coded 10-K/A or 10-Q/A, where the “/A” suffix marks an amended filing, and an 8-K reporting Item 4.02, which is the item a company must use to announce that previously issued financial statements should no longer be relied upon. An Item 4.02 8-K is the cleanest single marker of a restatement in progress. Third, inside the filing, read the footnote titled something like “Restatement of Previously Issued Financial Statements” — it states exactly which years and which line items changed, and by how much. Normal range and warning sign: an isolated restatement affecting one or two minor line items is common and usually immaterial; a restatement that moves revenue or earnings by a double-digit percentage across multiple years, or one paired with an SEC enforcement action, is the warning sign that any rule tested only on the current, cleaned-up database may have been quietly scored on hindsight.

公開資料在哪裡看：怎麼自己查到一次財報重編。第一步，打開 EDGAR——每一家美股上市公司都必須申報進去的免費公開資料庫，用公司名稱加上「restate」或「restatement」搜尋。有一個限制要先知道，不然查不到會誤判：EDGAR 的全文檢索只涵蓋 2001 年以後的申報。更早的資料要改用「依表格類型瀏覽公司申報清單」的方式找，不能靠關鍵字。第二步，鎖定兩樣東西。一是代碼帶「/A」的表格，例如 10-K/A、10-Q/A（10-K 是美國上市公司每年要交的年報，10-Q 是每季要交的季報，「/A」代表修正版）。二是內容為 Item 4.02 的 8-K（公司臨時要公告重大事件時交的文件）：公司要宣告「先前公布的財報已不可信賴」，依規定就得用這個項目。一份 Item 4.02 的 8-K，是「重編正在發生」最乾淨的單一標記。第三步，打開文件，找標題大概是「Restatement of Previously Issued Financial Statements（先前公布財報之重編）」的附註。裡面會寫清楚哪幾年、哪個科目變了、變了多少。正常範圍與警訊：只動到一兩個次要科目的單次重編很常見，通常不重要。如果一次重編讓多年的營收或獲利變動達到兩位數百分比，或同時伴隨 SEC 的執法行動，那就是警訊：任何只用「現在、已清理過」資料庫測出來的規則，可能是被後見之明打的分數。

Think first: why does a slightly weaker out-of-sample result actually count as good news, not bad news?先想一下：為什麼樣本外表現稍微變差一點，其實是好消息，不是壞消息？

A rule designed and calibrated on one stretch of history will always fit that stretch slightly better than it fits data it has never seen — some of what made it look good in-sample is genuine, repeatable structure, and some of it is the design process quietly bending itself around noise specific to that window. A small, honest gap between in-sample and out-of-sample performance is the expected signature of a real, if imperfect, edge: most of the effect survived, a little bit of overfitting bled off. The dangerous pattern is not a modest gap — it is out-of-sample performance collapsing toward zero, flipping sign, or the in-sample number being suspiciously perfect in the first place. A rule that performs identically in-sample and out-of-sample, with zero degradation at all, should raise its own kind of suspicion: real market structure evolves, and a rule immune to that evolution is unusual enough to deserve a second look at how the split was actually constructed.

一條規則是在某一段歷史上設計、校準出來的，所以它一定會比較貼合那一段，勝過它從沒看過的資料。它在樣本內表現好，有兩個來源：一部分是真實、可重複的結構，一部分是設計過程不知不覺順著那段窗口特有的雜訊彎了過去。所以樣本內與樣本外之間出現一個小落差，正是一個真實但不完美的優勢該有的樣子——大部分效果留下來了，一點過度擬合被排掉了。危險的不是這個溫和落差。危險的是樣本外崩到接近零、甚至反過來變號；或者樣本內數字一開始就好得可疑。還有一種情況也該懷疑：樣本內外表現完全一樣、一點退化都沒有。市場結構會變，一條完全不受影響的規則太罕見了，值得回頭查那個切分到底是怎麼做的。

In-Sample Fit Is Not Survival: Why a Rule Must Prove Itself on Data It Has Never Seen

樣本內擬合不等於活下來：為什麼規則要先在沒看過的資料上證明自己

The mechanism: any rule with enough adjustable knobs — a threshold here, a lookback window there — can eventually be tuned to fit almost any specific stretch of history well, the same way a curve with enough bendable points can be made to pass through almost any scattered set of dots. This is called overfittingTuning a rule's parameters until it fits a specific historical dataset unusually well, at the cost of that fit having little to do with any repeatable, forward-looking edge., and the honest defense against it is walk-forward validationSplitting historical data into a training period, used to design or calibrate a rule, and a separate testing period the rule never saw during design, then checking whether performance holds up on the testing period.: split the available history into a training stretch, used to design or calibrate the rule, and a held-out testing stretch the rule was never allowed to see during design. Only performance on the testing stretch tells you anything about whether the rule found a repeatable pattern or just memorized noise. This is not an optional refinement to run after a rule looks promising — treating it as a hard precondition for adopting any rule, rather than an afterthought, is the entire point.

機制是這樣：一條規則只要有夠多可調的旋鈕——這裡一個門檻、那裡一個回顧窗口——最後幾乎都能被調到很貼合某一段特定歷史。就像一條有夠多彎折點的曲線，幾乎能穿過任何一堆散亂的點。這叫過度擬合把一條規則的參數調整到特別貼合某一份特定的歷史資料，代價是這份貼合跟任何真實、可重複、面向未來的優勢幾乎無關。。誠實的對策叫前進式驗證（樣本外驗證）把可用的歷史資料切成訓練期（用來設計或校準規則）與另一段測試期（規則在設計過程中完全沒看過），再檢查表現在測試期是否依然成立。：把歷史切成兩段，一段訓練期用來設計和校準規則，另一段測試期在設計過程中完全不准看。只有測試期的表現，才能告訴你這條規則找到的是真實可重複的型態，還是只背下了雜訊。這是採用一條規則之前的硬性前提，不是規則看起來有搞頭之後才補做的加分項。

A real illustration of how large the gap can be: academic research into the population of published stock-market “factors” — systematic characteristics claimed to predict returns — has grown to several hundred distinct candidates in the finance literature. A widely cited 2016 study by Campbell Harvey, Yan Liu, and Heqing Zhu catalogued 316 factors published in top finance journals and argued that, given how many had been tested across decades of research using heavily overlapping datasets, the conventional statistical bar for calling a result “significant” — a t-statistic of about 2.0 — is far too low. Their proposed replacement was a t-statistic of about 3.0, a bar high enough that a large share of the published factor literature would not clear it. Their point was not that every published factor is fake — it is that when hundreds of researchers each test dozens of variations against the same limited history of market data, some fraction of what gets published as a discovery is, mathematically, expected to be noise that happened to look like a pattern, and there is no way to tell which fraction without out-of-sample confirmation on data none of those studies used.

這個落差可以有多大？看一個真實例子。學術界對股市「因子」（宣稱能預測報酬的系統性特徵）已經累積出好幾百個候選項目。哈維（Campbell Harvey）、劉彥（Yan Liu）與朱赫青（Heqing Zhu）在 2016 年一篇被廣泛引用的研究裡，清點出頂尖財務期刊發表過的 316 個因子。他們主張：幾十年來這些研究反覆用著高度重疊的資料集，所以傳統判定「統計顯著」的門檻——t 值（統計檢定裡用來判斷觀察到的差異是不是只是巧合的一個指標，數字越大代表越不像巧合）大約 2.0——太低了。他們提出的替代門檻是 t 值大約 3.0，高到會讓已發表的因子文獻有相當大一部分過不了關。他們的重點不是說每個已發表的因子都是假的。而是：幾百位研究者各自對同一段有限的歷史資料測幾十種變體，數學上必然有一部分被當成「發現」發表出來的東西只是雜訊，只是碰巧長得像型態。而且在拿一份誰都沒用過的樣本外資料驗證之前，沒辦法知道是哪一部分。

The pattern predates factor research by decades. A widely cited 1992 study by William Brock, Josef Lakonishok, and Blake LeBaron tested simple moving-average and trading-range-breakout rules against nearly a century of Dow Jones Industrial Average data, from 1897 to 1986, and found that several of the rules produced statistically significant outperformance over that stretch. The finding looked like durable evidence that simple technical rules worked. Later researchers retested the same class of rules across a far larger universe of variations while formally correcting for how many rules had effectively been searched over. The result splits in two, and the split is instructive enough that this lesson returns to it in detail further down. The short version: the edge was real in the sample it was found in, and gone in the decade after it was published.

這個型態比因子研究早了幾十年就存在。布洛克（William Brock）、拉科尼修克（Josef Lakonishok）與勒巴隆（Blake LeBaron）在 1992 年一篇被廣泛引用的研究，把簡單的移動平均與區間突破規則，套用在將近一世紀的道瓊工業指數資料上（1897 到 1986 年），發現其中好幾條規則在這段期間有統計上顯著的超額報酬。這個發現當時看起來，像是簡單技術規則確實有用的扎實證據。後來的研究者把同一類規則的變體擴大到大得多的規模重測，並正式修正「等於已經搜尋過多少條規則」這件事。結果分成兩半，而這個分裂值得細看，這一課後面會再回來談。簡短版是：優勢在它被發現的那段樣本裡是真的，在它被發表之後的那 10 年裡消失了。

This connects to a companion discipline: when a research team adds a new filter, threshold, or confirmation condition to an existing rule, the addition should be tested with an ablation testRemoving or adding one single mechanism at a time and measuring its independent contribution to the result, rather than judging a bundle of changes together. — isolating exactly what that one addition contributed, independent of everything else. A genuinely counter-intuitive result shows up often enough here to be worth naming directly: a filter added specifically because it looked like it should make a rule more robust sometimes makes every performance measure worse at once, because the extra layer delays a reaction the original rule's edge actually depended on. The lesson is not “never add filters” — it is that a filter earns its place only by passing its own independent test, not by sounding more careful than the version without it.

這裡連到另一項配套紀律。研究團隊在既有規則上加一個新的濾網、門檻或確認條件時，這個新增動作要用消融測試一次只移除或加入單一一個機制，量測它獨立的貢獻，而不是把一整包改動放在一起判斷。檢驗：把這一項單獨隔離出來，看它自己貢獻了什麼。這裡常出現一個真正反直覺的結果，值得直接點名：一個「看起來就該讓規則更穩健」而加進去的濾網，有時候會讓每一項績效指標同時變差。原因是多出來的那一層，延遲了原始規則的優勢本來就依賴的反應速度。教訓不是「永遠不要加濾網」。是一個濾網要留下來，得靠通過自己的獨立測試，不是靠聽起來比較謹慎。

📜War Story戰爭故事

Long-Term Capital Management launched in 1994 with a partner list that included two future Nobel laureates in economics and a track record, in its first years, of annual returns in the high thirties and low forties percent. Its trades were built on rigorous historical modeling of how related bond and derivative prices should converge over time, sized with leverage that, by 1998, put on-balance-sheet assets above $125 billion against roughly $4.8 billion of capital — balance-sheet leverage of more than 25-to-1, with derivative exposure extending the effective bet size far beyond even that. The models had been tested extensively against historical data and treated the fund's many separate convergence trades, spread across different markets, as largely independent bets whose risks would diversify against one another.

長期資本管理公司（LTCM）1994 年成立，合夥人名單裡有兩位日後拿下諾貝爾經濟學獎的學者。成立最初幾年，年報酬率落在 30% 多到 40% 出頭之間。它的交易建立在一套嚴謹的歷史模型上：相關的債券與衍生品價格，隨時間應該怎麼收斂。倉位再用槓桿（借錢或用工具放大部位的倍數，例如 1.5 倍槓桿代表用 100 元本金操作 150 元部位）撐大。到 1998 年初，資產負債表上的資產超過 1,250 億美元，對應的資本只有大約 48 億美元，資產負債表槓桿超過 25 倍，而衍生品曝險讓實際的賭注規模比這還大。這些模型用大量歷史資料測試過。它們把基金橫跨不同市場的多筆收斂交易，當成彼此大致獨立的賭注，理論上風險會互相抵銷。

Russia's default on its domestic debt in August 1998 triggered a global flight to quality that made trades LTCM's models treated as independent move together instead — correlations across markets that had rarely moved in tandem in the historical sample used to build the models converged toward one all at once, exactly when leverage meant there was no room to absorb it. The fund lost roughly $4.6 billion in less than four months, and the Federal Reserve organized a private-sector recapitalization by fourteen banks to unwind the position in an orderly way rather than let a forced liquidation of that size destabilize the broader market. The models were not wrong about how those instruments behaved most of the time — they were tested carefully, on real historical data, and correctly described ordinary market conditions. What the historical sample used to build them had never contained was a global event large enough to make normally uncorrelated trades move as one at the same moment the fund had no spare capital to survive it.

1998 年 8 月，俄羅斯對本國債務違約，引發全球資金搶進避險資產。LTCM 模型原本視為互相獨立的交易，開始同時朝同一個方向走。那些在建模樣本裡很少同步變動的跨市場相關性，一次全部收斂到接近 1，而且剛好發生在槓桿讓基金沒有任何空間吸收衝擊的時候。基金在不到四個月內虧損大約 46 億美元。聯準會出面協調 14 家銀行做民間部門的資本重組，用有秩序的方式解除部位，而不是任由這麼大規模的強制平倉去撼動整個市場。模型對這些工具在多數時候的行為，描述得並沒有錯。它們用真實歷史資料仔細測試過，也正確描述了一般市況。它們的歷史樣本裡從來沒出現過的，是這樣一場全球事件：規模大到讓原本不相關的交易全部同步移動，而且發生在基金已經沒有剩餘資本可以撐過去的那一刻。

The speed compounded once leverage removed any room to wait it out. Equity had already fallen to roughly $2.3 billion by the end of August 1998, an August loss of about $1.8 billion on its own, while liabilities stayed above $100 billion — so every further percentage point of loss was measured against a capital base that was itself shrinking. By September 23 the position could no longer be held: a consortium of fourteen firms injected roughly $3.6 billion of new equity in exchange for 90% of the fund, leaving the original owners with the remaining 10%. The dilution is the cleanest measure of how little capital was left by then.

槓桿讓基金沒有等待轉圜的空間，惡化速度因此自我加乘。到 1998 年 8 月底，權益已經掉到大約 23 億美元，光是 8 月一個月就虧掉約 18 億美元，而負債仍在 1,000 億美元以上——之後每多虧一個百分點，對照的都是一個自己還在縮小的資本基數。到 9 月 23 日，這個部位已經撐不住：14 家機構組成的聯合體投入大約 36 億美元新資本，換走基金 90% 的權益，原始股東只剩下 10%。這個稀釋比例（新股東加入，讓原始股東的持股占比變小）就是當時還剩多少資本最清楚的度量。

The lesson to carry forward, distinct from the leverage math itself: a risk budget built on a backtested correlation structure is only as honest as the range of regimes the backtest actually contained. Diversification measured across trades that have never yet been tested by a systemic shock is not the same thing as diversification that has survived one.

要帶走的教訓跟槓桿數學是兩回事：一份風險預算如果建立在回測量出來的相關性上，它的誠實程度不會超過這份回測涵蓋過的市況範圍。一組交易還沒被系統性衝擊測試過，量出來的「分散」，跟真正撐過一次衝擊、活下來的「分散」，不是同一件事。

Worked Example: Auditing a Backtest That Looks Too Good逐步範例：審一份「看起來好到不像話」的回測

Meridian Rotation is an illustrative teaching case, not a real published product — a hypothetical monthly sector-ETF rotation strategy across ten sector ETFs, rebalanced on the first trading day of each month. Its published 2005–2024 backtest reports: CAGR 24.3%, maximum drawdown −11.8%, Sharpe ratio 2.1, zero losing calendar years. Below are the six questions worth asking before believing any of it, in the order they matter, with the answers a careful review would actually find.

「經緯輪動」是虛構的教學案例，不是真實發表過的商品：一套每月輪動 10 檔產業型 ETF 的策略，每月第一個交易日再平衡。它公布的 2005 到 2024 年回測結果是年化報酬率 24.3%、最大回撤 −11.8%、夏普比率 2.1，而且沒有一個曆年虧損。相信這份數字之前，有六個問題值得依序問。以下是問題，以及一次認真複查真的會查到的答案。

  1. 1

    Are the prices adjusted? Three of the ten ETFs underwent fund mergers or special distributions during the window that the backtest treated as raw price drops, occasionally triggering a false “buy the dip” rotation signal. Correcting for this alone brings CAGR down to about 21.8%.

    股價有還原嗎？10 檔 ETF 裡有三檔在這段窗口內經歷過基金合併或特別配發，回測把這些事件當成真實的股價下跌處理，偶爾觸發了假的「逢低買進」輪動訊號。光是修正這一項，年化報酬率就降到 約 21.8%。

  2. 2

    Is the universe point-in-time? The ten ETFs used were selected as “the ten most liquid sector ETFs today” and applied backward — three of the sector ETFs that existed and were reasonably liquid for parts of the 2005–2012 stretch were excluded because they later merged out of existence or lost relevance. Rebuilding the universe from what actually existed and traded at each historical date, using fifteen candidate ETFs instead of ten, brings CAGR down further to about 17.4%.

    母體是時點正確的嗎？這 10 檔 ETF 是用「今天流動性最好的 10 檔」挑出來，再往回套用。有三檔在 2005 到 2012 年部分時段確實存在、流動性也還可以的產業 ETF 被排除了，因為它們後來合併消失或不再重要。改用「每個歷史時點實際存在並交易」的母體重建，候選從 10 檔擴大到 15 檔，年化報酬率再降到 約 17.4%。

  3. 3

    Was it walk-forward tested? The published number is a single 20-year in-sample fit with no held-out period. Splitting the same 20 years into a 10-year training window and a separate 10-year test window shows the Sharpe ratio falling from 2.1 in-sample to about 0.9 out-of-sample — a meaningful gap, though not a collapse to zero.

    有做過前進式驗證嗎？公布的數字是單一一段 20 年的樣本內擬合，沒有留出任何一段測試期。把同樣 20 年切成 10 年訓練窗口與另外 10 年測試窗口，可以看到夏普比率從樣本內的 2.1，降到樣本外的 約 0.9——落差不小，但沒有崩到零。

  4. 4

    Was timing luck audited? The rule always rebalances on the first trading day of the month. Rerunning the identical rule on each of the roughly twenty other trading days available in a typical month produces Calmar ratios ranging from about 0.6 to about 1.3 — and the first-trading-day anchor, the one actually published, happens to be the best-performing choice in the set.

    有做時機運氣審計嗎？規則固定在每月第一個交易日再平衡。把完全相同的規則，換成一般月份裡其他大約 20 個交易日各跑一次，卡瑪比率（Calmar，另一種衡量報酬相對痛苦的比率，用年化報酬除以最大回撤，數字越高越好）落在約 0.6 到約 1.3 之間。而「第一個交易日」這個被拿去公布的錨點，剛好就是這組裡表現最好的那一個。

  5. 5

    Is trading cost modeled? The published backtest assumes zero slippage and zero commission. Monthly rotation replaces roughly half of the ten-position book each month, which is about six full round trips of the portfolio per year; at a conservative round-trip cost of 0.30% in slippage and commission, honest cost modeling removes 6 × 0.30% ≈ 1.8 percentage points of CAGR per year.

    有把交易成本建模進去嗎？公布的回測假設零滑價、零手續費。每月輪動會換掉 10 檔部位裡大約一半，等於整個組合一年來回大約六趟；以保守估計、含滑價與手續費約 0.30% 的來回成本計，誠實建模成本後每年會從年化報酬率裡扣掉 6 × 0.30% ≈ 1.8 個百分點。

  6. 6

    Is performance reported by regime, not just full-sample? Excluding the 2008–2009 financial crisis and the 2020 pandemic crash, almost all of the strategy's edge over simply holding a broad index comes from the 2009–2021 bull run; inside the two crash windows specifically, it performs no better than a passive benchmark, and briefly worse. The full-sample headline number buries this entirely.

    有沒有分市場狀態報告，而不是只給全樣本一個數字？把 2008 到 2009 年金融海嘯與 2020 年疫情崩盤排除掉之後可以看到：這套策略相對於單純持有大盤的優勢，幾乎全部來自 2009 到 2021 年的多頭。單獨看那兩段崩盤窗口，它不比被動基準好，甚至一度更差。全樣本的頭條數字，把這件事完全埋掉了。

The composite is fictional so that all six failures can be shown stacking on one report. Each failure individually is drawn from a documented counterpart named elsewhere in this lesson — real splits, a real factor-count study, a real weekday audit — not from imagination.

用虛構的複合案例，是為了讓六個失誤能同時疊在同一份報告上展示。每一個失誤單獨拿出來看，都有一個真實、有紀錄可查的對應版本，這一課別的地方都點名過：真實的股票分割、真實的因子清點研究、真實的週幾審計。沒有一個是憑空想像的。

Takeaway收斂

Six questions turned “CAGR 24.3%, Sharpe 2.1, no losing years, must-buy” into “a real but modest and regime-dependent edge, roughly Sharpe 0.9 out-of-sample, that adds nothing during the two crashes an investor would most want protection in.” Both descriptions are consistent with the exact same underlying rule — only one of them is honest enough to size a position on.

六個問題，把「年化 24.3%、夏普 2.1、沒有虧損年、非買不可」翻成了另一句話：「優勢是真的，但溫和，有沒有用要看市場狀態，樣本外夏普大約 0.9，而且在投資人最需要保護的那兩段崩盤裡完全幫不上忙。」兩句話講的是同一條規則。只有其中一句誠實到可以拿來決定部位大小。

Timing Luck: When the Calendar Itself Is a Hidden Free Parameter

時機運氣：連日曆本身都是一個藏起來的自由參數

Every rule that operates on a fixed schedule — rebalance monthly, judge trend weekly, roll options on expiration — has to pick a specific anchor within that schedule: which day of the month, which day of the week. That choice rarely gets treated as a parameter worth testing, because it does not feel like a trading decision the way a threshold or a lookback window does. It is one anyway. Timing luckThe portion of a backtest's performance that comes from an arbitrary but unexamined implementation choice, such as which specific date a rule rebalances on, rather than from the rule's actual logic. is the performance difference that shows up purely from which anchor got picked, holding every other part of the rule's logic identical.

任何按固定週期運作的規則——每月再平衡、每週判斷趨勢、選擇權到期日轉倉——都得在週期裡挑一個具體錨點：每月第幾天、每週第幾天。這個選擇很少被當成值得測試的參數，因為它感覺不像門檻或回顧窗口那樣是「交易決策」。但它就是。時機運氣一份回測的表現裡，來自某個任意、卻從未被檢驗過的實作選擇（例如規則具體在哪一天再平衡）的那一部分，而不是來自規則本身真正的邏輯。指的是：規則的其他邏輯完全不變，光靠錨點選了哪一個，就冒出來的表現差異。

A timing-luck auditSystematically rerunning an identical rule across every reasonable choice of an implementation detail that was never treated as a free parameter, such as the specific weekday used for a weekly signal, to see how much the headline result depends on that one arbitrary choice. makes this visible: rerun the identical rule across every reasonable choice of the anchor and look at the spread of results. A rule with a genuine edge shows a modest, tight spread — the specific day matters a little, not a lot. A rule whose headline number depends heavily on one particular anchor, especially when that anchor happens to be exactly the one published, is telling you that a meaningful share of its apparent edge is not edge at all.

時機運氣審計系統性地把完全相同的規則，套用在每一個合理的實作細節選項上重跑一遍（例如週線訊號具體用週幾，這個細節從來沒被當成自由參數處理過），藉此看頭條數字有多依賴這一個任意選擇。能讓這件事現形：把完全相同的規則，套在每一個合理的錨點上各跑一次，看結果散得多開。真的有優勢的規則，散布會很收斂——具體哪一天有影響，但影響不大。如果頭條數字嚴重依賴某一個特定錨點，而那個錨點剛好就是被拿去公布的那一個，等於在告訴你：它表面上的優勢，有相當一部分根本不是優勢。

The counter-example worth naming: a report that discloses the full distribution of results across every anchor tested, not just the best one, and states its headline number as a range or a median rather than the single most flattering data point. That kind of disclosure is rare precisely because it makes the headline look less impressive — which is exactly why its presence is a positive signal about the report's honesty.

值得點名的反例是這樣一份報告：它揭露每一個測過的錨點的完整分布，不是只給最好的那一個；頭條數字用區間或中位數表達，不是用最漂亮的單一數字。這種揭露很罕見，因為它會讓頭條看起來沒那麼亮眼。也正因為如此，它一旦出現，就是誠實度的正面訊號。

A real, long-documented instance of the same fragility: investment banker Sidney Wachtel wrote up what came to be called the January effect in a 1942 article, observing in decades of prior data that small-company stocks had tended to outperform the broader market disproportionately in January. For decades afterward this looked like a durable, exploitable calendar pattern, commonly attributed to year-end tax-loss selling unwinding and January reinvestment. It did not hold up as a reliable edge going forward. Burton Malkiel's account is that the effect became undependable soon after it was publicized. G. William Schwert's 2003 survey of market anomalies states the finding more carefully, and the care is the lesson: the small-firm turn-of-the-year effect “became weaker in the years after it was first documented in the academic literature, although there is some evidence that it still exists.” Not deleted — degraded, to a size that no longer clears the cost of trading it. The mechanism is exactly the one this section is built around — a calendar anchor that was never treated as a free parameter, tested against decades of history, degraded once it became widely known and widely traded against.

同一種脆弱性，有一個真實、被記錄了幾十年的例子：投資銀行家瓦切爾（Sidney Wachtel）在 1942 年一篇文章裡，最早寫下後來被稱為「一月效應」的現象——他在往前數十年的資料裡觀察到，小型股在一月往往會不成比例地跑贏大盤。之後好幾十年，這看起來像一個穩定、可以拿來套利的日曆型態，一般歸因於年底節稅賣壓在年初解除、加上一月的資金再投入。它後來並沒有維持成一個可靠的優勢。墨基爾（Burton Malkiel）的說法是，這個效應在被公開討論之後很快就變得不可靠。史沃特（G. William Schwert）2003 年那篇市場異常現象的綜述講得更謹慎，而這份謹慎本身就是重點：小型股的年底年初效應「在學術文獻首次記錄之後的那些年變弱了，不過也有一些證據顯示它還存在」。不是消失——是退化到一個小得不再蓋得過交易成本的幅度。這正是這一節圍繞的機制：一個從沒被當成自由參數處理過的日曆錨點，套用在幾十年歷史上測試出來時很像真的，一旦廣為人知、廣被交易之後，就退化了。

📜War Story戰爭故事

A systematic research team, working on a rules-based trend-following engine, widened the threshold that triggers a rebalancing trade — a change made explicitly to cut the number of trades and notifications generated each year, not to chase a higher return. As a side effect, one market's headline Calmar ratio also happened to improve. Rather than accepting that as a bonus, the team ran the specific audit this lesson describes: they reran the identical widened-threshold rule using each of five different weekdays as the weekly rebalancing anchor. On four of the five weekday choices, the same threshold change made the Calmar ratio worse. Only on the weekday that had actually been adopted in production did it improve — and that was the only version anyone had looked at before the audit.

一個做規則化趨勢跟蹤引擎的研究團隊，把觸發再平衡交易的門檻放寬了。動機講得很明白：減少每年的交易與通知次數，不是追求更高報酬。結果順帶發生一件事——其中一個市場的頭條卡瑪比率也跟著變好了。團隊沒有把這當成白撿的紅利收下。他們跑了這一課講的那個審計：把完全相同、已經放寬門檻的規則，換五個不同的週幾當週線再平衡錨點，各跑一遍。五個裡有四個，同一個門檻改動讓卡瑪比率變差。只有實際被拿去使用的那一個週幾變好。而那個版本，正是審計之前唯一一個有人看過的版本。

The team had a standing internal rule stating that any performance change coming from an execution-layer adjustment should be treated as noise, not a claimed improvement, until proven otherwise — the audit confirmed that rule had been right from the start. The change to the threshold was kept, because reducing trading frequency was genuinely worth doing on its own terms. The improved Calmar ratio was not kept as a claim, because the audit showed it was not real.

這個團隊本來就有一條既定規則：任何來自執行層調整的績效變化，在證明不是雜訊之前一律當成雜訊，不能拿去宣稱是改善。這次審計證實了這條規則從一開始就是對的。門檻的改動被保留下來，因為降低交易頻率本身就有獨立成立的價值。但那個變好的卡瑪比率沒有被拿去宣稱，因為審計證明它不是真的。

One caveat worth carrying forward from this specific audit: it tells you the truth about the sample it was run on, not about every future sample. US equity settlement itself moved from a two-business-day cycle to a one-business-day cycle in May 2024, changing exactly the kind of mechanical, calendar-driven plumbing that determines which weekday is operationally convenient for a rebalance. A timing-luck audit run once, years before a structural change like that, does not automatically stay valid afterward — the discipline is to rerun the audit periodically, not to treat a single clean result as permanent.

這次審計還有一個值得記住的但書：它告訴你的是「在跑這次審計的那份樣本上」是真的，不是在未來每一份樣本上都是真的。美國股票的交割週期本身，在 2024 年 5 月從兩個營業日縮短為一個營業日，改變的正是那種決定「哪個週幾在操作上方便再平衡」的機械式、日曆驅動的底層管線。一次幾年前跑過的時機運氣審計，不會因為結果乾淨，就自動在這類結構性改變之後繼續有效——正確的紀律是定期重跑這項審計，而不是把單一次乾淨的結果當成永久有效。

Optimize the Logic, Not the Parameters

要優化的是邏輯，不是參數

There is an honest order of operations and a dishonest one, and they use exactly the same software. The honest order argues about the mechanism first — should this filter exist at all, what is the causal reason a market would pay for this behavior, what would have to be true about supply, demand, or investor structure for the effect to persist — and only then measures. The dishonest order runs a grid of parameter values first and adopts whichever cell scored highest, then writes the causal story afterward to explain the winner. The second order is guaranteed to produce a good-looking number, which is exactly the problem: the highest cell in any grid is, by construction, the one that absorbed the most noise, because noise is what varies across cells that the mechanism does not distinguish between.

同一套軟體，可以跑出誠實的順序，也可以跑出不誠實的順序。誠實的順序是先吵清楚機制：這個濾網該不該存在？市場為什麼會為這種行為付錢？供給、需求或投資人結構要滿足什麼條件，這個效果才可能持續？講清楚這些，才動手量。不誠實的順序是先跑一輪參數格點，挑分數最高的那一格採用，事後再補一段因果故事解釋這個贏家。第二種順序保證跑得出漂亮數字，問題正在這裡。任何一張格點裡分數最高的那一格，結構上必然是吸收最多雜訊的那一格——因為機制分不出差別的格子之間，會變的就只剩雜訊。

This does not mean a parameter sweep is worthless. It means it answers a different question than most people use it for. Run the sweep to look at the shape, not to pick the peak: what matters is the parameter response surfaceThe pattern of how a strategy's performance changes as a parameter is varied across its plausible range — a flat plateau suggests the edge comes from the mechanism, a narrow spike suggests it was fitted to this particular history.. If a trend rule scores about the same using a 40-week, a 52-week, and a 60-week lookback, the edge probably lives in the mechanism rather than in the number, and the specific number is close to arbitrary. If it scores well at exactly 47 weeks and falls off steeply at 45 and 49, that spike is a fingerprint of this particular stretch of history, not a discovery. The counter-intuitive practical consequence is worth stating plainly, because it reverses what most people do: given a choice between the single highest-scoring setting and a slightly lower-scoring setting sitting in the middle of a wide, flat plateau, the plateau is the better choice. The peak is where the fit is; the plateau is where the mechanism is.

這不代表參數掃描沒用。只代表它回答的問題，跟多數人拿它來回答的問題不一樣。跑掃描是為了看形狀，不是為了挑山頂。要看的是參數響應曲面當一個參數在合理範圍內變動時，策略表現跟著怎麼變的整體型態——平坦的高原代表優勢來自機制，狹窄的尖峰代表它是被貼合到這一段特定歷史上的。。如果一條趨勢規則用 40 週、52 週、60 週回顧期，跑出來的分數都差不多，那優勢多半住在機制裡，不在那個數字裡，具體用幾週幾乎是任意的。如果它剛好在 47 週表現很好，45 週跟 49 週卻明顯掉下來，那個尖峰是這一段歷史的指紋，不是一項發現。這裡有一個反直覺的結論，值得直說，因為它跟多數人的做法正好相反：要在「分數最高的設定」跟「分數稍低、但坐在一片寬闊平坦高原正中間的設定」之間選，該選高原。山頂是擬合待的地方，高原才是機制待的地方。

The correction referenced two sections back has a name and a real illustration worth knowing: Ryan Sullivan, Allan Timmermann, and Halbert White retested the same class of Dow Jones moving-average and breakout rules across a universe of 7,846 rule variations, using a bootstrap built specifically to account for how much searching the technical-trading literature had, in effect, already done. Their result is more interesting than a simple debunking. On the original 1897–1986 window the best rule held up even after all 7,846 candidates were accounted for — that historical edge was not merely a lucky draw from a big haystack. Then on the following decade, 1987 to 1996, the best rule was not even statistically significant. Two separate questions were being answered: how much credit a result deserves given how many rules were tried, and whether the result keeps paying once it is known. A rule can pass the first test and still fail the second.

前面提到的那次修正，有名字也有一個值得記住的真實示範：沙利文（Ryan Sullivan）、提摩曼（Allan Timmermann）與懷特（Halbert White）三人，把同一類道瓊移動平均與區間突破規則擴大到 7,846 條變體重測，用的是一種專門設計來修正「技術分析文獻其實已經搜尋過多少規則」的拔靴法（bootstrap，一種靠重複隨機抽樣歷史資料、估計統計結果可不可靠的方法）。他們的結果比單純的「推翻」更有意思。在原本 1897 到 1986 年那段樣本上，把全部 7,846 條候選都算進去之後，最好的那條規則依然站得住——那段歷史優勢不只是從大海裡撈到的運氣。但到了接下來的 10 年，1987 到 1996 年，最好的那條規則連統計顯著都達不到。這裡其實在回答兩個不同的問題：一個結果在「試過多少條規則」之後還值多少分，以及這個結果被知道之後還會不會繼續有用。一條規則可以通過第一個測試，卻在第二個測試上失敗。

The failure mode this discipline is really defending against is gradual, and it has a name worth carrying: the parameter-picking slippery slopeA sequence of individually defensible parameter loosenings, where each step cites the unverified performance improvement produced by the previous step as its justification, ending somewhere nobody would have accepted in one move.. Each step is defensible on its own terms. What makes it a slope is that each step uses the previous step's unverified improvement as its reason. The timing-luck case earlier in this lesson is the first step of exactly such a sequence: a threshold was widened for a stated, legitimate reason — fewer trades — and a risk-adjusted return figure happened to improve. The tempting next move writes itself: widening helped, so widen further. The defense is a flat prohibition stated in advance — a performance improvement that has not survived its own audit may never be cited as the reason to loosen the same class of threshold again. How to spot the slope from the outside, in a report you did not write: read the revision history rather than the current version. A rule whose thresholds have moved three times, always in the same direction, each move citing the result of the last one, has been optimized on parameters just as surely as if someone had run a grid search — it simply took three years instead of three minutes.

這項紀律真正要防的失效是漸進式的，而且它有一個值得記住的名字：參數擇優滑坡一連串單獨看都說得通的參數放寬動作，每一步都拿前一步那個未經驗證的績效改善當作理由，最後滑到一個沒有人會一次同意的位置。。每一步單獨看都站得住腳。讓它變成滑坡的，是每一步都拿前一步那個「還沒被驗證過」的改善當理由。這一課前面那則時機運氣的案例，就是這種連鎖的第一步：門檻為了一個明講、正當的理由被放寬（減少交易次數），而某個風險調整報酬數字剛好跟著變好。下一步的誘惑幾乎自動成形：放寬有幫助，那就再放寬一點。防線是一條事先講明的禁令：一個還沒通過審計的績效改善，永遠不能拿來當「所以要再放寬同一類門檻」的理由。在一份不是你寫的報告裡怎麼看出這條滑坡？去讀修訂歷史，不要只讀當前版本。一條門檻改過三次、每次都往同一個方向、每次都引用上一次結果當理由——它被參數優化的程度，跟有人真的跑了一輪格點搜尋沒有兩樣。只是它花了三年，不是三分鐘。

One more thing a backtest quietly hides, even a well-built one: it is a single path. Market history handed everyone exactly one sequence of events, and a headline maximum drawdown is the worst thing that happened along that one sequence — not the worst thing that could plausibly have happened to the same rule. This is single-path noiseThe uncertainty that remains because history delivered only one sequence of events, so a backtest measures what happened to a rule along one path rather than the range of outcomes the rule was actually exposed to., and the honest handles for it are practical. First, count the independent bets rather than the data rows: a twenty-year monthly rotation looks impressive as five thousand daily observations, but it is 240 monthly decisions, and if the rotation is really driven by market regime, it may rest on a dozen or so genuinely independent episodes. More rows do not create more evidence. Second, resample: rerun the same rule with a randomized starting month, or on bootstrapped blocks of the return series, and see whether the headline drawdown moves a little or a lot. A maximum drawdown that ranges from −18% to −34% across resamples of the same history is telling you that “−18%” was never a property of the rule — it was a property of where the sample happened to start.

還有一件事，連做得很好的回測也會安靜藏起來：它只是一條路徑。市場歷史交給所有人的，就只有一串事件順序。頭條的最大回撤，是這一串順序上實際發生過最糟的事，不是同一條規則「合理可能遭遇到」最糟的事。這叫單一路徑雜訊因為歷史只給出一串事件順序而殘留下來的不確定性——回測量到的是規則在這一條路徑上發生了什麼，不是這條規則實際暴露在哪些可能結果的範圍裡。，處理的辦法很實際。第一，數獨立的賭注次數，不要數資料筆數。一套 20 年的月頻輪動，用五千根日線來看很有份量，但它其實只有 240 次月度決策；如果驅動它的真正力量是市場狀態，它可能只靠十來次真正獨立的行情事件撐著。筆數變多不會讓證據變多。第二，重抽樣。把同一條規則換一個隨機的起始月份重跑，或對報酬序列做區塊重抽樣再跑，看頭條回撤是小動還是大動。如果最大回撤在 −18% 到 −34% 之間跑來跑去，等於在告訴你：「−18%」從來不是這條規則的性質，它是「樣本剛好從哪裡開始」的性質。

Underneath all of this sits a single reframing that changes how a backtest gets used. The purpose of running one is not to confirm that an idea works; it is to try as hard as possible to kill it, and to adopt it only if it survives. The operational version of that question is not “how good does this look” but “what result would this test have had to produce for me to abandon the idea — and did I actually run the version of the test capable of producing that result.” A researcher who cannot answer the second half has not tested anything; they have decorated a decision that was already made.

這一切底下有一個重新定位，會改變回測的用法。跑回測的目的不是確認一個想法可行，是盡最大努力去殺死它，殺不死才採用。實際操作的問法不是「這看起來有多好」，而是兩句話：這個測試要跑出什麼結果，我才會放棄這個想法？我實際上有沒有跑那個做得出這種結果的測試版本？答不出後半句的研究者，其實什麼都沒測過。他只是在裝飾一個早就做好的決定。

Takeaway收斂

Pick the plateau, not the peak; never let an unaudited improvement justify the next loosening; and remember the headline drawdown is a property of one path through history, not a property of the rule.

選高原，不要選山頂；永遠不要讓一個沒通過審計的改善，變成下一次放寬的理由；並且記住，頭條的最大回撤是「歷史上這一條路徑」的性質，不是那條規則本身的性質。

Hidden Variables in Comparison Tables, and Honest Cost Attribution

對照表裡藏著的變數，以及誠實的成本歸因

A comparison table with two columns and one clearly labeled variable invites a reader to assume that variable is the only thing that changed. It is a natural assumption and, often, a wrong one. An apples-to-apples comparisonA comparison where every condition except the one variable being studied is held identical between the two things being compared — the opposite of a table that silently varies two things at once. requires actively confirming that every setting other than the one under discussion is genuinely held constant — not assuming it because the table's formatting implies it. The error is not arithmetic: it lives in what the layout silently implies, not in any single cell.

一張兩欄對照表，標明了一個變數，讀者就會自然假設那是唯一改變的東西。這個假設很自然，卻經常是錯的。同條件比較除了正在研究的那一個變數之外，其餘所有條件在被比較的兩者之間都完全維持一致——跟一張悄悄同時改變兩個變數的表格正好相反。的意思是：你要主動確認除了正在討論的那個變數之外，其餘每一項設定真的沒動過。不能因為排版看起來像那樣，就當作是那樣。錯不在算術，在排版悄悄暗示了什麼。

A related discipline covers trading cost specifically: turnoverThe total amount of position change within a period, typically expressed as a percentage of assets bought or sold — used to estimate the trading cost a strategy incurs. and slippageThe gap between the price a signal was calculated at and the price actually achieved when the trade executed, usually caused by liquidity or timing delay. must be modeled explicitly, and any return gap between two configurations must be split into the part explained by different trading costs and the part that is a genuine difference in edge. A common misjudgment is seeing that two configurations have different turnover and assuming the entire performance gap between them comes from cost, when part of it is a real difference in what the two rules chose to hold.

另一項相關紀律專門針對交易成本：周轉率一段期間內部位變動的總量，通常用買進或賣出金額占資產比重表示——用來估算一套策略會承受多少交易成本。與滑價訊號算出來的價格，跟交易實際成交價之間的落差，通常由流動性不足或執行延遲造成。都必須顯性建模。兩個版本之間的報酬落差，也必須拆成兩塊：由交易成本差異解釋的那一塊，跟真正屬於優勢差異的那一塊。常見的誤判是看到兩個版本周轉率不同，就假設整段績效落差都來自成本。實務上誠實拆下來，成本往往只解釋落差裡的一小部分，剩下的才是兩條規則真正選擇持有的東西不一樣。

A real illustration of how large this gap can be: Narasimhan Jegadeesh and Sheridan Titman's 1993 study of price momentum in US stocks — buying recent past winners and selling recent past losers — reported raw outperformance on the order of roughly 1% per month for the winner basket over the loser basket, a headline figure that has circulated in finance research ever since. What that number does not show on its own is how much survives realistic trading costs, because a momentum portfolio is rebuilt frequently and is made, by construction, of stocks that just moved sharply and skew smaller and less liquid — the conditions under which slippage runs highest. How much of the 1% the costs eat is genuinely contested. Robert Novy-Marx and Mihail Velikov, surveying a broad set of published anomalies in 2016, found the dividing line is turnover rather than the raw cost estimate: anomalies whose one-sided monthly turnover runs below roughly 50% mostly keep a statistically significant return after realistic costs, and most of the higher-turnover ones do not. Momentum sits on the wrong side of that line, which is why Lesmond, Schill and Zhou titled their 2004 study of it “The Illusory Nature of Momentum Profits.” Pulling the other way, Frazzini, Israel and Moskowitz measured $1.7 trillion of actual institutional trades across 21 developed markets and concluded real-world costs are an order of magnitude smaller than the academic models assumed. That disagreement is the point: a reader who takes the raw figure as an implementable return has not skipped a small refinement, they have skipped the one step where the answer is still being argued about.

這個落差可以有多大，一個真實的例子：傑加迪許（Narasimhan Jegadeesh）與提特曼（Sheridan Titman）1993 年關於美股動能的研究（買進近期表現最好的股票、賣出近期表現最差的股票）記錄到贏家組合相對輸家組合，未扣成本的原始超額報酬大約每月 1%。這個頭條數字從此在金融學術界流傳。它本身沒告訴你的是，扣掉真實交易成本之後還剩多少。動能組合要頻繁重建，而且依照建構方式，裡面的股票本來就是剛剛劇烈變動過、規模偏小、流動性偏差的那一批，剛好是滑價最高的條件。至於成本會吃掉這 1% 的多少，學術界到今天還在吵。諾維馬克斯（Robert Novy-Marx）與維利科夫（Mihail Velikov）2016 年掃過一大批已發表的異常現象，發現分界線不在成本估計本身，而在周轉率：單邊月周轉率低於大約 50% 的策略，扣掉真實成本後多數還保有統計顯著的報酬；周轉率更高的那些，多數就沒有了。動能正好落在錯的那一邊——這也是萊斯蒙（David Lesmond）、席爾（Michael Schill）與周（Chunsheng Zhou）2004 年那篇研究直接取名為「動能利潤的虛幻本質」的原因。往反方向拉的證據也在：弗拉齊尼（Andrea Frazzini）、伊斯萊爾（Ronen Israel）與莫斯科維茲（Tobias Moskowitz）用 21 個成熟市場、總額 1.7 兆美元的真實機構成交資料量出來的結論是，實務成本比學術模型假設的小了一個數量級。這個分歧本身就是重點：只看原始數字、就當成可執行報酬的讀者，跳過的不是一個小修正，而是答案還在被爭論的那一步。

How to see either problem from a published report: for hidden variables, read the table's footnotes for exactly which rule version, parameter set, and time window each column used, and treat any table that does not specify this for both columns as unverified. For cost attribution, look for a report that states a cost-only estimate in percentage points separately from the total return gap; a report that only shows a final net number, with no cost breakdown at all, has not actually demonstrated where its edge comes from.

怎麼從公開報告裡看出這兩個問題：關於藏起來的變數，去讀表格的註腳，確認每一欄用的規則版本、參數組合、時間窗口分別是什麼，任何一張沒有對兩欄都講清楚這些的表格，就當成尚未查證。關於成本歸因，找報告有沒有把「純成本估計」用百分點單獨列出來，跟總報酬落差分開講；一份只給最後淨數字、完全沒有成本拆解的報告，其實還沒有真正證明它的優勢是從哪裡來的。

📜War Story戰爭故事

A widely circulated internal leverage comparison table, cited repeatedly as evidence for how much extra return a leveraged configuration produced, put a 1.5× leveraged version running under a newer, wider execution-layer ruleset side by side with a 1.0× unleveraged version running under the older, narrower ruleset — presented as a clean, single-variable leverage comparison. It was not one. Two things had changed between the columns at once: the leverage multiple and the execution rules governing how often and how aggressively the system traded. Every individual number in both columns was calculated correctly; the error was not arithmetic. It was that the table's layout implicitly told readers only one variable had moved, when in fact two had.

有一張槓桿對照表被反覆引用，用來證明「槓桿版本多賺了多少」。它把兩欄並排：左邊是 1.5 倍槓桿版本，用較新、較寬鬆的執行層規則跑出來；右邊是 1.0 倍無槓桿版本，用較舊、較窄的執行層規則跑出來。看起來是一張乾淨的、只差一個變數的槓桿對照表。它不是。兩欄之間同時變了兩件事：槓桿倍數，以及決定系統交易頻率與力道的執行層規則。每一格數字的計算都是對的，問題不在算術。問題在排版隱含告訴讀者只有一個變數變了，實際上變了兩個。

Nobody caught it by eyeballing the numbers, because there was nothing wrong with any single number to catch. It surfaced only when someone asked the specific question this lesson recommends: besides the variable this table claims to be about, what else is different between the two columns? The fix was not a recalculation — it was rebuilding the comparison with the execution ruleset held constant on both sides, isolating the leverage variable on its own.

沒有人光看數字就抓到這件事，因為沒有任何一個數字算錯。它是在有人問出這一課建議的那個問題之後才浮現的：除了這張表宣稱要比較的變數，兩欄之間還有什麼不一樣？修正的方法不是重算，是重建一張兩欄都固定同一套執行層規則的表，把槓桿這個變數真正單獨隔離出來。

A cheaper tell, available before anyone had to rebuild anything: the footnotes to each column typically also report a trade count or turnover figure, and the two rulesets in question traded at meaningfully different frequencies. A reader who compared those two numbers before trusting the leverage claim would have seen a mismatch that had nothing to do with leverage at all — a five-minute cross-check that would have flagged the second hidden variable without needing to know either ruleset's exact mechanics.

還有一個更便宜的線索，早在要重建任何東西之前就存在：每一欄的註腳通常會附交易次數或周轉率，而這兩套規則的交易頻率明顯不一樣。讀者只要在相信槓桿宣稱之前先比一下這兩個數字，就會看到一個跟槓桿完全無關的落差。五分鐘的交叉核對，不必知道任何一套規則的機制細節，就能把第二個藏起來的變數標出來。

Think first: why is a rule's adjustable format — say, whether it rebalances weekly or monthly — also something that needs a kill condition, not just a good backtest?先想一下：為什麼連規則的格式本身——比如每週再平衡還是每月再平衡——也需要一個撤回條件，不能只靠一份漂亮的回測就定案？

A good backtest answers “did this work in the past,” not “will this keep working, and how would I know if it stopped.” Committing in advance to a specific, falsifiable condition under which the rule would be downgraded or retired forces a decision to be made before results are known, rather than after a loss has already happened and hindsight is doing the deciding. Without that commitment, every future underperformance gets explained away as noise, and every future outperformance gets kept as confirmation — a one-way ratchet that no amount of backtesting rigor protects against.

好的回測回答的是「這在過去有沒有用」，不是「這會不會繼續有用，還有它失效的話我怎麼知道」。事先講好一個具體、可證偽的條件，一旦出現就把規則降級或撤回，等於強迫你在結果出來之前先做這個決定，而不是等虧損發生、後見之明開始主導的時候才決定。沒有這個事先承諾，未來每一次表現不佳都會被解釋成雜訊，每一次表現超標都會被留下來當成確認。這是一個單向棘輪，回測做得再嚴謹都擋不住。

From an Honest Number to a Risk Budget You Can Actually Trust

從誠實的數字，到一份你真的能信任的風險預算

Everything in this lesson so far has been about getting an honest number out of a backtest. What that number is actually for is sizing — deciding how much of a portfolio, and how much leverage on top of it, a given rule can responsibly carry. This is where an inflated backtest stops being an academic embarrassment and starts being a real financial injury: a maximum drawdown understated by survivorship bias, or a volatility estimate that never lived through a true tail event, feeds directly into a leverage or position-size decision that will be wrong in exactly the direction that hurts — too large, discovered on the one day it matters.

這一課到這裡講的都是怎麼從回測裡拿到一個誠實的數字。那個數字真正的用途是決定部位大小：一條規則能扛多少比例的組合，上面還能再疊多少槓桿。這也是灌水的回測從「難堪」變成「真的虧錢」的地方。被存活者偏誤低估的最大回撤，或者從沒經歷過尾端事件的波動率估計，會直接餵進槓桿與部位大小的決定。而它錯的方向剛好最傷人——部位放太大，而且要到最要命的那一天才被發現。

Two disciplines close the loop between an honest backtest and a defensible risk budget. The first is pre-registrationFreezing a rule's parameters and thresholds before seeing how they perform, publicly committing to them in advance so they cannot be quietly adjusted after the fact based on results.: freezing a rule's parameters and thresholds before its results are known, so nobody can quietly nudge a threshold after the fact once the outcome is visible. Attached to it is a kill conditionA specific, falsifiable piece of evidence, agreed on in advance, that would cause a rule to be downgraded or retired — a rule without one cannot be distinguished from a permanent, unfalsifiable assumption. — a specific, falsifiable piece of evidence, stated in advance, that would cause the rule to be downgraded or dropped. A rule that nobody can articulate a kill condition for is not a tested hypothesis; it is an assumption wearing a backtest as a costume, and no amount of historical rigor changes that.

有兩項紀律，把「誠實的回測」跟「站得住腳的風險預算」之間的迴路接起來。第一項是預註冊在看到規則的表現之前，先把它的參數與門檻凍結下來，公開事先承諾，這樣結果出來之後就沒辦法悄悄回頭調整門檻。：結果還沒出來之前先把參數與門檻凍結，這樣結果一出現，就沒有人能回頭悄悄微調。跟它一起的是撤回條件事先講好、具體且可證偽的一項證據，一旦出現就會讓這條規則被降級或撤回——一條說不出撤回條件的規則，跟一個永遠不會被推翻的假設沒有分別。——事先講好、具體、可證偽的一項證據，一旦出現就要把規則降級或撤回。說不出撤回條件的規則不是被測試過的假設，是一個披著回測外衣的假設。歷史驗證做得再嚴謹，也改不了這一點。

The second discipline is publishing negative results with the same rigor and the same visibility as positive ones. A research process that only ever shows the rules that worked, while quietly discarding every configuration that failed a pre-registered test, is running the exact multiple-comparisons problem this lesson's calculator illustrates — just at the institutional level rather than the individual-parameter level. “This candidate did not clear the bar that was set for it in advance” is not a failed report; it is data, and a research process that treats it with less rigor than a success story is quietly rebuilding the same overfitting problem it claims to have solved.

第二項紀律，是用跟正面結果一樣的嚴謹度、一樣的能見度發布負面結果。只展示成功的規則、悄悄丟掉每個沒過關的版本，等於把這一課試算器示範的多重比較問題，從單一參數層級搬到整個研究流程層級再做一次。「這個候選沒有通過事先設定的門檻」不是失敗的報告，它是資料。把這種結果看得比成功故事輕的流程，等於悄悄重建了它自己聲稱已經解決的過度擬合問題。

Here is this lesson's explicit boundary, and it is worth stating without softening: no backtest, however carefully built — adjusted prices, point-in-time universe, walk-forward validated, timing-luck audited, apples-to-apples costed, pre-registered with a kill condition — can validate a market regime that has never occurred inside its sample. This is not a flaw to be engineered away with more rigor; it is the honest ceiling on what the entire discipline can promise. The correct response is not to distrust the numbers — it is to size positions and leverage with room to survive a day the backtest never saw, which is exactly what a risk budget, rather than a return target, is for.

這是這一課明確的邊界，值得不打折扣地講清楚。不管一份回測做得多嚴謹（還原股價、時點正確的母體、樣本外驗證、時機運氣審計、成本拆解、預註冊撤回條件），它都無法驗證一種從沒在它樣本裡出現過的市況。這不是靠更多嚴謹度就能工程掉的缺陷，是這整套紀律能承諾的誠實天花板。正確的回應不是不信任這些數字，而是把部位與槓桿留出餘裕，撐得過一個回測沒見過的日子——這正是「風險預算」而不是「報酬目標」該做的事。

One more connection worth making explicit, tying this back to how the market prices things: even a rule that survives every honesty check in this lesson — genuinely out-of-sample validated, not overfit, not timing luck — is not guaranteed to keep paying once it is well known. A validated historical pattern that becomes widely published and widely traded tends to attract capital chasing exactly the same signal, which can compress or erase the very edge the backtest correctly measured in the past. A clean backtest proves what happened in a sample of history. It says nothing about how many other people have since read the same report and are now trying to arrive earlier than you.

還有一個連結值得明講，接回市場怎麼定價：即使一條規則通過了這一課的每一項誠實檢查——真的樣本外驗證過、不是過度擬合、不是時機運氣——也不保證它廣為人知之後還會繼續有用。被廣泛發表、廣泛交易的歷史型態，會吸引資金追著同一個訊號湧入，而這股資金本身就可能壓掉回測正確量到的那個優勢。乾淨的回測證明的是某一段歷史裡發生過什麼。它沒告訴你，有多少人已經讀過同一份報告，正在想辦法比你更早進場。

This is not a hypothetical risk. Long-Term Capital Management's own early results are a documented instance of it happening to the very fund discussed earlier in this lesson: its reported profit rate fell from roughly 40% in 1996 to roughly 17% in 1997, a decline the fund's own principals attributed at the time to other firms observing and copying its convergence trades, which narrowed the price gaps those trades depended on before the 1998 crisis ever arrived. The rule's underlying logic had not changed and its historical validation had not changed; what changed was that the edge itself was a function of how few other people were trading on the same information, and that number stopped being small once the strategy's success became visible to competitors.

這不是假設性的風險。長期資本管理公司（LTCM）自己早期的績效就是一個紀錄在案的例子，而且發生在這一課前面談過的同一支基金身上：它公布的獲利率從 1996 年大約 40% 掉到 1997 年大約 17%。基金自己的合夥人當時把這個下滑歸因於同業觀察並複製了它的收斂型交易，在 1998 年危機到來之前就先把這些交易靠的價差壓窄了。規則的邏輯沒變，歷史驗證也沒變。變的是這個優勢本來就取決於「有多少人也在用同一份資訊交易」，而這個人數在策略的成功被同業看見之後，就不再是小數字了。

📜War Story戰爭故事

Start with two dates anyone can look up. On August 31, 2020, Apple executed a four-for-one stock split: the closing price the previous Friday was about $499, and the first close after the split was about $129. On June 10, 2024, NVIDIA executed a ten-for-one split: the previous close was about $1,209, and the first post-split close was about $122. In both cases every shareholder held exactly the same fraction of the same company before and after, and not one dollar of wealth moved. In both cases a raw, unadjusted price series records a single-day loss of roughly 74% and roughly 90% respectively. Splits of that size are not rare events buried in the 1990s — they land on the largest, most widely tracked companies in the market, and they land without warning to any calculation that was not designed for them.

先從兩個任何人都查得到的日期講起。2020 年 8 月 31 日，蘋果執行一股拆四股：前一個星期五收在大約 499 美元，分割後第一個收盤價大約 129 美元。2024 年 6 月 10 日，輝達執行 1 股拆 10 股：前一個收盤價大約 1,209 美元，分割後大約 122 美元。這兩次，每位股東分割前後持有的都是同一家公司、完全相同的比例，沒有一塊錢的財富移動過。而這兩次，未還原的原始股價序列記錄下來的，分別是單日大約 −74% 與 −90% 的虧損。這種規模的分割不是埋在 1990 年代的稀有事件。它發生在市場上最大、被追蹤得最密的公司身上，而且對任何沒有為它設計過的計算式來說，都是毫無預警地降臨。

A publicly posted long-term track-record page walked into exactly this. Its design was reasonable on the surface: for each published call, freeze the actual traded price on the day the call went out as a permanent starting point, then compare it against the latest close pulled from a live data feed. The subtle flaw was that the two prices came from different worlds — the live feed returned adjusted prices, while the frozen starting point was a raw, as-traded number. For years the two agreed perfectly, because none of the tracked names had split since publication, so nothing ever surfaced. Then one of them did. From that day forward the frozen base sat on the pre-split scale while every incoming price arrived on the post-split scale, and that instrument's displayed return collapsed by roughly the split factor: a position that was modestly ahead was published to public readers as having lost most of its value. Nothing on the page appeared broken. Every other row was still right, every arithmetic operation was still correct, and there was no error state for a page to display when the inputs are individually valid and merely incompatible with each other. It surfaced only because the displayed loss was too large to be plausible for that name over that window — a human sanity check, not a system alarm.

一個公開發布的長期戰績追蹤頁面，正好一頭撞進這件事。設計表面上很合理：每一則公開發布的判斷，都把發布當天的實際成交價凍結成永久的起算點，再拿去跟即時資料源抓回來的最新收盤價比。隱蔽的缺陷在於，這兩個價格來自兩個不同的世界。即時資料源回傳的是還原後的價格，凍結的起算點卻是未還原的原始成交價。好幾年來兩者完全對得上，因為被追蹤的標的在發布之後都沒分割過，所以什麼也沒現形。直到其中一檔分割了。從那天起，凍結的基期停在分割前的尺度，進來的價格都在分割後的尺度，那檔標的顯示出來的報酬率就依分割比例整個塌下去：一個原本小幅領先的部位，對著公開讀者顯示成幾乎虧光。頁面上看不出任何東西壞掉。其他每一列都還是對的，算術也都還是對的。當所有輸入單獨看都合法、只是彼此尺度不相容，頁面根本沒有一個錯誤狀態可以顯示。它最後現形，只因為那個虧損幅度對那檔標的在那段期間來說大到不合理——靠的是人的常識檢查，不是系統警報。

The fix was not to patch that one number, which would have left the same trap armed for the next split. It was to change the rule that defines the base: take a split-and-dividend-adjusted close, as of the publication date or the most recent trading day before it, as the fixed starting point for every calculation, and recompute that base from adjusted data on every refresh, so base and current price always live on the same scale no matter what corporate actions happen afterward. Two general points survive this specific case. First, mixing an adjusted series with an unadjusted one is more dangerous than using unadjusted prices everywhere, because a uniform error is at least uniform, while a mixed one appears on some rows and some dates only — which is precisely the pattern that survives review, since most rows keep looking right. Second, this failure mode is silent by construction: it does not throw an exception, it produces a plausible-looking number. That is why the defense has to be a rule about how the base is defined, written once into the calculation, rather than vigilance applied every time someone reads the page.

修正的做法不是去補那一個數字，那樣只會讓同一個陷阱繼續為下一次分割上膛。真正的修正是改掉「基期怎麼定義」這條規則：一律取還原分割與配息後的收盤價，時點取發布日當天或之前最近一個交易日，當成固定起算點，而且每次更新資料都用還原後的資料重算一次基期。這樣不管之後發生什麼公司行動，基期與當前價格永遠停在同一個尺度上。這個案例能帶走兩個一般性的重點。第一，把還原序列跟未還原序列混著用，比全部都用未還原更危險。一致的錯誤至少是一致的；混用的錯誤只出現在某些列、某些日期，而這正是最容易通過複查的型態，因為大部分列看起來都對。第二，這種失效在設計上就是無聲的。它不會拋出例外，它產生的是一個看起來很合理的數字。所以防線必須是一條「基期怎麼定義」的規則，一次寫進計算式裡，而不是靠每次有人看頁面時的警覺。

One more general diagnostic worth naming: the size of a suspicious single-day move is itself a clue, because major exchanges and financial data providers publish upcoming stock-split calendars well in advance of the effective date. A reported single-day loss that lands close to 50%, 75%, or 90% — the signature of a 2-for-1, 4-for-1, or 10-for-1 split — is far more likely to be an unadjusted-price artifact than a genuine crash, and checking that day's date against a public split calendar takes less time than investigating the number as if it were real.

還有一條判斷線索值得點名：可疑單日變動的幅度本身就是線索。主要交易所與財經資料供應商，都會在生效日之前就公開發布即將發生的股票分割日曆。一個幅度接近 50%、75%、或 90% 的單日虧損——分別是一拆二、一拆四、一拆十的特徵——比較可能是未還原股價造成的假象，不太可能是真的崩盤。拿那個日期去對照公開的分割日曆，花的時間比把這個數字當真去調查還短。

⚠

Pitfall: crediting an execution-layer tweak with a performance improvement it did not earn. When a change is made for one clearly stated reason — usually reducing trading frequency or cost — and a risk-adjusted return number also happens to improve, the default assumption should be that the improvement is timing luck until a dedicated audit says otherwise, not that a second unrelated benefit was discovered for free.

陷阱：把一個執行層的微調，記成它沒有真正賺到的績效改善。當一個改動是為了一個講得很清楚的理由而做——通常是降低交易頻率或成本——而風險調整後的報酬數字也剛好變好了，預設假設應該是：在專門的審計證明不是之前，這個改善就當成時機運氣，不是白撿到的第二個好處。

⚠

Pitfall: trusting a comparison table because every number in it checks out. A table can be arithmetically flawless in every cell and still mislead, because the error lives in what its layout silently implies about which variables were held constant. Before trusting any two-column comparison, confirm from the footnotes — not the headline — that only the stated variable actually differs.

陷阱：只因為每個數字都對得起來，就信任一張對照表。一張表格可以每一格的算術都完美無誤，卻依然誤導人，因為問題出在排版悄悄暗示了哪些變數被固定不變。在信任任何兩欄對照表之前，先從註腳（不是頭條）確認真的只有標明的那個變數不一樣。

⚠

Pitfall: mistaking “it has been live for a while without a bad surprise” for out-of-sample validation. A short live-trading window is not the same thing as a held-out historical period that was deliberately excluded from the rule's design process. A rule can go months without an incident simply because the market has not yet presented the condition it is weak against — the same gap this lesson opened with.

陷阱：把「已經上線一段時間沒出過事」誤當成樣本外驗證。一段短暫的實盤交易窗口，跟一段刻意從規則設計過程中排除出去的歷史測試期，不是同一件事。一條規則可以好幾個月都相安無事，單純只是因為市場還沒出現它撐不住的那種條件——正是這一課開頭那個落差。

The portfolio structure lesson (26 · From One Stock to a Portfolio) and the asset allocation lesson (27 · Asset Allocation: What the Evidence Says) both hand you numbers — expected maximum drawdown, an exposure cap, a leverage multiple — that only mean what they claim to mean if the backtest producing them survived every check in this lesson. This lesson is the honesty layer underneath both of those: it is what tells you whether the −20%-level drawdown or the 150% exposure cap you learned to reason about there was measured honestly, or measured off a survivorship-biased, overfit, timing-lucky sample that happens to look better than reality. The discipline of pre-registration and kill conditions covered here is also a direct application of the process-design lesson on bias (25 · Biases and Process Design): freezing a rule before seeing its results, and committing in advance to the evidence that would overturn it, is exactly the kind of structural defense against self-serving hindsight that lesson argued for, applied specifically to a backtest.

組合結構那一課（26・從一檔股票到一個組合）與資產配置那一課（27・資產配置：證據怎麼說），都交給你一些數字——預期最大回撤、曝險上限、槓桿倍數。但這些數字要名副其實，前提是產生它們的那份回測通過了這一課的每一項檢查。這一課是那兩課底下的誠實層：你在那兩課學會推理的回撤幅度與曝險上限，到底是誠實量出來的，還是量在一份帶著存活者偏誤、過度擬合、又剛好踩中時機運氣的樣本上。這一課講的預註冊與撤回條件，也是偏誤與流程設計那一課（25・偏誤與流程設計）的直接應用：在看到結果之前先凍結規則，並事先承諾什麼證據出現就要推翻它——那一課主張的結構性防線，在這裡具體套用在一份回測上。

Every earlier lesson in this framework leaned on a number at some point — an ROIC decomposition, a five-year demand multiple, a valuation multiple, a backtested exposure engine. This lesson is the last checkpoint on whether any of those numbers, when they come wrapped in a historical test rather than a single company's current filing, deserve the confidence a reader is about to place in them.

這套框架前面的每一課，多少都靠著某個數字撐著——ROIC 分解、五年需求倍數、估值倍數、回測出來的曝險引擎。當這些數字不是來自一家公司當下的財報，而是包在一份歷史測試裡時，它值不值得你接下來要放進去的信任——這一課就是那道最後的檢查點。

The framework has now walked through five parts — what a company is worth, what makes a business good, how the market prices it, how to judge and act, and how to size all of it into a portfolio. What remains is pulling every one of those pieces into a single page you can actually use the next time a specific ticker is in front of you.

這套框架至今已經走過五個部分——一家公司值多少、什麼讓生意變好、市場怎麼定價、怎麼判斷與行動、以及怎麼把這一切換算成一個組合的部位大小。剩下的，是把每一塊拼圖收進單獨一頁，讓你下一次真的有一檔股票擺在眼前時能直接拿來用。
