In February 2021, the ARK Innovation ETF (ticker ARKK) — one of the highest-profile actively managed funds of its era — reached an all-time high near $157 a share, having risen roughly seven-fold since its late-2014 launch near $20. The fund held dozens of positions, and its manager frequently pointed to that count, and to the fact the names were spread across categories as different as electric vehicles, telehealth, video communications, cryptocurrency infrastructure, robotic process automation, and e-commerce software, as evidence of genuine diversification. By the end of 2022, the fund had fallen by roughly 80% from that peak.

二〇二一年二月，方舟創新 ETF（代號 ARKK）——那個時代最引人矚目的主動管理基金之一——股價衝上每股約一百五十七美元的歷史新高，自二〇一四年底以約二十美元成立以來，大約漲了七倍。這檔基金持有數十檔部位，經理人也經常拿「檔數多」以及「這些股票分散在電動車、遠距醫療、視訊通訊、加密貨幣基礎建設、機器人流程自動化、電商軟體等看起來完全不同的類別」，當成真正做到分散的證據。到了二〇二二年底，這檔基金從高點跌了大約八成。

The sector labels were real. So was the collapse, and it did not arrive one name at a time. Nearly every one of the fund's largest positions fell by 60%, 70%, sometimes more than 90%, in the same twelve-to-eighteen-month stretch, for what turned out to be the same reason: each was priced as a long-duration growth story — a business whose real profits, if they arrived at all, sat many years out — and every one of those prices leaned on the same assumption, that interest rates would stay low. When rates rose sharply through 2022, the same discount-rate math hit every single holding at once, regardless of whether the underlying business made cars, ran diagnostics labs, or processed payments. Eight categories on a label sheet had turned out to be one bet wearing eight different costumes.

產業標籤是真的，崩跌也是真的，而且不是一檔一檔慢慢跌的。這檔基金幾乎每一個最大部位，都在同一段十二到十八個月的區間裡跌了六成、七成，有些甚至超過九成，理由竟然幾乎一模一樣：每一檔都被市場當成一個長天期成長故事來定價——一門真正的利潤（如果真的會出現的話）要等很多年後才會兌現的生意——而所有這些定價，都靠著同一個假設撐著：利率會一直維持低檔。當利率在二〇二二年急升，同一套折現率算式同時打到每一個持股身上，不管這家公司做的是汽車、診斷檢測、還是支付處理。標籤紙上的八個類別，到頭來是同一個賭注穿了八套不同的戲服。

This is the mistake this lesson is built to prevent, and it sits one level above everything covered so far. Every earlier lesson in this framework helped you judge one stock at a time — the industry it sits in, the moat protecting its returns, what its price already assumes. None of that tells you what happens when you put twenty of those individual judgments into the same account together. A portfolio is not just a list of individually good decisions; it is its own object, with its own structure, its own concentration, and its own drawdown — and getting that structure wrong can undo good individual judgment just as completely as getting any single stock wrong.

這正是這一課要防止的錯誤，而且它站在比前面任何一課都高一層的位置上。這套框架前面每一課，教你的都是怎麼一檔一檔判斷一支股票——它站在哪個產業裡、護城河能不能保住報酬、價格已經假設了什麼。這些都沒有告訴你，把二十個這樣的個別判斷同時放進同一個帳戶裡，會發生什麼事。一個組合不只是一串各自獨立、個別正確的決定的清單；它本身就是一個獨立的物件，有自己的結構、自己的集中度、自己的回撤——而把這個結構搞錯，能像看錯任何一檔股票一樣，徹底抵銷掉原本正確的個別判斷。

Two Sleeves: The Engine and the Alpha

兩塊拼圖：引擎與超額報酬

A mature stock portfolio is not one undifferentiated pool of names — it splits into two pieces that answer two different questions, using two different methods. The index sleeveThe rule-driven portion of stock exposure, built from broad or sector index funds, that decides "in the market or not, how much" without relying on daily human judgment.股票曝險裡由規則驅動的那一塊，用大盤或產業型指數基金構成，負責決定「現在該不該在場、壓多重」，不靠每天的人為判斷。 answers the question "should I be in the market at all right now, and how much." It is built from the most liquid, broadest instruments available — typically a small number of index-tracking or sector-tracking funds — and its behavior is fully rule-based: a mechanical signal, not a daily judgment call, decides whether it is on or off and how large it is (the next lesson opens that mechanism up in full). The stock sleeveThe judgment-driven portion of a portfolio where individual company research is actually deployed as concentrated bets, sized smaller than the index sleeve precisely because it cannot be fully mechanized.組合裡由判斷驅動的那一塊，個股研究在這裡真正被部署成集中的賭注，規模刻意小於指數部，正是因為它沒辦法完全規則化。 answers a different question: "which specific business is worth concentrating capital in." It is where the judgment built across every earlier lesson in this framework — moat durability, growth quality, what the price already assumes — actually gets deployed, and because that judgment cannot be fully mechanized, this sleeve is smaller, and its risk is bounded by size rather than by a rule.

一個成熟的股票組合，不是一整鍋沒有分工的名字——它拆成兩塊，各自回答一個不同的問題，用的方法也不一樣。指數部The rule-driven portion of stock exposure, built from broad or sector index funds, that decides "in the market or not, how much" without relying on daily human judgment.股票曝險裡由規則驅動的那一塊，用大盤或產業型指數基金構成，負責決定「現在該不該在場、壓多重」，不靠每天的人為判斷。回答的問題是「現在到底該不該在場、該壓多重」。它用市場上流動性最高、最廣泛的工具構成——通常是少數幾檔追蹤大盤或產業指數的基金——而且行為完全規則化：決定要不要在場、壓多重的是一個機械式訊號，不是每天的人為判斷（下一課會把這個機制完整打開來看）。個股部The judgment-driven portion of a portfolio where individual company research is actually deployed as concentrated bets, sized smaller than the index sleeve precisely because it cannot be fully mechanized.組合裡由判斷驅動的那一塊，個股研究在這裡真正被部署成集中的賭注，規模刻意小於指數部，正是因為它沒辦法完全規則化。回答的是另一個問題：「這個特定的生意，值不值得集中資金押注」。這套框架前面每一課建立起來的判斷——護城河能不能撐住、成長品質好不好、價格已經假設了什麼——真正被部署的地方就是這裡；也正因為這種判斷沒辦法完全規則化，這一塊的規模要更小，它的風險用「配置的比例」框住，不是用「一條規則」框住。

A typical split puts roughly 70% of stock exposure in the index sleeve and 30% in the stock sleeve — two ETFs sharing the first half roughly equally, and five to ten individual names sharing the second. Cash sits outside both, at roughly 8-10% of total assets, and this separation matters more than it looks: cash level is a decision made at the whole-portfolio level, about how much capital you want exposed to equities at all, while sleeve exposure is a decision about how that already-committed equity capital is currently positioned. Reading "the index sleeve is only 85% invested right now" as "there is idle cash sitting around to deploy" collapses two different decisions into one, and it is exactly the kind of error that leads someone to quietly stack a second layer of risk on top of a rule-driven system that was already doing its job of pulling back.

一個典型的分配，是把股票曝險裡大約 70% 放在指數部、30% 放在個股部——前面那一半通常由兩檔互補的 ETF 各半組成，後面那一半由五到十檔個股分食。現金另外獨立於這兩塊之外，大約占總資產的 8～10%，這個「另計」比看起來更重要：現金水位是在整個組合層級做的決定，決定的是你到底想讓多少資金曝險在股市裡；而曝險比例則是決定「已經投入股市的那筆資金，現在該擺在什麼位置」。把「指數部現在只有 85% 曝險」讀成「有一筆閒置現金可以動用」，等於把兩個不同層級的決定混成了一個，而這正是那種會讓人不知不覺在一套已經自動降低曝險的規則系統上，再疊一層風險的錯誤。

Why does the rule-driven sleeve get the larger share, when the stock sleeve is where all the interesting research happens? Three mechanisms, none of them about which sleeve is "better." First, the two sleeves fail in different ways. If the index sleeve's rule is wrong, the damage is a mistimed exposure rhythm — you were 60% invested when 100% would have been right — and the position is still in a broad basket that recovers when the market does. If a stock-sleeve judgment is wrong, the loss can be permanent: a business whose moat actually broke does not mean-revert just because the index did. Bounded, recoverable errors can carry more weight than unbounded, permanent ones. Second, research capacity is a hard constraint. Genuinely knowing a business — its supply side, its moat trend, what its price already assumes — takes weeks per name and has to be maintained, not just done once. Handing the majority of exposure to a rule frees that scarce capacity for the minority where it can actually produce an edge. Third, the honest base rate: across long horizons, most active stock selection has underperformed a broad index after costs. That does not mean an edge is impossible, but it does set the burden of proof — the engine is the default, and the stock sleeve has to earn its 30% by producing something the engine cannot.

為什麼比較大的那一份要給規則驅動的那一塊，明明所有有趣的研究都發生在個股部？有三個機制，而且沒有一個是在說哪一塊「比較好」。第一，兩塊出錯的方式不一樣。指數部的規則錯了，代價是曝險節奏抓錯——該滿載的時候只有六成——但部位還在一籃子廣泛的標的裡，大盤回來它就回來。個股部的判斷錯了，虧損可能是永久的：一門護城河真的破掉的生意，不會因為指數漲回去就跟著均值回歸。能自我修復、損失有上限的錯誤，可以配比較重的權重；不可逆的錯誤不行。第二，研究產能是一個硬性限制。真正搞懂一門生意——它的供給面、護城河趨勢、價格已經假設了什麼——每一檔要花上幾週，而且要持續維護，不是做完一次就結案。把多數曝險交給規則，才能把這份稀缺的產能，留給真正可能做出超額的那一小塊。第三，一個誠實的基準率：拉長期間來看，多數主動選股在扣掉成本後，表現不如一檔廣泛的指數。這不代表超額報酬不可能，但它決定了舉證責任落在誰身上——引擎是預設值，個股部那 30% 要靠交出引擎交不出來的東西，才賺得到自己的位置。

One sentence carries the whole structure: a portfolio is a set of rules, not a set of names. If the only thing written down about a portfolio is which tickers it holds, then nothing has been decided in advance — every future question (how much, when to add, when to sell, what would make this wrong) will be answered in the moment, under whatever mood the market is producing that week. Rules written before the pressure arrives are the only part of a portfolio that behaves consistently, which is why the rest of this lesson is about roles, ceilings, and triggers rather than about which companies to own.

有一句話可以扛起整個結構：一個組合是一組規則，不是一串名字。如果一個組合被寫下來的東西只有「持有哪些股票代號」，那等於什麼都還沒事先決定——未來每一個問題（壓多重、什麼時候加、什麼時候賣、什麼情況代表這個判斷是錯的）都會在當下才回答，而回答的時候，心情是那一週市場給你的心情。在壓力來臨之前先寫好的規則，是一個組合裡唯一會穩定運作的部分——這也是為什麼這一課接下來談的是角色、上限與觸發條件，而不是該持有哪幾家公司。

The boundary worth naming here: this two-sleeve split only does its job if the index sleeve genuinely behaves like an engine — a disciplined, rule-driven answer to "in or out, how much" — rather than simply being two more concentrated bets wearing an index-fund costume. A common false signal is two ETFs that are technically diversified funds by construction, but that overlap so heavily in their largest holdings (both dominated by the same handful of mega-cap technology names, for instance) that they move together as one position rather than as an independent engine. The way to check this from public data is simple and often skipped: look up the top-ten holdings of each fund in the index sleeve and see how much overlap exists before assuming the label "index fund" has done the diversification work for you.

這裡值得點名一個邊界：這套兩塊拼圖的分法，只有在指數部真的表現得像一具引擎——對「在不在場、壓多重」給出有紀律、規則驅動的答案——的時候才有用，而不是變成又兩筆穿著指數基金外衣的集中賭注。一個常見的假訊號是：兩檔 ETF 就基金結構而言確實是分散的，但它們最大持股重疊得非常嚴重（例如都被同一批少數幾檔超大型科技股主導），結果兩者其實一起動，像一個部位，而不是一具獨立的引擎。從公開資料檢查這件事的方法很簡單，卻常常被跳過：查一下指數部裡每一檔基金的前十大持股，看看重疊程度有多高，不要假設「指數基金」這個標籤已經替你把分散的工作做完了。

Takeaway一句話收斂

The index sleeve answers "in or out, how much" by rule; the stock sleeve answers "which business, how much conviction" by judgment — and confusing sub-100% exposure with spare cash is a common way to accidentally stack risk.指數部靠規則回答「在不在場、壓多重」；個股部靠判斷回答「哪門生意、有多少信心」——把低於滿載的曝險誤讀成閒置現金，是最常見、不知不覺疊加風險的方式之一。

From Role to Weight: Core, Satellite, Tracking

從角色到權重：核心、衛星、追蹤

Inside the stock sleeve, positions are not sized by how excited you feel about them today. Sizing is downstream of a role, and the role is downstream of a verdict you already reached in an earlier stage of this framework — how durable the moat is, how long the growth runway looks, whether a position is a timed cycle trade rather than a long hold. Deciding "I want this at 8%" first, and then reaching backward for a role label that would justify that number, reverses the correct order and is one of the most common ways conviction quietly turns into concentration risk that was never actually earned.

在個股部裡面，部位大小不是由你今天對它有多興奮來決定的。權重是角色的下游，而角色又是你在這套框架前面階段就已經做出的判斷的下游——護城河有多持久、成長跑道還有多長、這是一筆綁時效的循環交易還是一個長抱部位。先決定「我想要壓 8%」，再回頭找一個能替這個數字撐腰的角色標籤，順序整個反過來了，也是信心最常在不知不覺間變成一種其實從沒被真正賺到的集中度風險的方式之一。

Notice what role assignment is not doing: it is not ranking businesses from best to worst. A satellite-structural name can be a wonderful business with a real chance of becoming a future core holding — it is capped not because it is worse, but because the durability check it needs to graduate has not been passed yet, and position size should reflect the confidence you have actually earned, not the confidence you feel. A false signal worth naming directly: an analyst who is highly confident in a not-yet-durable growth story sizing it at 8% "because conviction is high" is substituting feeling for evidence — conviction is necessary for any position at all, but it is not sufficient to unlock a core-sized weight; only the durability check is.

留意角色配置不是在做什麼：它不是在替生意排名分高下。一檔衛星結構型的股票，完全可以是一門很棒的生意，也很有機會將來升格成核心持股——它現在被設上限，不是因為它比較差，而是因為它要升格所需要的持久性檢驗還沒通過，部位大小應該反映你「真正賺到」的信心，不是你「感覺到」的信心。這裡有一個值得直接點名的假訊號：一個分析者對一個還沒通過持久性檢驗的成長故事非常有信心，就把它壓到 8%，理由是「這次信心特別高」——這是拿感覺去替代證據；信心是任何部位存在的必要條件，但它不足以解鎖核心等級的權重，只有持久性檢驗才能。

The cap on satellites is not a stylistic preference; it is arithmetic about what a total loss costs. A position that goes to zero costs exactly its weight, and the rest of the portfolio then has to make that back on a smaller base. A 3% position wiped out needs about 3.1% from everything else to get whole; a 12% position wiped out needs about 13.6%; a 25% position wiped out needs 33%. The cost of being wrong is convex — it accelerates faster than the weight itself — and satellite roles are, by definition, the positions where "wrong" is most likely, because the durability evidence that would make a permanent loss unlikely is exactly what has not been supplied yet. A cap converts an unbounded judgment risk into a known, survivable number decided in advance, while the position still has full upside: a 3% position that becomes a five-bagger adds 12 percentage points to the portfolio, which is more than enough to matter. Capping the downside does not cap the upside — that asymmetry is the whole reason the rule is cheap to follow.

衛星部位的上限不是風格偏好，是關於「歸零要付多少代價」的算術。一個部位歸零，付出的成本剛好等於它的權重，而剩下的組合要用一個更小的基礎把它賺回來。一個 3% 的部位歸零，其餘部位要漲約 3.1% 才回得到原點；12% 的部位歸零，要漲約 13.6%；25% 的部位歸零，要漲 33%。犯錯的代價是凸的——上升速度比權重本身還快——而衛星角色依定義正是最可能犯錯的那一類部位，因為能讓「永久性虧損」變得不太可能的持久性證據，正好就是它還沒交出來的東西。上限的作用，是把一個沒有邊界的判斷風險，換成一個事先決定好、活得下來的數字，而部位的上檔完全沒有被限制：一個 3% 的部位漲五倍，替組合貢獻 12 個百分點，這個量級絕對有感。壓住下檔並不會壓住上檔——正是這個不對稱，讓這條規則便宜到沒有理由不遵守。

How to check this from your own records, or from a fund's public filings: line up each position's disclosed or logged weight against the role you claim it holds. Any "satellite" position sitting above the cap, or any position whose weight has drifted upward purely because it appreciated rather than because its role changed, is a place where the discipline has already quietly broken, whether or not a single trade was ever placed.

怎麼從自己的紀錄、或一檔基金的公開持股資料裡檢查這件事：把每個部位揭露或記錄下來的權重，跟你宣稱它擁有的角色對齊看一次。任何一檔「衛星」部位的權重超過上限，或者任何一檔部位的權重是純粹因為漲多了、而不是因為角色本身改變而往上漂移，都是紀律早就已經悄悄破功的地方，不管有沒有真的下過一筆交易。

Takeaway一句話收斂

Role comes first, weight follows — sizing a position by how confident you feel today, rather than by the durability check it has actually passed, is how conviction quietly becomes unearned concentration.角色在前、權重在後——用今天感覺到的信心去決定部位大小，而不是用它真正通過的持久性檢驗，是信心悄悄變成沒賺到的集中度風險的方式。

Counting Bets, Not Names: Correlation Clustering

算賭注，不算檔數：相關性群聚

The ARKK case at the top of this lesson demonstrated the mechanism; here is the tool that catches it before the drawdown does. Real diversification counts independent sources of risk, not the number of tickers on a statement. If three positions are all leveraged to the same underlying driver — a single commodity price, a single macro theme, a single supply-chain node — they behave, in a drawdown, as close to one position as three, regardless of how different their sector labels, business models, or ticker symbols look on paper.

這一課開頭的 ARKK 案例示範了機制；這裡要建立的，是能在回撤發生之前先抓到這件事的工具。真正的分散度算的是獨立的風險來源，不是對帳單上有幾檔股票代號。如果三個部位全部槓桿在同一個底層驅動因子上——同一個商品價格、同一個總經主題、同一個供應鏈節點——不管它們的產業標籤、商業模式、股票代號在紙面上看起來多麼不一樣，一旦回撤發生，它們的表現會近似於一個部位，不是三個。

A simple formula makes this concrete. Score each position's number of independent effective bets as the reciprocal of how many positions share its cluster, then sum across the whole sleeve: effective independent bets = Σ(1 / cluster size) for every position. Five positions spread across five genuinely unrelated themes score a full 5 effective bets. Five positions where three of them are all leveraged to the same theme score 2 (the two independent ones) plus 3×(1/3) — one more — for a total of 3, even though the statement still shows "5 positions." The headline count barely moved; the real diversification dropped by two-fifths.

一個簡單的公式可以把這件事算得很具體。把每個部位的獨立有效賭注，算成「一除以它所屬群聚裡的檔數」，再把整個個股部加總：有效獨立賭注數 = Σ(1 / 該部位所屬群聚的檔數)，對每個部位都算一次。五個部位分散在五個真正不相干的主題裡，算出來就是滿分 5 個有效賭注。五個部位裡有三個共用同一個主題，算出來是兩個獨立的部位 + 3×(1/3)——再加一個——合計 3 個，即使對帳單上還是寫著「5 檔」。表面上的檔數幾乎沒動，真正的分散度卻掉了五分之二。

How to see this from public data without any special tooling: instead of grouping holdings by the sector label a data provider assigns, group them by the answer to one question — "if this specific narrative turns out to be wrong, or this specific price stops rising, which other positions in my sleeve go down for the same reason, on the same day." That question routes past sector classification straight to the actual shared risk factor, which is exactly what standard sector labels are built to obscure rather than reveal, since they classify by product category, not by what is economically driving the stock.

不需要任何特殊工具，從公開資料看出這件事的方法：不要照資料商指派的產業標籤去分組持股，改成用一個問題去分組——「如果這個特定的敘事被證明是錯的，或者這個特定的價格不再往上走，我個股部裡還有哪些部位，會在同一天、為了同一個理由一起跌。」這個問題直接繞過產業分類，指向真正共用的風險因子——而這正是標準產業標籤本來就更擅長掩蓋、而不是揭露的東西，因為它們是按產品類別分類，不是按到底是什麼在經濟上驅動股價分類。

The boundary here deserves an honest statement: deciding what counts as "the same cluster" is itself a judgment call, not something a spreadsheet formula can compute on its own from ticker symbols and sector codes. A memory-chip maker and a data-center power-equipment supplier look nothing alike on a sector sheet, yet both can be leveraged to the same AI-buildout capital cycle — a link only visible if you have already done the industry-level work from earlier lessons. This means clustering has to be revisited whenever a position's thesis or macro sensitivity changes, not set once at purchase and forgotten, because the cluster map itself has a shelf life.

這裡有一個邊界值得誠實講出來：判斷什麼算「同一個群聚」，本身就是一個判斷，不是光靠股票代號跟產業代碼，一個試算表公式就能自己算出來的東西。一家記憶體晶片廠跟一家資料中心電源設備供應商，在產業分類表上看起來完全不像，但兩者都可能槓桿在同一個 AI 基礎建設資本週期上——這個連結，只有在你已經做過前面幾課的產業層級功課之後才看得見。這也代表群聚判斷必須在部位的論點或總經敏感度改變時重新檢視，不能買進那一刻設定一次就束之高閣，因為這張群聚地圖本身是有保質期的。

Takeaway一句話收斂

Count effective independent bets, not tickers — Σ(1/cluster size) across the sleeve — and group by shared driver, not by sector label, since the label is built to classify products, not risk.算有效獨立賭注數，不算股票代號——整個個股部算一次 Σ(1/群聚檔數)——而且要按共用的驅動因子分組，不是按產業標籤分組，因為標籤本來就是拿來分類產品，不是分類風險。

How Concentrated Is Too Concentrated

多集中算太集中

Diversification does not raise expected return. What it does is shrink the spread of possible outcomes around that return, and it does so only in proportion to how independent the bets actually are — which is why the clustering step comes first and the counting comes second. The mechanism is worth stating plainly because it explains where the benefit stops: if idiosyncratic, name-specific risks are genuinely independent, their contribution to portfolio variance falls roughly in proportion to one divided by the number of bets. Going from 1 independent bet to 5 removes about 80% of that name-specific variance. Going from 5 to 10 removes half of what is left. Going from 10 to 20 halves an already small remainder. The first few positions do almost all the work; by the twentieth, the marginal position is buying a rounding error — while still costing a full share of your attention.

分散並不會提高期望報酬。它做的事是縮小結果分布在那個報酬周圍的散布範圍，而且縮小的幅度，只跟這些賭注實際上有多獨立成正比——這也是為什麼群聚檢查要排在前面、數數量排在後面。這個機制值得講白，因為它說明了好處在哪裡就停住了：如果個股特有的風險真的彼此獨立，它們對組合變異數的貢獻，大約會隨著「一除以賭注數」下降。從 1 個獨立賭注增加到 5 個，可以消掉大約八成的個股特有變異。從 5 個增加到 10 個，消掉剩下的一半。從 10 個增加到 20 個，是把一個已經很小的餘數再對半砍。最前面那幾個部位幾乎做完了所有的工作；到了第二十個，邊際那一檔買到的是一個四捨五入的誤差——但它照樣要吃掉你一整份的注意力。

That attention is the real binding constraint, and it is not a mathematical one. Every position in the stock sleeve needs a live thesis, a written falsifier, and someone actually reading its quarterly results against both. Past roughly ten names, most individual investors are not maintaining that for any of them — and the cost of breadth is not that returns get diluted, it is that a broken thesis stops being noticed. You find out from the price instead of from the evidence, which is the most expensive way to learn it, because by then the market has already repriced the thing you were supposed to catch first. The honest way to check your own ceiling is not a formula: name every position you hold and, for each, state out loud the one piece of evidence that would prove you wrong. Whatever number you can do that for is your real capacity, and it is usually smaller than the number you own.

那份注意力才是真正的限制條件，而且它不是數學上的。個股部裡的每一個部位，都需要一個活著的論點、一條寫下來的證偽條件，以及真的有人拿每一季的財報去對照這兩件事。超過大約十檔之後，多數散戶投資人對其中任何一檔都沒有在維護這些東西——而廣度的代價不是報酬被稀釋，是論點破功的時候沒人發現。你會從股價知道，而不是從證據知道，這是學到這件事最貴的方式，因為到那個時候，市場早就把你本來該先抓到的東西重新定價完了。檢查自己上限的誠實方法不是一條公式：把你持有的每一個部位一一點名，然後對每一檔，出聲說出那一項會證明你錯了的證據是什麼。你能做到這件事的檔數，就是你真正的產能，而它通常比你實際持有的檔數還小。

There is a genuine argument running the other way, and skipping it would make this lesson dishonest. Wirecard, a German payments company that had been added to the DAX 30 index in 2018, disclosed in June 2020 that roughly €1.9 billion of cash supposedly sitting in Asian trust accounts did not exist. The stock fell from about €104 to under €2 within days, and the company filed for insolvency on 25 June 2020. Analysts and funds who held it were not lazy — several had done extensive work — and a journalist had been publishing detailed allegations for years while the German regulator investigated the short sellers instead. The point is not that Wirecard was unknowable; it is that a single name can go to zero for a reason no amount of fundamental analysis reliably surfaces in advance, and the only defense against that specific risk is not owning too much of any one thing. That is the honest case for a floor on position count, and it is why "just hold your three best ideas" is a dangerous prescription even for a genuinely good analyst.

另一個方向也有一個真實存在的論證，跳過它會讓這一課變得不誠實。Wirecard 是一家德國支付公司，二〇一八年還被納入德國 DAX 30 指數，卻在二〇二〇年六月坦承，帳上宣稱存放在亞洲信託帳戶裡、約十九億歐元的現金根本不存在。股價在幾天之內從約一百零四歐元跌到兩歐元以下，公司在二〇二〇年六月二十五日聲請破產。持有它的分析師與基金並不是偷懶——好幾家做過非常紮實的功課——而且多年來一直有記者發表詳細的指控，德國監管機關那時卻反過來去調查放空者。重點不是 Wirecard 完全無法看穿，而是：一檔股票可以因為一個沒有任何基本面分析能可靠事先揭露的理由而歸零，對這個特定風險唯一的防線，就是不要在任何單一標的上壓太重。這才是「部位檔數要有下限」誠實的論證，也是為什麼「只抱你最好的三個想法」即使對一個真的很強的分析者來說，仍然是一句危險的處方。

Put the two sides together and the five-to-ten band in the stock sleeve is bracketed from below and above: below five, a single fraud, accounting restatement, or regulatory event can take a permanent bite out of the whole sleeve; above ten, monitoring quality decays and you are paying the time cost of active management for something increasingly index-shaped. And one more thing decides whether concentration is even worth attempting: concentration multiplies whatever edge you have, including a negative one. An edge means knowing something the current price does not already reflect — if you cannot say what the price is currently assuming and why that assumption is wrong, then a concentrated position is not conviction, it is size applied to an unknown sign.

把兩邊放在一起看，個股部五到十檔這個區間，上下兩端都有東西夾著：低於五檔，單一一次造假、財報重編或監管事件，就能在整塊個股部上啃掉一塊永久的損失；高於十檔，追蹤品質開始衰退，而你付出的是主動管理的時間成本，換回來的東西卻越來越像一檔指數。還有一件事決定集中到底值不值得嘗試：集中會把你的優勢放大，包括負的優勢。所謂優勢，是你知道某件目前價格還沒反映的事——如果你講不出價格現在假設了什麼、以及那個假設為什麼是錯的，那麼一個集中的部位就不是信念，只是把規模壓在一個正負號未知的東西上。

🏦War Story戰爭故事

In March 2023, three US regional banks — Silicon Valley Bank, Signature Bank, and First Republic Bank — failed within about seven weeks of each other, wiping out shareholders at each. On paper, an investor holding all three would have looked reasonably diversified: three separate management teams, three separate ticker symbols, three different regional customer bases (technology-sector deposits, New York real estate and crypto-adjacent clients, and West Coast private banking, respectively). Underneath the labels, all three shared the same structural exposure: long-dated bonds purchased when rates were near zero, funded largely by deposits that were both highly concentrated by customer type and, in large part, uninsured above the $250,000 government guarantee.

When the fastest rate-hiking cycle in decades pushed the market value of those long-dated bonds down sharply, and word of unrealized losses spread among exactly the kind of sophisticated, fast-moving depositors each bank had concentrated its funding around, all three faced the same mechanism — a rapid, correlated deposit run triggered by the same underlying duration mismatch — within the same seven-week window. An investor who held all three "for diversification" was not running three separate bank bets. Structurally, it was one interest-rate-and-deposit-flight bet, sized three times over.

二〇二三年三月，三家美國區域銀行——矽谷銀行、Signature Bank、第一共和銀行——在大約七週之內接連倒閉，各自的股東血本無歸。表面上看，一個同時持有這三家的投資人，看起來相當分散：三個不同的經營團隊、三個不同的股票代號、三種不同的地區客群（分別是科技業存款、紐約房地產與加密貨幣相關客戶、以及西岸私人銀行業務）。但在標籤底下，這三家共用著同一個結構性曝險：都在利率接近零的時候買進了大量長天期債券，資金來源主要是高度集中於特定客群、而且很大一部分超過二十五萬美元政府保障上限的無擔保存款。

當史上數一數二快速的升息循環，把這些長天期債券的市值大幅壓低，未實現虧損的消息在每一家銀行都高度集中的那種消息靈通、動作又快的存戶之間傳開時，三家銀行在同一個七週的窗口裡，遇到了同一套機制——同一個底層存續期不匹配觸發的快速、連動擠兌。一個「為了分散」而同時持有這三家的投資人，其實運行的不是三個獨立的銀行賭注。結構上，這是同一個利率與存款外逃賭注，只是壓了三倍的量。

Think first: why can grouping holdings by standard sector labels actively hide a correlation cluster, rather than simply failing to reveal one?先想一下：為什麼用標準產業標籤分組持股，會主動掩蓋一個相關性群聚，而不只是沒能揭露出來而已？

Because a sector label classifies by what a company sells, not by what actually moves its stock price. The three regional banks above were correctly labeled "banks" and looked like a clean, single-sector allocation — the label was accurate and still misleading, because the real driver (a duration mismatch triggering a correlated deposit run) was invisible at the sector level and only became visible one layer down, in the balance-sheet structure each bank shared. A label that is technically correct can still route your attention away from the one question that matters: what specific event would make several of these positions fall for the same reason, on the same day.

What the Stock Sleeve Actually Costs: Drawdown and Sequence

個股部真正的代價：回撤與順序

A passive, buy-and-hold stock portfolio experiencing a drawdown of roughly 30% at some point is not an exception — it is the ordinary cost of being exposed to equities at all, and any portfolio structure built around the assumption that this will not happen is built on a false premise. The counterintuitive part is one level deeper: even a single, genuinely great, durable compounding business losing more than half its market value at some point along the way is also well within normal, not a sign the framework has failed. A moat that has not deteriorated does not guarantee a stock will not go through a brutal valuation compression — those are two different axes, one about the business's actual economics, the other about market sentiment and the interest-rate environment, and collapsing them into one leads to two opposite mistakes: panic-selling a business whose fundamentals never actually broke, or the mirror-image error of shrugging off a drawdown that arrives alongside real, deteriorating evidence about the business itself.

一個被動、買進持有的股票組合，在某個時間點經歷大約 30% 的回撤，不是例外——這是曝險在股市裡本來就要付的正常代價，任何建立在「這不會發生」這個假設上的組合結構，地基本身就是錯的。真正反直覺的地方還要再深一層：即使是一門真正很棒、持久的複利生意，在它的歷程裡某個時間點市值腰斬以上，同樣完全在正常範圍內，不代表這套框架失靈了。護城河沒有惡化，並不保證股價不會經歷一次很凶猛的估值壓縮——這是兩個不同的軸，一個關於生意本身真實的經濟狀況，一個關於市場情緒跟利率環境，把這兩件事混成一件事，會導致兩種相反的錯誤：對一門基本面根本沒有真的破功的生意恐慌性賣出，或者反過來，對一次跟真正惡化中的證據同時出現的回撤聳聳肩不當一回事。

Two well-documented real examples make the point concrete. Amazon's stock fell from a peak near $106 in December 1999 to roughly $6 by September 2001 — a decline of about 94%, on the split-adjusted basis in use before the 2022 twenty-for-one split — while the underlying business went on to become one of the greatest compounders of the following two decades. Netflix fell from roughly $300 in July 2011 to about $63 by late November 2011 — a decline of roughly 79% in four months, following a 60% price increase and a poorly received attempt to spin the DVD business off as "Qwikster" — and drifted to about $54 during 2012 before compounding for years afterward. In neither case did the drawdown itself tell you, in real time, whether the business was permanently broken or temporarily mispriced; that judgment came from the same moat and durability tools built earlier in this framework, not from the depth of the price move.

兩個有充分紀錄的真實案例，可以把這一點講得很具體。亞馬遜的股價，從一九九九年十二月的高點約一百零六美元，跌到二〇〇一年九月大約六美元——跌幅約 94%，這是以二〇二二年二十比一分割之前的還原基準計算——而這門生意接下來的二十年，卻成長為那個世代數一數二的複利巨獸。網飛的股價，從二〇一一年七月大約三百美元，跌到同年十一月底大約六十三美元——短短四個月跌了約 79%——導火線是一次幅度達六成的漲價，加上一次評價很差、想把 DVD 租借業務分割成「Qwikster」的嘗試——之後在二〇一二年一度再探到約五十四美元，然後又持續複利了好幾年。這兩個案例裡，回撤的深度本身，在當下都沒有告訴你這門生意到底是永久壞掉了、還是暫時被錯殺——真正做出這個判斷的，是這套框架前面建立起來的護城河與持久性工具，不是股價跌了多深這件事本身。

A public research team tracking two parallel strategy legs — each starting at the same nominal amount — once computed the combined portfolio's historical worst drawdown by adding the two dollar-value net-asset curves together directly, day by day, rather than first computing each leg's period return and then blending the two using the actual target weights. The arithmetic looked reasonable, since both legs had started at identical dollar amounts. It was wrong: once the two legs' performance diverged over time, direct dollar addition implicitly let whichever leg was worth more dollars on a given day silently dominate the combined number, rather than holding the intended fixed-weight blend constant.

The error was not cosmetic. It inflated the combined portfolio's worst historical drawdown to roughly negative 25.6% in the published figure. After the team switched to the correct method — computing each leg's return each period and blending by target weight — not only did the number change, but the identity of which year actually held the worst drawdown changed too. The lesson generalizes past this one mistake: portfolio-level net asset value must always be built from weighted returns, never from adding raw dollar curves, even when both legs happen to start at the same nominal figure, because "same starting dollars" says nothing about whether the two legs still deserve equal weight after time and divergent performance have passed.

一個公開的研究團隊，追蹤兩條並行的策略腿，兩者起始的名目金額相同，曾經用「把兩條淨值曲線的美元數字逐日直接相加」的方式，算出合成組合歷史上最深的回撤，而不是先各自算出每一腿的期間報酬率，再用真正的目標權重把兩者合成。這個算法乍看合理，因為兩腿一開始的美元金額確實相同。但它是錯的：一旦兩腿的績效隨時間分道揚鑣，直接把美元數字相加，會讓當天美元金額比較大的那一腿，悄悄主導合成後的數字，而不是維持原本設定好的固定權重組合不變。

這個錯誤不是表面上的小瑕疵。它把合成組合公布出來的歷史最深回撤，膨脹到大約負 25.6%。等團隊改用正確方法之後——每一期先算出每腿各自的報酬率，再用目標權重合成——不只數字本身變了，連「哪一年才是真正最深回撤的那一年」這個結論本身都跟著變了。這個教訓可以推廣到這個單一錯誤之外：組合層級的淨值，永遠應該從加權報酬率建構出來，絕不能靠直接相加兩條原始美元曲線，即使兩腿一開始的名目金額剛好相同也一樣，因為「起始美元相同」完全沒有回答一個問題：時間過去、績效分道揚鑣之後，這兩腿是不是仍然值得被賦予相同的權重。

Because direct dollar addition implicitly assumes the two legs are never rebalanced back to their intended weights — it lets whichever leg happens to be worth more dollars on a given day silently carry more weight in the combined number, drifting further from the target split with every day the two legs' performance diverges. A high ticker count with several positions sharing one macro driver — as in the ARKK case — is not diversification; it is one concentrated bet, counted many times. Check the effective independent-bets number, not the raw count, before calling any portfolio "spread out."

當「持有幾檔」（18 檔、22 檔）這個數字本身，就被當成分散投資的完整論證時，這通常是上面那個相關性群聚步驟根本沒有真的做過的訊號。持有很多檔，但其中好幾檔共用同一個總經驅動因子——就像 ARKK 案例——這不是分散，這是同一個集中賭注，被重複算了很多次。判斷任何一個組合「夠不夠分散」之前，該看的是有效獨立賭注數，不是原始檔數。

⚠

Cash sitting in the account because the index sleeve's own trend-and-volatility engine has scaled its exposure down is not the same thing as spare cash available to fund new stock-sleeve purchases. That cash is doing a job — it is the deliberate output of a rule the engine follows for a reason (the next lesson covers exactly how that number gets calculated) — and spending it on a stock-sleeve idea quietly overrides the engine's own risk call without ever making that decision explicitly. If the two sleeves are meant to be sized independently, a change in one sleeve's cash level should never be read as a green light to add exposure through the other.

因為指數部自己的趨勢加波動率引擎把曝險降下來、而留在帳戶裡的現金，跟「可以拿去買個股部新標的」的閒置現金，不是同一件事。那筆現金正在做一份工作——它是引擎依照某個理由跟隨規則算出來的刻意結果（下一課會詳細講這個數字怎麼算出來）——把它花在個股部的新想法上，等於在沒有明確做出這個決定的情況下，悄悄推翻了引擎自己的風險判斷。如果兩塊本來就設計成各自獨立配置，某一塊的現金水位變化，永遠不該被讀成「可以透過另一塊加碼」的綠燈。

⚠

Averaging down into a capped satellite until it is core-sized is the trap that looks most like discipline, because "buying more of something you liked at a lower price" is genuinely good advice in other contexts. Here is the mechanism that breaks it: the 3% cap was set because the durability evidence had not been supplied. A falling price supplies no such evidence — it is not a moat test, a return-on-capital test, or anything else the cap was waiting for. So each purchase raises the weight of the single least-proven name in the sleeve while the reason for capping it is still fully intact, and a position sized for a bounded loss quietly becomes one that can take a permanent bite out of the whole sleeve. The operational fix is to write the cap against total capital committed, not against current market value: once you have put 3% of assets into a name, the next purchase needs new evidence about the business, not a lower quote.

對一檔設了上限的衛星部位一路往下攤平，攤到它變成核心的規模，是最像紀律的一個陷阱，因為「一檔你本來就看好的東西跌了、就多買一點」在別的情境下確實是好建議。這裡有一個機制讓它失效：那條 3% 的上限，一開始就是因為持久性證據還沒交出來才設的。股價下跌不會交出這種證據——它不是護城河的檢驗，不是資本報酬率的檢驗，也不是那條上限在等的任何一件事。於是每買一次，都在提高整個個股部裡最沒被驗證過的那一檔的權重，而當初設上限的理由卻原封不動地還在；一個原本為「有限虧損」而設定大小的部位，就這樣悄悄變成一個能在整塊個股部上咬掉一口永久損失的部位。可操作的修法是：把上限寫在「已投入的資金總額」上，不是寫在「目前市值」上——一旦你已經把總資產的 3% 投進一檔股票，下一筆買進需要的是關於這門生意的新證據，不是一個更低的報價。

Worked Example: Sorting Eight Positions

逐步範例：把八個部位分類

A teaching-only, illustrative example — not a real portfolio or a recommendation. Imagine a stock sleeve holding eight funded positions, labeled A through H. Every percentage in this example is a share of the stock sleeve, not of total assets — if the sleeve is roughly 30% of the account, divide by about three to convert. Keeping the unit fixed matters, because mixing "percent of sleeve" and "percent of total assets" inside one calculation is one of the easiest ways to talk yourself into a position three times larger than intended.

這是一個純教學、示範用的例子——不是真實組合，也不是任何投資建議。想像一個個股部持有八個已投入資金的部位，代號從 A 到 H。這個例子裡所有的百分比，都是占個股部的比例，不是占總資產——如果個股部大約是帳戶的 30%，除以三左右就換算得回去。把單位釘死很重要，因為在同一段計算裡混用「占個股部」跟「占總資產」，是最容易把自己說服去壓一個大三倍的部位的方式之一。

Step by step逐步拆解
  1. Assign each position a role first, before any weight is chosen. A and B are durable compounders with long track records and a passed durability check — core, capped near 12–15% of the sleeve each. C is a fast-growing challenger still proving out its model — satellite-structural, capped near 6–8%. D and E are a memory-chip maker and a memory-equipment supplier, both riding one hardware capital-spending cycle — satellite-cyclical, capped near 5% each, with an explicit exit rule tied to the cycle-position signal. F and G are two different companies whose entire near-term earnings story is "build out AI data-center capacity" — one sized as core, one as satellite-structural, purely by role, before any clustering check. H is a newly initiated satellite-structural position, started at the bottom of its cap near 2%. A ninth name, J, sits on the tracking-only list at 0% and therefore never enters the arithmetic below — which is exactly what that role is for.先給每個部位分配角色，在決定任何權重之前。A 跟 B 是有長期紀錄、已經通過持久性檢驗的複利股——核心，各上限約占個股部 12–15%。C 是還在驗證商業模式的快速成長挑戰者——衛星結構，上限約 6–8%。D 跟 E 分別是一家記憶體晶片廠與一家記憶體設備供應商，同樣搭在一輪硬體資本支出循環上——衛星循環，各上限約 5%，並綁定一條跟循環位置訊號連動的明確出場規則。F 跟 G 是兩家不同公司，但近期獲利故事完全是同一件事：「擴建 AI 資料中心產能」——在做任何群聚檢查之前，純粹依角色，一個被配成核心、一個被配成衛星結構。H 是剛建立的衛星結構部位，從上限的下緣、約 2% 起步。第九個名字 J 掛在追蹤觀察清單上、權重 0%，因此從頭到尾不進入下面的算術——這正是那個角色存在的意義。
  2. Sum the role-based weights: A 14% + B 13% + C 7% + D 5% + E 5% + F 12% + G 7% + H 2% = 65% of the stock sleeve, leaving 35% of the sleeve either uninvested or held in additional positions not shown here.加總依角色配出的權重：A 14% + B 13% + C 7% + D 5% + E 5% + F 12% + G 7% + H 2% ＝ 個股部的 65%，剩下 35% 尚未投入，或是配在這裡沒列出的其他部位上。
  3. Now run the clustering check, grouping by shared driver rather than by sector label or by role. F and G, despite sitting in different sub-industries (one in networking hardware, one in power infrastructure) and holding different formal roles, both derive the overwhelming majority of their near-term earnings growth from the same driver — data-center buildout spending — and their share prices have moved together on the same days for the same headlines. That makes them one cluster worth 12% + 7% = 19% of the sleeve, not two independent 12% and 7% bets. D and E form a second cluster, and this one is easy to miss precisely because the role label already sounds like a warning: "satellite-cyclical" names the type of bet, not the fact that both are the same bet on one hardware capital-spending cycle. Together they are 5% + 5% = 10% of the sleeve riding a single cycle turn.現在跑群聚檢查，用共用的驅動因子分組，不是用產業標籤、也不是用角色分組。F 跟 G 儘管落在不同的子產業（一個是網通硬體、一個是電力基礎建設），也掛著不同的正式角色，但它們近期獲利成長的絕大部分，都來自同一個驅動因子——資料中心擴建支出——而且股價經常在同一天、因為同一則新聞同向變動。這讓它們成為同一個群聚，合計 12% + 7% ＝ 占個股部 19%，而不是兩個各自獨立的 12% 跟 7% 賭注。D 跟 E 是第二個群聚，而這一個特別容易漏掉，正是因為角色標籤本身聽起來就像一個警告：「衛星循環」講的是這筆賭注的類型，而不是「兩檔押的是同一輪硬體資本支出循環」這件事。兩者合計 5% + 5% ＝ 占個股部 10%，全押在同一次循環轉折上。

Recount independent bets using Σ(1 / cluster size): A, B, C and H stand alone (4 × 1 = 4 bets), F and G share a cluster (2 × ½ = 1 bet), and D and E share a cluster (2 × ½ = 1 bet) — 6 effective independent bets out of 8 funded positions. The headline count says eight; the real answer is six, and the concentration picture moves with it. The largest true exposure to a single underlying driver is the F+G cluster at 19% of the sleeve, not any single ticker's weight — and since the sleeve is itself about 30% of total assets, that is roughly 6% of the whole account riding one driver.用 Σ(1 ÷ 群聚檔數) 重新計算獨立賭注：A、B、C、H 各自獨立（4 × 1 ＝ 4 個賭注），F 跟 G 共用一個群聚（2 × ½ ＝ 1 個），D 跟 E 共用一個群聚（2 × ½ ＝ 1 個）——8 個已投入資金的部位，實際上是 6 個有效獨立賭注。表面數字說八，真正的答案是六，集中度的畫面也跟著改變。對單一底層驅動因子的真實最大曝險，是 F+G 群聚合計占個股部的 19%，而不是任何單一檔股票各自的權重——而個股部本身約占總資產 30%，換算回整個帳戶，大約是 6% 押在同一個驅動因子上。
  5. The action this produces is not necessarily "sell G." It is a deliberate, explicit choice, made now rather than in the middle of a drawdown: either accept 19% of the sleeve riding on one data-center-buildout driver as an intentional concentrated view, or trim one leg of the cluster — most likely the satellite-structural one, since it carries the lower evidence bar of the two roles — until the combined cluster weight sits inside the written single-theme ceiling. Measured against the illustrative 30%-of-sleeve ceiling this lesson uses, both clusters here (19% and 10%) already pass, so the honest output of this check is "documented and inside the limit," not a trade. A check that only counts when it produces a trade will quietly stop being run.這個檢查結果導出的動作，不一定是「賣掉 G」。而是一個刻意、明確的選擇，而且是現在做，不是等回撤發生到一半才做：要嘛接受個股部的 19% 押在同一個資料中心擴建驅動因子上，把它當成一個刻意的集中觀點；要嘛把群聚裡的其中一腿——最可能是衛星結構的那一腿，因為兩個角色裡它的證據門檻本來就比較低——減碼下來，直到合計的群聚權重落回寫下來的單一主題上限之內。對照這一課示範用的「占個股部 30%」上限，這裡兩個群聚（19% 與 10%）其實都已經合格，所以這次檢查誠實的產出是「已記錄、在限額之內」，不是一筆交易。一個只有在產出交易時才算數的檢查，很快就會沒有人再跑它。
