The Complete Stock Analysis Framework

股票分析完整框架

A stock is a claim on a business, inside an industry, priced by a crowd. This course teaches you to read all three layers in order — supply and demand, business quality and moat, market expectations — and then to judge, act, and size the position with rules you can defend.

一張股票，是一家公司的一部分；公司活在一個產業裡；價格由一群人共同決定。這門課教你依序讀懂這三層——供給與需求、商業本質與護城河（公司能長期擋住競爭對手搶走利潤的優勢）、市場期望——然後學會下判斷、決定行動、把部位放進一個有規則可守的組合。

Start with Module 01 →從第 01 課開始 → Review Deck複習牌組

Most stock-analysis teaching starts with valuation — P/E, DCF, multiples — as if the hard part were arithmetic. It is not. By the time you are dividing price by earnings, three bigger questions have already been silently answered for you: whether the industry lets anyone earn good returns, whether this company earns more than its cost of capital and can keep doing so, and what the crowd has already assumed. Get any of those wrong and the arithmetic only makes the mistake more precise.

多數的股票分析教學從估值開始——本益比（股價是每股盈餘的幾倍）、現金流折現（用公司未來預估能賺到的現金，反推它現在值多少錢的估價法）、各種倍數——好像難的部分是算術。其實不是。當你在把股價除以獲利的時候，有三個更大的問題已經悄悄被替你回答了：這個產業讓不讓人賺到好報酬、這家公司賺的錢是不是比資金成本高（資金成本＝把這筆錢放到別的地方，原本能賺到的報酬）、能不能一直這樣、還有市場已經假設了什麼。任何一題答錯，算術只是把錯誤算得更精確而已。

This course runs in the order the questions actually matter. Part I asks where returns come from at the industry level — demand engines, supply discipline, the capital cycle, substitution. Part II asks what a good business looks like — business model, moat sources and moat evidence, how long high returns can last, growth and earnings quality, management. Part III asks how the market prices it — what a price implies, how price feeds back into the business, and how valuation fits at the end rather than the beginning. Part IV is about judgment itself: weighing evidence, writing a falsifiable thesis, inverting, deciding to act, wait or avoid, and building a process that survives your own biases. Part V takes one stock to a portfolio — sizing, structure, trend and volatility rules, and how to test rules honestly.

這門課照問題真正重要的順序走。第一部問報酬從哪裡來：產業層級的需求引擎、供給紀律、資本週期、替代威脅。第二部問好生意長什麼樣：商業模式、護城河的來源與證據、高報酬能維持多久、成長與獲利的品質、經營層。第三部問市場怎麼定價：價格已經假設了什麼、價格怎麼反過來影響生意、以及為什麼估值放在最後而不是最前面。第四部講判斷本身：怎麼秤證據、怎麼寫一個能被證偽的論點、反過來想、決定進場／等待／迴避，並建立一套能抵抗自己偏誤的流程。第五部從一檔股票走到一個組合：部位大小、結構、趨勢與波動率規則，以及怎麼誠實地測試規則。

The one sentence to keep: the business decides whether to buy; the price decides when. Everything in the 36 modules hangs off that sentence. 要記住的一句話：生意決定買不買，價格決定何時買。36 課的所有內容都掛在這句話上。
How This Course Teaches本課程的教學方法
Concrete before abstract具體先於抽象

Every module opens with one real company or one real number, then names the concept it illustrates.

每一課都先從一家真實公司或一個真實數字開始，再說出它示範的是哪個概念。

Predict, then reveal先預測，再揭曉

Before a key answer is shown you commit to a guess. Being wrong first is what makes the right answer stick.

關鍵答案揭曉前，你先押一個猜測。先錯一次，正確答案才會真的留下來。

Worked, then faded examples先看完整範例，再自己補空

Calculations shown fully step by step, then repeated with a blank for you to fill.

計算過程先完整逐步呈現，接著再來一次，這次留一格給你自己填。

Retrieval and spacing主動回想與間隔複習

Every module ends with a quiz; later modules re-ask earlier questions; the review deck brings back what you missed.

每一課結尾都有測驗；後面的課會回頭問前面的題；複習牌組把你答錯的再帶回來。

Self-explanation自我解釋

"Think first" boxes ask you to say why before you read why. Explaining beats re-reading.

「先想一下」框請你在讀到答案前先說出理由。自己解釋一遍比重讀有效。

War stories實戰案例

Named cases where the framework was followed or ignored — and what it cost. Lessons paid for by someone else.

有名有姓的案例：框架被遵守或被忽略時，各自付出了什麼代價。別人付過學費的教訓。

The Framework at a Glance框架一覽
Part I第一部Where Returns Come From報酬從哪裡來
Does the industry let anyone earn good returns — and for how long? Demand engines, supply discipline, the profit pool, the capital cycle, substitution.這個產業讓不讓人賺到好報酬？能賺多久？需求引擎、供給紀律、利潤池（整條產業鏈裡，利潤最後被哪個環節拿走）、資本週期、替代威脅。03 – 07
Part II第二部What a Good Business Looks Like好生意長什麼樣
Does this company earn more than its cost of capital, and can it keep doing so? Business model, moat sources and evidence, return durability, growth and earnings quality, management.這家公司賺得比資金成本多嗎？能一直這樣嗎？商業模式、護城河來源與證據、報酬持續期、成長與獲利品質、經營層。08 – 17
Part III第三部How the Market Prices It市場怎麼定價
What does today's price already assume — and how does price feed back into the business? Expectations, reflexivity, valuation last.今天的價格已經假設了什麼？價格又怎麼反過來影響生意？市場期望、反身性（股價本身回頭改變公司實際體質的現象）、估值放最後。18 – 20
Part IV第四部How to Judge怎麼下判斷
How do you weigh evidence, build a falsifiable view, invert, decide to act / wait / avoid, and protect the process from yourself?怎麼秤證據、怎麼建立能被證偽的看法、反過來想、決定進場／等待／迴避、怎麼防自己壞了流程？21 – 25
Part V第五部From One Stock to a Portfolio從一檔股票到一個組合
How much of it, next to what else, under which rules? Structure, evidence-based asset allocation, and how to test rules honestly.買多少？和什麼放在一起？用什麼規則管？組合結構、有證據佐證的資產配置、怎麼誠實地測試規則。26 – 28
Part VI第六部Case Library: The Framework on Real Companies案例實戰：把框架用在真實公司上
One real company per archetype, frozen at a decision date: apply the whole framework, write your verdict, then open the reveal.每種公司原型一家真實公司，凍結在一個決策日：把整套框架跑一遍、寫下你的裁決，再打開揭曉。30 – 36
