Review Deck

複習牌組

Re-reading feels productive and mostly is not. What moves a concept from “read once” to “usable under pressure” is being asked to recall it — days later, mixed in with other topics, without the chapter heading telling you which tool to use. This deck does that. It draws twelve questions from every module you have visited, over-weighting the ones you got wrong and the ones you have never seen, and lightly retiring the ones you have answered correctly several times.

重讀讓人覺得有生產力，但大多沒有。真正把一個概念從「讀過一次」推進到「壓力下用得出來」的，是幾天後、混在其他主題裡、沒有章節標題提示你該用哪個工具的時候，被要求把它想起來。這副牌組做的就是這件事。它從你走過的每一課抽出 12 題，加重你答錯的與從沒看過的題目，已經答對好幾次的題目則慢慢減少出現。

How to use it well怎麼用才有效

Come back two or three days after a module, not the same evening. Answer before you look at the options if you can. When you get one wrong, open the module and read only the section that explains it — then close it. The point is the effort of recall, not the score.

上完一課後隔兩三天再回來，不要當天晚上。可以的話先在心裡作答再看選項。答錯了就打開那一課、只讀解釋那一題的段落，然後關掉。重點是回想的那份費力，不是分數。
