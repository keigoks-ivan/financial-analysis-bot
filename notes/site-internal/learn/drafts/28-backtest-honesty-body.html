Seven Years of Almost Free Money

七年幾乎像白撿的錢

For seven years, an exchange-traded note called XIV did something that looked, on paper, like one of the best risk-adjusted trades available to any retail investor. Launched in November 2010, it offered inverse exposure to VIX futures — in plain terms, a daily-reset bet that market volatility would stay calm or keep falling. From 2011 into late 2017, with only brief and shallow interruptions, it did almost exactly that: its net asset value compounded to roughly ten times its starting level, its annualized volatility ran well below the stock market's own, and any Sharpe ratio computed over that stretch would have made nearly every other publicly available strategy look mediocre by comparison. Independent traders who ran their own backtests on the same mechanism got the same answer: seven clean years of in-sample evidence, and a return stream that looked close to a free lunch.

有七年的時間，一檔叫做 XIV 的交易所交易票券，做到了一件光看紙面數字、像是散戶能拿到的最佳風險調整報酬交易之一。這檔商品在 2010 年 11 月掛牌，提供 VIX 期貨的反向曝險——白話說，就是每天重新計算一次的賭注，賭市場波動率會維持平靜或繼續下降。從 2011 年到 2017 年底，除了幾次短暫又不深的中斷，它幾乎完全做到了這件事：淨值累積成長到起始水位的約十倍，年化波動率遠低於股市大盤本身，而這段期間算出來的夏普比率，會讓幾乎其他所有公開可查的策略相形見絀。獨立操作者拿同一套機制自己回測，得到的是同一個答案：七年乾淨的樣本內證據，一條看起來近乎白撿的報酬曲線。

On February 5, 2018, the VIX index roughly doubled within a single session as a broad market selloff triggered a rush to hedge. XIV, structurally built to move several multiples in the opposite direction of VIX on that same daily-reset basis, lost more than 80% of its value after the close that day. Its issuer, Credit Suisse, announced weeks later that it would terminate and liquidate the note; investors who had ridden a seven-year winning streak were left holding a product that no longer existed. Nothing about the mechanism had failed — inverse VIX exposure did exactly what its structure always promised to do under those conditions. What failed was an assumption embedded in every backtest anyone had run on it: that the sample window used to measure risk contained the full range of what volatility could actually do. It did not. The sample simply had not yet lived through a day like that one.

2018 年 2 月 5 日，一波全市場的拋售引發搶購避險，VIX 指數在單一交易日之內大約翻了一倍。XIV 的設計本來就是要用同樣每日重設的機制，朝 VIX 的反方向放大好幾倍波動，那天收盤後，它的淨值蒸發超過八成。發行機構瑞士信貸幾週後宣布將終止並清算這檔票券；那些搭了七年順風車的投資人，手上留下的是一個已經不存在的商品。機制本身沒有失靈——反向 VIX 曝險在那種市況下，完全照著它的結構該做的事去做。真正失靈的，是所有人跑在它身上的每一份回測都內含的一個假設：拿來衡量風險的樣本窗口，已經涵蓋了波動率真正能做到的全部範圍。事實並非如此，樣本當時根本還沒經歷過那樣的一天。

This is a teaching case about the limits of what a backtest can prove, not a comment on any product available today — the note in question stopped trading in 2018. The mechanism worth carrying forward from it is the one this entire lesson is built around: a backtest, however carefully built, can only describe the range of conditions its sample actually lived through, and the gap between that range and reality does not show up as an error message. It shows up, if at all, on the one day it matters most.

這是一段關於「回測到底能證明什麼、證明到什麼程度為止」的教學案例，不是對任何現行商品的評論——這裡談到的商品在 2018 年就已經下市。值得從這個案例帶走的機制，正是整堂課要圍繞的核心：一份回測不管做得多嚴謹，都只能描述它的樣本實際經歷過的那個條件範圍，而那個範圍跟現實之間的落差，並不會用一則錯誤訊息提醒你。如果它真的要現形，通常就是在最要命的那一天才會現形。

Takeaway收斂

A backtest tells you what happened inside the window you fed it. It cannot tell you what the window left out — and the window always leaves something out.

回測告訴你的是「餵給它的那段窗口裡發生了什麼」。它沒辦法告訴你這個窗口漏掉了什麼——而窗口永遠會漏掉一些東西。

Survivorship Bias and Unadjusted Prices: The Starting Line Is Already Tilted

存活者偏誤與未還原股價：起跑線本身就是歪的

Two of the oldest and most basic distortions in any backtest happen before a single trading rule is even applied — they live in how the raw data was assembled.

任何回測裡最古老、也最基本的兩種扭曲，發生在任何一條交易規則被套用之前——它們藏在原始資料是怎麼被組出來的這件事本身。

The first is survivorship biasBuilding a historical test using only the companies, funds, or index members that still exist today, silently excluding everything that failed, was delisted, or was acquired along the way.. If a backtest of, say, quality large-cap stocks pulls today's list of index members and applies a rule to that list going back thirty years, every company that was removed from the index along the way — because it went bankrupt, was delisted, or simply shrank out of relevance — is invisible to the test. Enron and WorldCom were both large, well-known index constituents before their accounting failures became public in 2001 and 2002; both were removed from major indices as their share prices collapsed. A backtest built off “today’s S&P 500 membership, run backward” never sees either company's failure, because by the time you pulled the list, both were long gone from it. The effect is not random noise: it mechanically removes failures and keeps successes, which is why academic studies of mutual fund databases that fail to account for it have found the resulting historical average return inflated by somewhere in the neighborhood of one to two percentage points a year — a gap large enough to turn a mediocre long-run record into a seemingly good one.

第一種叫存活者偏誤建立歷史測試時只用「今天還存在」的公司、基金或指數成分股，把沿路失敗、下市、或被收購的標的，全部悄悄排除在外。。如果一份針對優質大型股的回測，是拿「今天」的指數成分股清單，套用一條規則往回跑三十年，那麼沿路曾經被剔除出指數的每一家公司——因為破產、下市、或單純規模萎縮到不再重要——對這份測試而言完全不存在。安隆（Enron）與世界通訊（WorldCom）在 2001 年與 2002 年會計弊案曝光之前，都是規模龐大、廣為人知的指數成分股；隨著股價崩跌，兩者都陸續被主要指數剔除。一份「拿今天的標普 500 成分股清單、往回套用」的回測，永遠看不到這兩家公司的失敗，因為等到你把清單抓下來的時候，它們早就不在清單上了。這個效果不是隨機雜訊：它是機械性地把失敗的標的拿掉、把成功的標的留下，這也是為什麼學術上研究共同基金資料庫時，一旦沒有處理這個問題，算出來的歷史平均報酬率會被灌高大約每年一到兩個百分點——這個落差已經足以把一份平庸的長期紀錄，包裝成看起來還不錯的紀錄。

The second, related but distinct, is failing to use adjusted pricesA price history rescaled so that stock splits and dividend payments do not create artificial jumps in the series — also called total-return or split-and-dividend-adjusted prices.. A stock split or a large special dividend changes the per-share price without changing anything about the underlying business or a shareholder's actual wealth. A raw, unadjusted price series treats a 4-for-1 split as if the price had genuinely fallen 75% overnight; feed that into a rule that buys on sharp price drops, and the rule will fire on a corporate action that was never a real drawdown at all, distorting both the entry signal and any return calculated across that date. The error is not small and not random — it is tied precisely to the date of the corporate action and can be off by a factor of two, four, or more depending on the split ratio involved.

第二個問題跟第一個有關但不一樣，就是沒有使用還原股價重新按比例縮放過的股價歷史，讓股票分割與配息不會在序列裡製造出人為的跳空——也叫做總報酬價或拆股與配息調整後價格。。股票分割或大額特別股息，改變的是每股價格，跟底層生意、股東實際的財富完全無關。一段未還原的原始股價序列，會把一次 1 股分割成 4 股的動作，當成股價一夜之間真的跌了 75%；把這種資料餵給一條「股價大跌就買進」的規則，這條規則就會對一次根本不是真實回撤的公司行動觸發買進，同時扭曲進場訊號與任何橫跨那個日期算出來的報酬率。這個誤差不小也不隨機——它精確綁定在那次公司行動的日期上，依分割比例不同，可能整整錯了兩倍、四倍甚至更多。

The counter-example worth holding onto: a data provider that explicitly labels its price series as split-and-dividend-adjusted, and a universe construction method described as point-in-timeHistorical data reconstructed to show exactly what a database would have contained at each past date — including companies since delisted, acquired, or gone bankrupt — rather than today's list applied backward. — meaning the index membership used for any given historical date reflects who was actually in the index on that date, delistings and all. Neither guarantee is visible in a single headline chart; both are usually disclosed, if at all, in a methodology footnote most readers skip.

值得記住的反例是：一家資料供應商明確標註它的股價序列已經「拆股與配息調整過」，而母體的建構方式描述成時點正確重建的歷史資料能精確顯示資料庫在過去每一個日期「當時實際包含什麼」——包括後來下市、被收購、破產的公司——而不是拿今天的清單往回套用。——意思是任何一個歷史日期用的指數成分股，反映的是那一天實際在指數裡的名單，下市名單也算進去。這兩個保證都不會出現在頭條圖表上，通常只會寫在方法論的一個小註腳裡，而多數讀者根本不會去看那個註腳。

How to see it from public information: look for the specific phrases “adjusted close,” “total return,” or “point-in-time” in a report's methodology section. If a report only says it used “historical constituents of the index” without specifying whether that means today's list applied backward or a reconstructed point-in-time list, treat the number as unverified rather than assuming the better-faith interpretation.

怎麼從公開資訊看出來：在報告的方法論段落裡找「調整後收盤價」「總報酬」或「時點正確」這幾個具體字眼。如果一份報告只寫「使用指數的歷史成分股」，卻沒說清楚那是今天的清單往回套，還是重建過的時點正確清單，就把這個數字當成尚未查證，不要自動往對報告有利的那個方向解讀。

Look-Ahead Bias: Using Information the Market Did Not Have Yet

前視偏誤：用了市場當時還沒有的資訊

Look-ahead biasA backtest accidentally using information that would not actually have been available at the historical point in time it claims to be trading — the test quietly gets to see the future. is subtler than survivorship bias because it rarely announces itself. A common version: testing an earnings-based rule using a company's financial data as it appears today, in a database that has since been restated. Companies revise reported figures after the fact for all kinds of reasons — accounting corrections, occasionally outright fraud unwound years later — and a database that stores only the current, corrected numbers is quietly feeding a trading rule information that no investor actually holding the stock at that historical date could have known. A rule built on “buy when reported earnings quality looks clean” can look excellent in a backtest that only ever sees the cleaned-up, restated version of history, and far weaker once tested on data as it was originally reported at the time.

前視偏誤回測不小心用了在它宣稱交易的那個歷史時點，實際上根本還不存在的資訊——測試安靜地「偷看」了未來。比存活者偏誤更隱蔽，因為它幾乎不會主動現形。一個常見的版本：用一家公司「現在」的財報資料去測試一條盈餘相關的規則，但這個資料庫後來已經把當年的數字重編過。公司事後修正已公布的數字，原因五花八門——會計更正、有時候是好幾年後才被拆穿的舞弊——一個只存最新、修正後數字的資料庫，等於悄悄餵給交易規則一份在那個歷史時點，真正持有這檔股票的投資人根本不可能知道的資訊。一條「盈餘品質看起來乾淨就買進」的規則，如果測試永遠只看得到事後修正乾淨的版本，回測結果可能非常漂亮，但如果改用「當時原始公布」的資料重測，往往會弱得多。

The counter-example is a database explicitly built to preserve the original, as-first-reported figures alongside any later restatement, letting a researcher choose which version a rule should see. The positive case worth naming: academic research comparing earnings-surprise strategies built on restated data versus as-first-reported data has repeatedly found that a meaningful share of the apparent edge disappears once the test is limited to information genuinely available at the time — not because the original finding was fabricated, but because part of it was, without anyone intending it, testing hindsight.

反例是一個明確設計成同時保留「最初公布版本」與「後續任何修正版本」的資料庫，讓研究者自己選擇一條規則該看到哪一個版本。值得點名的正面案例是：學術研究比較「用事後修正資料建構的盈餘意外策略」跟「用當時原始公布資料建構的同一套策略」，一再發現只要把測試限制在那個時點真正能取得的資訊，原本看起來的優勢有相當一部分會消失——不是因為原始發現是造假的，而是因為其中一部分，在沒有人刻意這麼做的情況下，其實測到的是後見之明。

How to test for it without access to the underlying code: ask whether the report describes using “as-reported” or “as-first-available” data, or whether it simply says “financial data from [database name]” without specifying the vintage. A more direct test, when the underlying process is available for inspection, is to take the historical data after some cutoff date, distort it deliberately — for instance, multiply prices after that date by ten — and rerun the exact same signal and weight calculations. If any output before the cutoff date changes at all, the system was looking at data it should not have had access to yet; if the pre-cutoff output is bit-for-bit identical, the system passes this specific test, though it does not by itself rule out every other form of the bias.

在拿不到底層程式碼的情況下怎麼測：問報告有沒有說明用的是「當時公布」或「當時可得」的資料，還是只寫「資料來自某某資料庫」而完全沒說明版本。如果能檢視底層流程，一個更直接的測試方法是：把某個切點之後的歷史資料刻意改動——例如把切點之後的價格全部乘以十——然後重新跑一次完全相同的訊號與權重計算。如果切點之前的任何輸出因此改變了，代表系統偷看了它當時不該拿到的資料；如果切點之前的輸出逐格不變，系統就通過了這個特定測試，雖然這不代表它排除了所有其他形式的前視偏誤。

Think first: why does a slightly weaker out-of-sample result actually count as good news, not bad news?先想一下：為什麼樣本外表現稍微變差一點，其實是好消息，不是壞消息？

A rule designed and calibrated on one stretch of history will always fit that stretch slightly better than it fits data it has never seen — some of what made it look good in-sample is genuine, repeatable structure, and some of it is the design process quietly bending itself around noise specific to that window. A small, honest gap between in-sample and out-of-sample performance is the expected signature of a real, if imperfect, edge: most of the effect survived, a little bit of overfitting bled off. The dangerous pattern is not a modest gap — it is out-of-sample performance collapsing toward zero, flipping sign, or the in-sample number being suspiciously perfect in the first place. A rule that performs identically in-sample and out-of-sample, with zero degradation at all, should raise its own kind of suspicion: real market structure evolves, and a rule immune to that evolution is unusual enough to deserve a second look at how the split was actually constructed.

一條在某段歷史上設計並校準出來的規則，永遠會比它從沒看過的資料，稍微更貼合那段歷史一點——它在樣本內表現好的原因，一部分是真實、可重複的結構，一部分是設計過程不知不覺順著那段窗口特有的雜訊彎過去了。樣本內與樣本外表現之間，出現一個小而誠實的落差，正是一個真實但不完美的優勢該有的樣子：大部分效果留下來了，一點點過度擬合被排掉了。真正危險的型態，不是這個溫和的落差——而是樣本外表現直接崩到接近零、甚至反過來變號，或者樣本內數字一開始就好得可疑。一條規則在樣本內與樣本外表現完全一樣、一點退化都沒有，反而應該引來另一種懷疑：真實的市場結構會演化，一條完全不受影響的規則，罕見到值得回頭檢查那個切分到底是怎麼做出來的。

In-Sample Fit Is Not Survival: Why a Rule Must Prove Itself on Data It Has Never Seen

樣本內擬合不等於活下來：為什麼規則要先在沒看過的資料上證明自己

The mechanism: any rule with enough adjustable knobs — a threshold here, a lookback window there — can eventually be tuned to fit almost any specific stretch of history well, the same way a curve with enough bendable points can be made to pass through almost any scattered set of dots. This is called overfittingTuning a rule's parameters until it fits a specific historical dataset unusually well, at the cost of that fit having little to do with any repeatable, forward-looking edge., and the honest defense against it is walk-forward validationSplitting historical data into a training period, used to design or calibrate a rule, and a separate testing period the rule never saw during design, then checking whether performance holds up on the testing period.: split the available history into a training stretch, used to design or calibrate the rule, and a held-out testing stretch the rule was never allowed to see during design. Only performance on the testing stretch tells you anything about whether the rule found a repeatable pattern or just memorized noise. This is not an optional refinement to run after a rule looks promising — treating it as a hard precondition for adopting any rule, rather than an afterthought, is the entire point.

機制是：只要一條規則有夠多可調整的旋鈕——這裡一個門檻、那裡一個回顧窗口——最終幾乎都能被調到很貼合某一段特定的歷史，就像一條有夠多可彎折點的曲線，幾乎能穿過任何一堆散亂的點。這叫過度擬合把一條規則的參數調整到特別貼合某一份特定的歷史資料，代價是這份貼合跟任何真實、可重複、面向未來的優勢幾乎無關。，而誠實的對策是前進式驗證（樣本外驗證）把可用的歷史資料切成訓練期（用來設計或校準規則）與另一段測試期（規則在設計過程中完全沒看過），再檢查表現在測試期是否依然成立。：把可用的歷史資料切成一段訓練期用來設計或校準規則，再留出一段測試期，規則在設計過程中完全不准看到。只有測試期的表現，才能告訴你這條規則找到的究竟是真實可重複的型態，還是只是背下了雜訊。這不是規則看起來有搞頭之後才順手做的加分項——把它當成採用任何規則之前的硬性前提，而不是事後補做的動作，正是整件事的重點。

A real illustration of how large the gap can be: academic research into the population of published stock-market “factors” — systematic characteristics claimed to predict returns — has grown to several hundred distinct candidates in the finance literature. A widely cited 2016 study by Campbell Harvey and co-authors argued that, given how many factors have been tested across decades of research using overlapping datasets, the conventional statistical bar for calling a result “significant” is far too low, and proposed a much higher bar specifically to correct for how many variations had effectively already been tried. Their point was not that every published factor is fake — it is that when hundreds of researchers each test dozens of variations against the same limited history of market data, some fraction of what gets published as a discovery is, mathematically, expected to be noise that happened to look like a pattern, and there is no way to tell which fraction without out-of-sample confirmation on data none of those studies used.

這個落差可以有多大，一個真實的例子：學術界對已發表的股市「因子」——宣稱能預測報酬的系統性特徵——研究到現在已經累積到金融文獻裡有好幾百個不同的候選項目。哈維（Campbell Harvey）與合著者在 2016 年一篇被廣泛引用的研究裡主張，考量過去幾十年的研究其實反覆用著高度重疊的資料集測試了這麼多因子，傳統上用來判定「統計顯著」的門檻其實太低了，並提出一個明顯更高的門檻，專門用來修正「等於已經試過多少種變體」這件事。他們的重點不是說每一個已發表的因子都是假的——而是當幾百位研究者，各自對同一段有限的市場歷史資料測試幾十種變體，數學上必然有一部分被發表成「發現」的東西，其實只是碰巧看起來像型態的雜訊，而且在沒有一份誰都沒用過的樣本外資料驗證之前，沒有辦法知道是哪一部分。

This connects to a companion discipline: when a research team adds a new filter, threshold, or confirmation condition to an existing rule, the addition should be tested with an ablation testRemoving or adding one single mechanism at a time and measuring its independent contribution to the result, rather than judging a bundle of changes together. — isolating exactly what that one addition contributed, independent of everything else. A genuinely counter-intuitive result shows up often enough here to be worth naming directly: a filter added specifically because it looked like it should make a rule more robust sometimes makes every performance measure worse at once, because the extra layer delays a reaction the original rule's edge actually depended on. The lesson is not “never add filters” — it is that a filter earns its place only by passing its own independent test, not by sounding more careful than the version without it.

這連結到另一項配套紀律：當一個研究團隊在既有規則上加一個新的濾網、門檻或確認條件，這個新增動作應該用消融測試一次只移除或加入單一一個機制，量測它獨立的貢獻，而不是把一整包改動放在一起判斷。來檢驗——單獨隔離出這一項新增動作，究竟獨立貢獻了什麼，跟其他一切無關。這裡經常出現一個真正反直覺的結果，值得直接點名：一個「看起來就該讓規則更穩健」而加進去的濾網，有時候反而讓每一項績效指標同時變差，因為多出來的那一層，延遲了原始規則的優勢原本就依賴的那個反應速度。這裡的教訓不是「永遠不要加濾網」——而是一個濾網要贏得留下來的資格，靠的是通過它自己獨立的測試，不是聽起來比沒加它的版本更謹慎。

📜War Story戰爭故事

Long-Term Capital Management launched in 1994 with a partner list that included two future Nobel laureates in economics and a track record, in its first years, of annual returns in the high thirties and low forties percent. Its trades were built on rigorous historical modeling of how related bond and derivative prices should converge over time, sized with leverage that, by 1998, put on-balance-sheet assets at roughly $125 billion against roughly $4.7 billion of capital — leverage on the order of 25-to-1, with derivative exposure extending the effective bet size far beyond even that. The models had been tested extensively against historical data and treated the fund's many separate convergence trades, spread across different markets, as largely independent bets whose risks would diversify against one another.

長期資本管理公司（LTCM）於 1994 年成立，合夥人名單裡包括兩位日後拿下諾貝爾經濟學獎的學者，成立最初幾年，年報酬率落在百分之三十幾到四十出頭。它的交易建立在對相關債券與衍生品價格「隨時間應該如何收斂」的嚴謹歷史建模之上，倉位規模用槓桿撐大，到 1998 年，資產負債表上的資產大約 1,250 億美元，對應資本大約只有 47 億美元——槓桿倍數大約落在 25 倍上下，而衍生品曝險又把實際的賭注規模再往外延伸得更遠。這些模型經過廣泛的歷史資料測試，而且把基金橫跨不同市場的多筆收斂交易，當成彼此大致獨立的賭注，理論上風險會互相分散。

Russia's default on its domestic debt in August 1998 triggered a global flight to quality that made trades LTCM's models treated as independent move together instead — correlations across markets that had rarely moved in tandem in the historical sample used to build the models converged toward one all at once, exactly when leverage meant there was no room to absorb it. The fund lost roughly $4.6 billion in a matter of weeks, and the Federal Reserve organized a private-sector recapitalization by fourteen banks to unwind the position in an orderly way rather than let a forced liquidation of that size destabilize the broader market. The models were not wrong about how those instruments behaved most of the time — they were tested carefully, on real historical data, and correctly described ordinary market conditions. What the historical sample used to build them had never contained was a global event large enough to make normally uncorrelated trades move as one at the same moment the fund had no spare capital to survive it.

1998 年 8 月，俄羅斯對本國債務違約，引發全球資金搶進避險資產的浪潮，讓 LTCM 模型原本視為互相獨立的交易，同時朝同一個方向移動——這些在建模用的歷史樣本裡很少同步變動的跨市場相關性，一次全部收斂到接近 1，而且剛好發生在槓桿讓基金完全沒有空間吸收這種衝擊的時候。基金在短短幾週內虧損大約 46 億美元，聯準會出面協調十四家銀行進行民間部門的資本重組，以有秩序的方式解除部位，而不是任由這麼大規模的強制平倉去撼動更廣泛的市場。模型在多數時候對這些工具行為的描述，其實並沒有錯——它們是用真實歷史資料仔細測試過的，也正確描述了一般市況。它們用來建模的那份歷史樣本裡從來沒有出現過的，是一場規模大到足以讓原本不相關的交易，在基金完全沒有剩餘資本可以撐過去的同一刻，全部同步移動的全球事件。

The lesson to carry forward, distinct from the leverage math itself: a risk budget built on a backtested correlation structure is only as honest as the range of regimes the backtest actually contained. Diversification measured across trades that have never yet been tested by a systemic shock is not the same thing as diversification that has survived one.

要帶走的教訓，跟槓桿數學本身是分開的一件事：一份建立在回測相關性結構上的風險預算，誠實程度只跟這份回測實際涵蓋過的市況範圍一樣高。用一組還沒被系統性衝擊真正測試過的交易去量出來的「分散」，跟真正撐過一次系統性衝擊、活下來的「分散」，並不是同一件事。

Worked Example: Auditing a Backtest That Looks Too Good逐步範例：審一份「看起來好到不像話」的回測

Meridian Rotation is an illustrative teaching case, not a real published product — a hypothetical monthly sector-ETF rotation strategy across ten sector ETFs, rebalanced on the first trading day of each month. Its published 2005–2024 backtest reports: CAGR 24.3%, maximum drawdown −11.8%, Sharpe ratio 2.1, zero losing calendar years. Below are the six questions worth asking before believing any of it, in the order they matter, with the answers a careful review would actually find.

「經緯輪動」是一個虛構的教學案例，不是真實發表過的商品——一套假設性的每月產業型 ETF 輪動策略，橫跨十檔產業 ETF，每月第一個交易日再平衡。它公布的 2005 到 2024 年回測結果是：年化報酬率 24.3%、最大回撤 −11.8%、夏普比率 2.1、沒有任何一個曆年是虧損的。以下是相信這份數字之前，值得依序問的六個問題，以及一次認真複查真的會查到的答案。

  1. 1

    Are the prices adjusted? Three of the ten ETFs underwent fund mergers or special distributions during the window that the backtest treated as raw price drops, occasionally triggering a false “buy the dip” rotation signal. Correcting for this alone brings CAGR down to about 21.8%.

    股價有還原嗎？十檔 ETF 裡有三檔在這段窗口內經歷過基金合併或特別配發，回測把這些事件當成真實的股價下跌處理，偶爾觸發了假的「逢低買進」輪動訊號。光是修正這一項，年化報酬率就降到 約 21.8%。

  2. 2

    Is the universe point-in-time? The ten ETFs used were selected as “the ten most liquid sector ETFs today” and applied backward — three of the sector ETFs that existed and were reasonably liquid for parts of the 2005–2012 stretch were excluded because they later merged out of existence or lost relevance. Rebuilding the universe from what actually existed and traded at each historical date, using fifteen candidate ETFs instead of ten, brings CAGR down further to about 17.4%.

    母體是時點正確的嗎？這十檔 ETF 是用「今天流動性最好的十檔產業 ETF」挑出來再往回套用的——三檔在 2005 到 2012 年部分時段確實存在、流動性也還算不錯的產業 ETF，被排除在外，因為它們後來合併消失或不再重要。改用「每個歷史時點實際存在並交易」的母體重建，候選 ETF 從十檔擴大到十五檔，年化報酬率再往下降到 約 17.4%。

  3. 3

    Was it walk-forward tested? The published number is a single 20-year in-sample fit with no held-out period. Splitting the same 20 years into a 10-year training window and a separate 10-year test window shows the Sharpe ratio falling from 2.1 in-sample to about 0.9 out-of-sample — a meaningful gap, though not a collapse to zero.

    有做過前進式驗證嗎？公布的數字是單一一段 20 年的樣本內擬合，沒有留出任何一段測試期。把同樣 20 年切成 10 年訓練窗口與另外 10 年測試窗口，可以看到夏普比率從樣本內的 2.1，降到樣本外的 約 0.9——落差不小，但沒有崩到零。

  4. 4

    Was timing luck audited? The rule always rebalances on the first trading day of the month. Rerunning the identical rule on each of the roughly twenty other trading days available in a typical month produces Calmar ratios ranging from about 0.6 to about 1.3 — and the first-trading-day anchor, the one actually published, happens to be the best-performing choice in the set.

    有做時機運氣審計嗎？規則固定在每月第一個交易日再平衡。把完全相同的規則，換成一般月份裡其他大約二十個交易日各跑一次，算出來的卡瑪比率落在約 0.6 到約 1.3 之間——而「第一個交易日」這個實際被拿去公布的錨點，剛好就是這組結果裡表現最好的那一個。

  5. 5

    Is trading cost modeled? The published backtest assumes zero slippage and zero commission. With roughly 200% annual turnover from monthly rotation across ten concentrated positions and a conservative round-trip slippage estimate of about 0.15%, honest cost modeling removes roughly 2 percentage points of CAGR per year.

    有把交易成本建模進去嗎？公布的回測假設零滑價、零手續費。以每月輪動十檔集中部位、約 200% 的年周轉率，加上保守估計約 0.15% 的來回滑價，誠實建模成本後，每年會從年化報酬率裡扣掉大約 2 個百分點。

  6. 6

    Is performance reported by regime, not just full-sample? Excluding the 2008–2009 financial crisis and the 2020 pandemic crash, almost all of the strategy's edge over simply holding a broad index comes from the 2009–2021 bull run; inside the two crash windows specifically, it performs no better than a passive benchmark, and briefly worse. The full-sample headline number buries this entirely.

    有沒有分市場狀態報告，而不是只給全樣本一個數字？排除 2008 到 2009 年金融海嘯與 2020 年疫情崩盤，這套策略相對於單純持有大盤指數的優勢，幾乎全部來自 2009 到 2021 年的多頭行情；單獨看這兩段崩盤窗口，它的表現不比被動基準更好，甚至一度更差。全樣本的頭條數字，把這件事完全埋起來了。

Takeaway收斂

Six questions turned “CAGR 24.3%, Sharpe 2.1, no losing years, must-buy” into “a real but modest and regime-dependent edge, roughly Sharpe 0.9 out-of-sample, that adds nothing during the two crashes an investor would most want protection in.” Both descriptions are consistent with the exact same underlying rule — only one of them is honest enough to size a position on.

六個問題，把「年化 24.3%、夏普 2.1、沒有虧損年、非買不可」，翻成了「一個真實但溫和、而且看市場狀態決定有沒有用的優勢，樣本外夏普大約 0.9，而且在投資人最需要保護的那兩段崩盤裡完全沒有幫助」。這兩種描述講的是同一條底層規則，只是其中一種誠實到可以拿來決定部位大小，另一種不行。

Timing Luck: When the Calendar Itself Is a Hidden Free Parameter

時機運氣：連日曆本身都是一個藏起來的自由參數

Every rule that operates on a fixed schedule — rebalance monthly, judge trend weekly, roll options on expiration — has to pick a specific anchor within that schedule: which day of the month, which day of the week. That choice rarely gets treated as a parameter worth testing, because it does not feel like a trading decision the way a threshold or a lookback window does. It is one anyway. Timing luckThe portion of a backtest's performance that comes from an arbitrary but unexamined implementation choice, such as which specific date a rule rebalances on, rather than from the rule's actual logic. is the performance difference that shows up purely from which anchor got picked, holding every other part of the rule's logic identical.

任何按固定週期運作的規則——每月再平衡、每週判斷趨勢、選擇權到期日轉倉——都得在這個週期裡挑一個具體的錨點：每月第幾天、每週第幾天。這個選擇很少被當成一個值得測試的參數，因為它感覺不像門檻或回顧窗口那樣是一個「交易決策」。但它其實就是。時機運氣一份回測的表現裡，來自某個任意、卻從未被檢驗過的實作選擇（例如規則具體在哪一天再平衡）的那一部分，而不是來自規則本身真正的邏輯。指的是：規則的其他邏輯完全不變，光靠錨點選了哪一個，就冒出來的表現差異。

A timing-luck auditSystematically rerunning an identical rule across every reasonable choice of an implementation detail that was never treated as a free parameter, such as the specific weekday used for a weekly signal, to see how much the headline result depends on that one arbitrary choice. makes this visible: rerun the identical rule across every reasonable choice of the anchor and look at the spread of results. A rule with a genuine edge shows a modest, tight spread — the specific day matters a little, not a lot. A rule whose headline number depends heavily on one particular anchor, especially when that anchor happens to be exactly the one published, is telling you that a meaningful share of its apparent edge is not edge at all.

時機運氣審計系統性地把完全相同的規則，套用在每一個合理的實作細節選項上重跑一遍——例如週線訊號具體用週幾——這個細節從來沒被當成自由參數處理過，藉此看頭條數字有多依賴這一個任意選擇。能讓這件事現形：把完全相同的規則，套用在每一個合理的錨點選項上重跑一次，看結果的分散程度。一條真的有優勢的規則，分散程度會溫和又收斂——具體是哪一天，影響一點點，不是影響很多。一條頭條數字嚴重依賴某一個特定錨點的規則，尤其當那個錨點剛好就是被拿去公布的那一個，等於在告訴你：它表面上的優勢，有相當一部分根本不是優勢。

The counter-example worth naming: a report that discloses the full distribution of results across every anchor tested, not just the best one, and states its headline number as a range or a median rather than the single most flattering data point. That kind of disclosure is rare precisely because it makes the headline look less impressive — which is exactly why its presence is a positive signal about the report's honesty.

值得點名的反例是：一份報告揭露了測試過的每一個錨點的完整結果分布，不是只給最好的那一個，並且把頭條數字用一個區間或中位數表達，而不是那個最漂亮的單一數字。這種揭露之所以罕見，正是因為它會讓頭條數字看起來沒那麼亮眼——這也正是為什麼它一旦出現，就是這份報告誠實度的正面訊號。

📜War Story戰爭故事

A systematic research team, working on a rules-based trend-following engine, widened the threshold that triggers a rebalancing trade — a change made explicitly to cut the number of trades and notifications generated each year, not to chase a higher return. As a side effect, one market's headline Calmar ratio also happened to improve. Rather than accepting that as a bonus, the team ran the specific audit this lesson describes: they reran the identical widened-threshold rule using each of five different weekdays as the weekly rebalancing anchor. On four of the five weekday choices, the same threshold change made the Calmar ratio worse. Only on the weekday that had actually been adopted in production did it improve — and that was the only version anyone had looked at before the audit.

一個做規則化趨勢跟蹤引擎的研究團隊，把觸發再平衡交易的門檻放寬了——這個改動的動機明確是為了減少每年產生的交易與通知次數，不是為了追求更高的報酬。結果作為副作用，其中一個市場的頭條卡瑪比率也剛好跟著變好了。這個團隊沒有直接把這當成額外的紅利收下，而是跑了這一課描述的那個具體審計：他們把完全相同、已經放寬門檻的規則，套用在五個不同的週幾各當一次週線再平衡錨點重跑一遍。五個週幾選項裡，有四個讓同一個門檻改動反而讓卡瑪比率變差。只有一個實際被拿去實際使用的週幾，卡瑪比率才變好——而那個版本，正是稽核之前唯一一個有人看過的版本。

The team had a standing internal rule stating that any performance change coming from an execution-layer adjustment should be treated as noise, not a claimed improvement, until proven otherwise — the audit confirmed that rule had been right from the start. The change to the threshold was kept, because reducing trading frequency was genuinely worth doing on its own terms. The improved Calmar ratio was not kept as a claim, because the audit showed it was not real.

這個團隊本來就有一條內部既定的規則：任何來自執行層調整的績效改變，在證明不是雜訊之前，一律視為雜訊，不能當成一項可以宣稱的績效改善——這次審計證實了這條規則從一開始就是對的。門檻的改動被保留了下來，因為降低交易頻率本身就有它獨立成立的價值。但那個看起來變好的卡瑪比率沒有被拿去當作宣稱，因為審計證明它不是真的。

Hidden Variables in Comparison Tables, and Honest Cost Attribution

對照表裡藏著的變數，以及誠實的成本歸因

A comparison table with two columns and one clearly labeled variable invites a reader to assume that variable is the only thing that changed. It is a natural assumption and, often, a wrong one. An apples-to-apples comparisonA comparison where every condition except the one variable being studied is held identical between the two things being compared — the opposite of a table that silently varies two things at once. requires actively confirming that every setting other than the one under discussion is genuinely held constant — not assuming it because the table's formatting implies it. This kind of error is not a calculation mistake; every individual number in each column can be perfectly correct, and the table can still mislead, because the mistake lives in what the table's layout silently implies rather than in any single cell.

一張兩欄式、標明了一個變數的對照表，會讓讀者自然假設那個變數是唯一改變的東西。這是一個很自然的假設，卻經常是錯的。同條件比較除了正在研究的那一個變數之外，其餘所有條件在被比較的兩者之間都完全維持一致——跟一張悄悄同時改變兩個變數的表格正好相反。需要主動去確認，除了正在討論的那個變數之外，其餘每一項設定是不是真的維持不變——不能只因為表格的排版看起來暗示如此，就想當然爾。這種錯誤不是計算錯誤；每一欄裡的每一個數字都可以完全正確，表格照樣可能誤導人，因為問題出在表格排版悄悄暗示了什麼，不是出在任何一格數字本身。

A related discipline covers trading cost specifically: turnoverThe total amount of position change within a period, typically expressed as a percentage of assets bought or sold — used to estimate the trading cost a strategy incurs. and slippageThe gap between the price a signal was calculated at and the price actually achieved when the trade executed, usually caused by liquidity or timing delay. must be modeled explicitly, and any return gap between two configurations must be split into the part explained by different trading costs and the part that is a genuine difference in edge. A common misjudgment is seeing that two configurations have different turnover and assuming the entire performance gap between them comes from cost — in practice, honest decomposition often finds that cost explains only a modest single-digit-percentage share of a return gap, with the rest being a real difference in what the two rules actually chose to hold.

另一項相關紀律專門針對交易成本：周轉率一段期間內部位變動的總量，通常用買進或賣出金額占資產比重表示——用來估算一套策略會承受多少交易成本。與滑價訊號算出來的價格，跟交易實際成交價之間的落差，通常由流動性不足或執行延遲造成。必須顯性建模，而且兩個版本之間任何一段報酬落差，都必須拆成「由不同交易成本解釋的部分」跟「真正屬於優勢差異的部分」。一個常見的誤判，是看到兩個版本的周轉率不同，就直接假設兩者之間全部的績效落差都來自成本——實務上，誠實拆解出來的結果，經常發現成本只能解釋落差裡個位數百分比的一小部分，剩下的絕大部分，才是兩條規則真正選擇持有的東西不一樣所造成的真實差異。

How to see either problem from a published report: for hidden variables, read the table's footnotes for exactly which rule version, parameter set, and time window each column used, and treat any table that does not specify this for both columns as unverified. For cost attribution, look for a report that states a cost-only estimate in percentage points separately from the total return gap; a report that only shows a final net number, with no cost breakdown at all, has not actually demonstrated where its edge comes from.

怎麼從公開報告裡看出這兩個問題：關於藏起來的變數，去讀表格的註腳，確認每一欄用的規則版本、參數組合、時間窗口分別是什麼，任何一張沒有對兩欄都講清楚這些的表格，就當成尚未查證。關於成本歸因，找報告有沒有把「純成本估計」用百分點單獨列出來，跟總報酬落差分開講；一份只給最後淨數字、完全沒有成本拆解的報告，其實還沒有真正證明它的優勢是從哪裡來的。

📜War Story戰爭故事

A widely circulated internal leverage comparison table, cited repeatedly as evidence for how much extra return a leveraged configuration produced, put a 1.5× leveraged version running under a newer, wider execution-layer ruleset side by side with a 1.0× unleveraged version running under the older, narrower ruleset — presented as a clean, single-variable leverage comparison. It was not one. Two things had changed between the columns at once: the leverage multiple and the execution rules governing how often and how aggressively the system traded. Every individual number in both columns was calculated correctly; the error was not arithmetic. It was that the table's layout implicitly told readers only one variable had moved, when in fact two had.

一份被廣泛引用、多次拿來當作「槓桿版本多產生了多少報酬」證據的內部槓桿對照表，把一個用較新、較寬鬆執行層規則跑出來的 1.5 倍槓桿版本，跟一個用較舊、較窄執行層規則跑出來的 1.0 倍無槓桿版本並排放在一起——呈現成一張乾淨的、單一變數的槓桿對照表。它其實不是。兩欄之間同時改變了兩件事：槓桿倍數，以及決定系統交易頻率與力道的執行層規則。兩欄裡的每一個數字，計算本身都是對的；問題不在算術。問題在於，表格的排版隱含地告訴讀者只有一個變數變了，實際上有兩個。

Nobody caught it by eyeballing the numbers, because there was nothing wrong with any single number to catch. It surfaced only when someone asked the specific question this lesson recommends: besides the variable this table claims to be about, what else is different between the two columns? The fix was not a recalculation — it was rebuilding the comparison with the execution ruleset held constant on both sides, isolating the leverage variable on its own.

沒有人光靠肉眼看數字就抓到這個問題，因為根本沒有任何一個單一數字算錯。它是在有人問出這一課建議的那個具體問題之後才浮現的：除了這張表宣稱要比較的那個變數之外，兩欄之間還有什麼不一樣？修正的方法不是重新計算，而是重新建構一張兩欄都固定同一套執行層規則的對照表，把槓桿這一個變數真正單獨隔離出來。

Think first: why is a rule's adjustable format — say, whether it rebalances weekly or monthly — also something that needs a kill condition, not just a good backtest?先想一下：為什麼連規則的格式本身——比如每週再平衡還是每月再平衡——也需要一個撤回條件，不能只靠一份漂亮的回測就定案？

A good backtest answers “did this work in the past,” not “will this keep working, and how would I know if it stopped.” Any rule, no matter how carefully validated out-of-sample, was still built against a finite slice of market history that may simply not contain the regime that eventually breaks it — the XIV case earlier in this lesson is exactly that pattern. Committing in advance to a specific, falsifiable condition under which the rule would be downgraded or retired forces a decision to be made before results are known, rather than after a loss has already happened and hindsight is doing the deciding. Without that commitment, every future underperformance gets explained away as noise, and every future outperformance gets kept as confirmation — a one-way ratchet that no amount of backtesting rigor protects against.

一份好的回測回答的是「這在過去有沒有用」，不是「這會不會繼續有用，以及如果它失效了我要怎麼知道」。任何一條規則，不管樣本外驗證做得多仔細，終究是對著一段有限的市場歷史切片建構出來的，這段歷史可能單純沒有包含最終會讓它失效的那個市況——這一課前面的 XIV 案例，正是這個型態。事先就承諾一個具體、可證偽的條件，一旦出現就要把這條規則降級或撤回，能強迫這個決定在結果出現之前就先做好，而不是等虧損已經發生、後見之明開始主導判斷之後才決定。沒有這個事先承諾，未來任何一次表現不佳都會被解釋成雜訊帶過，任何一次表現超標都會被留下來當成確認——這是一個單向棘輪，不管回測做得多嚴謹都擋不住。

From an Honest Number to a Risk Budget You Can Actually Trust

從誠實的數字，到一份你真的能信任的風險預算

Everything in this lesson so far has been about getting an honest number out of a backtest. What that number is actually for is sizing — deciding how much of a portfolio, and how much leverage on top of it, a given rule can responsibly carry. This is where an inflated backtest stops being an academic embarrassment and starts being a real financial injury: a maximum drawdown understated by survivorship bias, or a volatility estimate that never lived through a true tail event, feeds directly into a leverage or position-size decision that will be wrong in exactly the direction that hurts — too large, discovered on the one day it matters.

這一課到目前為止講的都是怎麼從一份回測裡拿到一個誠實的數字。那個數字真正的用途，是拿去決定部位大小——一條規則到底能負責任地扛多少比例的組合、上面再疊多少槓桿。這正是一份灌了水的回測從「學術上的難堪」變成「真實財務傷害」的地方：一個被存活者偏誤低估的最大回撤，或者一個從未真正經歷過尾端事件的波動率估計值，會直接餵進槓桿或部位大小的決策，而它錯的方向剛好是最傷人的那個方向——部位放太大，而且是在最要命的那一天才被發現。

Two disciplines close the loop between an honest backtest and a defensible risk budget. The first is pre-registrationFreezing a rule's parameters and thresholds before seeing how they perform, publicly committing to them in advance so they cannot be quietly adjusted after the fact based on results.: freezing a rule's parameters and thresholds before its results are known, so nobody can quietly nudge a threshold after the fact once the outcome is visible. Attached to it is a kill conditionA specific, falsifiable piece of evidence, agreed on in advance, that would cause a rule to be downgraded or retired — a rule without one cannot be distinguished from a permanent, unfalsifiable assumption. — a specific, falsifiable piece of evidence, stated in advance, that would cause the rule to be downgraded or dropped. A rule that nobody can articulate a kill condition for is not a tested hypothesis; it is an assumption wearing a backtest as a costume, and no amount of historical rigor changes that.

有兩項紀律，把「誠實的回測」跟「站得住腳的風險預算」之間的迴路接起來。第一項是預註冊在看到規則的表現之前，先把它的參數與門檻凍結下來，公開事先承諾，這樣結果出來之後就沒辦法悄悄回頭調整門檻。：在規則的結果還沒出來之前，先把參數與門檻凍結，這樣結果一旦出現，就沒有人能悄悄回頭微調門檻。跟著它一起的是撤回條件事先講好、具體且可證偽的一項證據，一旦出現就會讓這條規則被降級或撤回——一條說不出撤回條件的規則，跟一個永遠不會被推翻的假設沒有分別。——一項事先講好、具體、可證偽的證據，一旦出現，就要讓這條規則被降級或撤回。一條沒有人講得出撤回條件的規則，不是一個被測試過的假設；它是一個披著回測外衣的假設，不管歷史驗證做得再嚴謹，都改變不了這一點。

The second discipline is publishing negative results with the same rigor and the same visibility as positive ones. A research process that only ever shows the rules that worked, while quietly discarding every configuration that failed a pre-registered test, is running the exact multiple-comparisons problem this lesson's calculator illustrates — just at the institutional level rather than the individual-parameter level. “This candidate did not clear the bar we set for it” is not a failed report; it is data, and a research process that treats it with less rigor than a success story is quietly rebuilding the same overfitting problem it claims to have solved.

第二項紀律，是用跟正面結果同樣的嚴謹度與同樣的能見度，發布負面結果。一個只展示成功規則、卻悄悄丟掉每一個沒通過預先設定測試的版本的研究流程，等於在制度層級而不是單一參數層級，重新製造了這一課試算器示範的同一個多重比較問題。「這個候選沒有通過我們設定的門檻」不是一份失敗的報告；它是資料，而一個對這類結果的重視程度低於成功故事的研究流程，等於悄悄重建了它自己聲稱已經解決的那個過度擬合問題。

Here is this lesson's explicit boundary, and it is worth stating without softening: no backtest, however carefully built — adjusted prices, point-in-time universe, walk-forward validated, timing-luck audited, apples-to-apples costed, pre-registered with a kill condition — can validate a market regime that has never occurred inside its sample. A backtest describes the past faithfully or it does not; it cannot manufacture evidence about a kind of day the market has not yet had. This is not a flaw to be engineered away with more rigor. It is the honest ceiling on what the entire discipline can promise, and the correct response is not to distrust every number this lesson has spent its time teaching you to compute correctly — it is to size positions and leverage with room to survive a day the backtest never saw, which is exactly what a risk budget, rather than a return target, is for.

這是這一課明確的邊界，值得不打折扣地講清楚：不管一份回測做得多嚴謹——還原股價、時點正確的母體、通過樣本外驗證、做過時機運氣審計、成本拆解到位、預先註冊撤回條件——它都無法驗證一種從沒在它的樣本裡發生過的市況。回測要嘛忠實描述了過去，要嘛沒有；它沒辦法對一種市場還沒經歷過的日子憑空生出證據。這不是一個可以靠更多嚴謹度工程掉的缺陷。它是這整套紀律能承諾的誠實天花板，而正確的回應不是因此不信任這一課花力氣教你正確算出的每一個數字——而是把部位大小與槓桿，留出能撐過一個回測從沒見過的日子的空間，這正是「風險預算」而不是「報酬目標」該做的事。

One more connection worth making explicit, tying this back to how the market prices things: even a rule that survives every honesty check in this lesson — genuinely out-of-sample validated, not overfit, not timing luck — is not guaranteed to keep paying once it is well known. A validated historical pattern that becomes widely published and widely traded tends to attract capital chasing exactly the same signal, which can compress or erase the very edge the backtest correctly measured in the past. A clean backtest proves what happened in a sample of history. It says nothing about how many other people have since read the same report and are now trying to arrive earlier than you.

還有一個值得明講的連結，接回市場怎麼定價這件事：即使一條規則通過了這一課的每一項誠實檢查——真正通過樣本外驗證、不是過度擬合、不是時機運氣——也不保證它一旦廣為人知之後還會繼續有用。一個經過驗證、被廣泛發表且被廣泛交易的歷史型態，往往會吸引資金追著同一個訊號湧入，這股資金本身就可能壓縮甚至抹掉回測正確量到的那個歷史優勢。一份乾淨的回測，證明的是歷史某一段樣本裡發生過什麼。它完全沒有告訴你，有多少其他人已經讀過同一份報告，正在想辦法比你更早進場。

📜War Story戰爭故事

A publicly posted long-term track record page, originally built to calculate returns using each strategy's official launch-day price as the fixed starting point, ran for years using unadjusted prices for that calculation. It worked without incident until one of the tracked instruments underwent a stock split after the record had been live for some time. Because the split was never adjusted for, the page's return calculation treated the post-split price drop as if it were a genuine loss, producing a badly distorted number for that specific instrument — not a small rounding difference, but a return figure wrong by a scale tied directly to the split ratio.

一個公開發布的長期戰績追蹤頁面，原本設計成用每套策略正式掛牌當天的價格當作固定起點來算報酬率，用未還原的股價這樣算了好幾年都沒出過事。直到其中一個被追蹤的標的，在這份紀錄已經上線一段時間之後，執行了一次股票分割。因為分割從來沒有被還原處理過，這個頁面的報酬計算，把分割後的價格下降當成一次真實的虧損來處理，讓那個特定標的的數字嚴重失真——不是一個小小的四捨五入誤差，而是一個依分割比例直接對應的錯誤幅度。

The fix, once caught, was not to patch that one number. It was to change the underlying rule: use a split-and-dividend-adjusted closing price, taken as of the launch date or the most recent trading day before it, as the fixed starting point for every future calculation, so the base and every downstream return stayed on the same price scale no matter what corporate actions happened afterward. The specific error is a smaller cousin of the same mechanism that distorts a twenty-year academic backtest — the same discipline named at the very start of this lesson, showing up again at the very end, on a page meant for public readers rather than a research file meant for internal review.

問題被抓到之後，修正的做法不是去補那一個數字，而是改掉底層規則本身：一律改用還原分割與配息後的收盤價，取掛牌日當天或之前最近一個交易日的價格，當成往後每一次計算的固定起點，這樣不管之後發生什麼公司行動，基期跟每一個下游算出來的報酬率，都會停留在同一個價格尺度上。這個具體錯誤，是扭曲一份二十年學術回測的同一種機制的一個較小的親戚——這一課一開頭點名的同一項紀律，在最後又出現了一次，只是這次出現在一個給公開讀者看的頁面上，不是一份給內部複查的研究檔案。

⚠

Pitfall: crediting an execution-layer tweak with a performance improvement it did not earn. When a change is made for one clearly stated reason — usually reducing trading frequency or cost — and a risk-adjusted return number also happens to improve, the default assumption should be that the improvement is timing luck until a dedicated audit says otherwise, not that a second unrelated benefit was discovered for free.

陷阱：把一個執行層的微調，記成它沒有真正賺到的績效改善。當一個改動是為了一個講得很清楚的理由而做的——通常是降低交易頻率或成本——而風險調整後的報酬數字也剛好變好了，預設的假設應該是：在專門的審計證明不是之前，這個改善就是時機運氣，不是額外白撿到的第二個好處。

⚠

Pitfall: trusting a comparison table because every number in it checks out. A table can be arithmetically flawless in every cell and still mislead, because the error lives in what its layout silently implies about which variables were held constant. Before trusting any two-column comparison, confirm from the footnotes — not the headline — that only the stated variable actually differs.

陷阱：只因為每個數字都對得起來，就信任一張對照表。一張表格可以每一格的算術都完美無誤，卻依然誤導人，因為問題出在排版悄悄暗示了哪些變數被固定不變。在信任任何兩欄對照表之前，先從註腳（不是頭條）確認真的只有標明的那個變數不一樣。

⚠

Pitfall: mistaking “it has been live for a while without a bad surprise” for out-of-sample validation. A short live-trading window is not the same thing as a held-out historical period that was deliberately excluded from the rule's design process. A rule can go months without an incident simply because the market has not yet presented the condition it is weak against — the same gap this lesson opened with.

陷阱：把「已經上線一段時間沒出過事」誤當成樣本外驗證。一段短暫的實盤交易窗口，跟一段刻意從規則設計過程中排除出去的歷史測試期，不是同一件事。一條規則可以好幾個月都相安無事，單純只是因為市場還沒出現它撐不住的那種條件——正是這一課開頭那個落差。
